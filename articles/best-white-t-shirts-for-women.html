Your Search for the Best White T-Shirt Is Over

Devon editors put together a list of the best white t-shirts for women in every style. Shop cotton tees, oversized, boxy shirts, baby tees & more.

By Andrea Zendejas

June 22, 2026

How do you find the best white T-shirt for women? It might seem like a simple question, but tracking one down that checks all the boxes—from fit to comfort—is quite a feat. After all, the more minimal a clothing item is, the more its flaws become glaringly clear.

The Best White T-Shirts on Sale for Prime Day 2026:

One great white T-shirt can make all the difference in your wardrobe, and Amazon Prime Day 2026 lets you restock worn-out favorites, or discover new ones currently on sale. From the cotton crewneck our editors love to the perfect long-sleeve for layering, these are the Prime Day clothing deals worth knowing.

  • The Multipack: Hanes Cotton Stretch Crewneck (3-Pack) , $38 , $22

No one likes the feeling of a boxy, ill-fitting white tee that feels as stiff as cardboard against their skin. Whether you’re layering a ribbed tee for cooler temperatures or styling a slub crewneck tee for warm weather, a white tee is a tried-and-true closet essential. From soft, fitted Pima cotton styles to relaxed, boxy silhouettes, the best styles hold shape, resist piling, and feel as good as they look.

Devon Editors’ Favorite White T-Shirts for Women

Throughout the history of pop culture, from Marlon Brando to Kendall Jenner, the white T-shirt has been ingrained in both our lives and our wardrobes. For us at Devon, the shirt has become a unifying style staple—and everyday at the office is an opportunity for an editor to showcase its versatility. After all, there’s comfort in reaching for a crisp, plain white T-shirt in the morning—fresh and dewy-faced. Perhaps we’ll add a touch of jewelry, whether that’s a pair of gold hoops or a statement pendant.

The search for perfect white T-shirt is an ambitious pursuit—below, two Devon editors share their favorites to help you bridge the gap between you and your own platonic ideal.

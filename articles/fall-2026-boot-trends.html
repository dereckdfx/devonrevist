6 Fall Boot Trends to Try, From Buckled Styles to Slim Knee-Highs

The silver lining of summer ending? All the fall 2026 boot trends. From stompy buckled styles to slim knee-highs, these are the shapes to know—and try.

By Christina Holevas

October 19, 2026

Great boots and fall weather go hand in hand (or should we say foot in foot?). When summer temps turn into crisp, leafy days, boots are the first thing we’re itching to wear again. Luckily for fall 2026, there are plenty of exciting new boot trends to try out.

Devon ’s Guide to Fall Boot Trends

On the fall runways, boots appeared with an extra dose of attitude. A favorite of the season is the knee-high slim, which isn’t a new style, per se, but appeared upgraded with a wicked heel, a structured shape, and extra hardware. Speaking of hardware, buckle boots were a hit this season too. The classic workwear style arrived to much fanfare at Dries Van Noten and Dior. Elsewhere, leg-elongating options like second-skin sock boots and extra-tall OTK boots were on view at Celine, Chanel, Chloé, and Balenciaga. Those looking for chic weather-ready options will be eager to shop Miu Miu’s grip-soled stompers and Loewe’s transparent take on the rain boot, while those wanting to add a bit of extra flair might gravitate toward the Western-inspired styles from Valentino and Rabanne.

Whatever your taste, there are plenty of boots to stomp in this season. Shop the top fall boot trends right here.

The Slim Knee-High

Knee-high boots, especially with heels with a slim, structured shape, feel fresh this fall. Go for a classic style in chocolate brown or Gucci’s horse-bit style for some extra attitude.

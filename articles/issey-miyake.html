Issey Miyake Spring 2027 Ready-to-Wear Collection

Issey Miyake Spring 2027 Ready-to-Wear collection, runway looks, beauty, models, and reviews.

By Luke Leitch

October 2, 2026

Ma is a Japanese word for the space in between. This Satoshi Kondo collection was specifically about the ma between garments and the bodies within them, the closest in-English dressmaking equivalent to which would be ease. That was why this show in the Paris Dojo began with 11 dancers, led by Tess Voelker, manipulating a vast piece of pale blue fabric as if it were a single garment inhabited by all of them. As they moved beneath and against it, breathing, stretching and pulling, the cloth billowed like a sail, rolled like a wave and swelled like a lung.

This performance was most closely reflected in a series of coats and skirts in lavender, pink and khaki that similarly billowed and snapped against the air as the models walked. Most antithetical to the dreamy sense of ease these mustered were the bodices that looked like felted plaster-of-Paris molds (plus a yellow vest and apparently denim shorts). The bodices were created with Wacoal, the Japanese intimate-apparel company, using a proprietary technology originally developed to mold bra cups. Here it was adapted to make torso-like forms that preserved the impression of fabric draped around a body: clothes that had a precise presentiment of the body even before they were put on it.

There was a lovely section of sculpturally fluid pieces in black or blue Miyake pleated fabric that swirled around the body and sometimes the head too: the pleating created the effect of garments as airily applied brushstrokes. Satin-shiny outfits in silver or blue were wrinkled as if stuffed last minute into a suitcase. Their silhouettes were broken by puckered, unwrinkled, roundish extrusions that looked like they had been grasped and squeezed between an enormous thumb and forefinger. Patterned scarves were draped through long plain dresses and opaque sheaths placed over other dresses: clothes wore clothes, ma within ma. This was an energizing inhalation of poetically conceived, technologically innovative fashion from Kondo and his team at Miyake.

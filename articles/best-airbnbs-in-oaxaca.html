The Best Airbnbs in Oaxaca Are Colorful, Charming, and Designed to Impress

For an alternative to Mexico City, stay in one of the best Airbnbs in Oaxaca—from cozy studios to open-air villas, these are the best places to book.

By Elly Leavitt, Angela Tafoya

September 29, 2026

A blend of a growing gastronomic scene and rich Indigenous artisan history (especially when it comes to pottery, a craft with a rich backstory in the region) Oaxaca has always been a beloved destination for in-the-know travelers. For those looking for an alternative to the fast pace of Mexico City, both the historic center and its surrounding areas offer a relaxed setting with plenty to do. Adventurers will find no shortage of hiking trails and beautiful beaches to explore; city mice have their pick of markets and gardens to peruse, as well as a few Michelin-starred restaurants. (And both types of travelers can enjoy unwinding with some mezcal tasting—the area is the biggest producer of the spirit in Mexico.) Convinced yet? Start your trip by booking one of the best Airbnbs in Oaxaca.

Devon’s Guide to the Best Airbnbs in Oaxaca:

  • For Unparalleled Ocean Views: BeachVilla , $677 per night
  • For Brutalist Charm: Lunaterra Alberca , $263 per night
  • For a Dose of Design Inspiration: Rojo Deseno , $170 per night
  • For a Remote Stay: Casa Labra , $159 per night
  • For a City Hideaway: Casa Criollo , $547 per night
  • For a Beach Option: Coyote Loft , $259 per night
  • For Indoor-Outdoor Living: Casa Teo , $163 per night

Our top picks are scattered throughout the region; we found cozy studios and sprawling villas alike, depending on your plans and the size of your travel party. One thing unites these properties: Between Brutalist spaces drenched in color and apartments that put local craft front and center, each of these 17 vacation rentals is brimming with design inspiration. Find the right Oaxacan Airbnb stay for you, below.

Casa Unión Oaxaca

Described as “an island in the heart of the city,” this one-bedroom apartment sits inside a traditional Oaxacan house in the historic city center, where the rooms wrap around a patio, shaded by an avocado tree. The home features modern interiors filled with handmade objects, organic materials, and assorted homeware from local artisans. The unit has its own entrance, a small private front patio, a full kitchen, and a compact workspace. In the bedroom, you’ll find an open shower that looks onto a small interior garden. Start each morning at the bakery and café located at the home’s street level, which many locals credit with some of the best bread in Oaxaca.

Rooms: 1 bedroom, 1 bathroom

Amenities: Kitchen, workspace, private patio, WiFi

Location: City Center

Lush Oasis

With colorful tiled floors, arched entryways, and tropical fronds on every side, this three-bedroom colonial from the late 1800s is a lush hideaway that’s been tastefully restored. Every room opens onto open-air patios; the dining room overlooks the courtyard’s winding cement staircase, and the artfully updated kitchen’s oversized windows offer treetop views. Besides plenty of outdoor seating nooks, there’s also a small soaking tub in the backyard so you can make the most of the home’s expansive square footage. You can even book a private mezcal tasting in the front of the property—the whole space offers a beautiful, transportive stay in the heart of the city (the main square is just five minutes away).

Rooms: 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms

Amenities: Air conditioning, rooftop terrace, laundry, housekeeping three days a week, private mezcal tasting room (by reservation)

Casa Muro

Run by a local family, this five-bedroom house is only a short stroll from the city’s main square—meaning all the best markets and galleries are within a quick walk. The rooms are decorated with rustic, carved wood furnishings and color-splashed walls, and each bedroom has its own bathroom. An interior terrace offers a quiet place to regroup; for the evenings where you want to stay in and cook, the dining room fits all ten guests around one table.

Rooms: 5 bedrooms, 5.5 bathrooms

Amenities: Interior terrace, WiFi, dedicated workspace, private balcony, AC

Casa De Ella

With lime-washed walls, time-worn furniture, and shelves lined with glossy black pottery, this two-bedroom historic home in central Oaxaca is a physical extension of the mezcal brand De Ella. (Of course, guests can sample the brand’s award-winning mezcals during their stay.) The owners renovated it using only local materials, down to furnishings shaped from the soil of the surrounding valleys. Its centerpiece is a hidden room for ritual and relaxation, built entirely from raw earth and leftover agave fibers—it’s a place that feels truly grounding and ceremonial.

Rooms: 2 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms

Amenities: WiFi, AC, backyard, private patio, indoor fireplace

Rooftop Glass House

Your very own Oaxacan treehouse. Located on the third floor of a building right in the historic centre of the town, this Japanese-inspired space blends indoor-outdoor living smoothly: The walls are all glass, letting in sun and views of the surrounding terrace garden. The spa-like bathroom is another highlight. Small and slightly cavelike with a wood-thatched roof, the cozy limewashed room is a tranquil spot.

Amenities: Sound system, outdoor dining area and furniture, grill, housekeeping available for a fee

Location: Jalatlaco

Mamita Santa Apartment

Bohemian in nature, this two-person apartment has the blues in the chicest way. Azure tiling, painted archways, and cabinetry give this home a sense of calm; the best room is by far the kitchen, which is light and airy and comes with a big table you can use alternatingly for meals and remote work. Take your morning coffee to the private interior patio; it comes with a swinging egg chair.

Amenities: Outdoor furniture, board games

BeachVilla

For a group trip closer to the sea, this sprawling villa is ultra luxurious. It sleeps 12, with amenities like a cleaning service and pool maintenance giving it a hotel-like quality that’ll make your stay more comfortable. There are several bedrooms and nooks inside the stylish house for each member of your party to make themselves at home, but the real draw is the outside area—from the pool to the terrace overlooking the ocean (a semi-private beach is just a short walk away), this is an Airbnb you book for its stunning surroundings.

Rooms: 6 bedrooms, 5.5 bathrooms

Amenities: Private pool, outdoor dining area and furniture, hammock, beach access, AC, crib, HDTV

Location: Crucecita

Lunaterra Alberca

Terracotta walls and brutalist architecture combine for a quintessential Oaxacan stay. This is a larger property ideal for a group trip—there are two garages, if you’re renting a car, and a large dining table for cooking meals at home. There’s even a heated pool; it’s small, but for being in the city center, we’ll take it.

Rooms: 4 bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms

Amenities: TV, private backyard, free carport, pool

Location: Reforma

La Danta Chayote

Experience indoor-outdoor living at its finest in this rustic spot. It’s one of four bungalows, built on a garden that’s over 30 years old and made almost entirely out of hand-carved Cantera stone (a volcanic rock that adds to the home’s rugged appeal). The hot tub on the rooftop terrace is the ideal way to unwind after a day of sightseeing.

Amenities: Crib, hot tub, balcony

Rojo Deseno

Here’s an Airbnb that shows commitment to a theme: From the patterned kitchen tiles to the colored cement walls of the luxurious bathroom, every inch is coated in a rich, earthy red. The three-bedroom home is in what used to be an artisanal chocolate factory, on one of the main streets in the historic center of the city. With local design emphasized (the tiles are made by Oaxacan artisans), it’s a truly memorable stay.

Amenities: Washer in building, TV, crib upon request, high chair upon request, baby bath upon request, balcony

Casa Labra

For an off-the-beaten-path option away from the hustle and bustle of the city, book this charming home. You’ll need a rental car (though the owners can arrange an airport shuttle upon request), but once you’re there, you may not want to leave: The property is surrounded by gardens, with hiking trails and bird-watching available as serene activities. You’ll feel like you’re in your very own grown-up treehouse.

Amenities: Bathtub, washer, outdoor playground, balcony, private backyard

Location: San Pablo Etla

Coyote Loft

Sweeping ocean views make this the ultimate coastal getaway. This one-bedroom casita is just a five-minute walk from the beach; with a large pool and tons of terrace space for lounging, you’ll be spoiled with choices for outdoor living. Inside, a pool table and impressive kitchen offer just as much to do.

Amenities: Outdoor shower, TV, pool table, crib upon request, pack ’n play upon request, high chair, dishwasher, beachfront, balcony, private backyard, fire pit, BBQ, free parking, pool

Location: Puerto Ángel

Casa Criollo

Situated just behind the restaurant of the same name, Casa Criollo serves as a calming respite from the city. It’s all cool tones, low furniture, and natural textures; there’s a steam room included, as well as a plunge pool. Guests also have the benefit of complimentary breakfast served daily and bottles of house mezcal—a perk of staying in a spot with direct connection to one of the city’s top eateries.

Amenities: Washer in building, dryer in building, TV, crib, balcony, private backyard, hot tub

Casa Verde

It’s fairly rare to find a property that has both a massive garden and a pool right in the historic center of Oaxaca, but this rental does just that. It sleeps up to eight people, with plenty of room for stretching out both indoors and out. Design-wise, there’s a throughline of green that highlights the natural aspects of the architecture.

Rooms: 4 bedrooms, 3.5 bathrooms

Amenities: Washer, dryer, TV, crib upon request, pack ’n play, baby bath upon request, high chair upon request , private backyard, BBQ, pool, free parking

Casa de la Libélula

We recommend booking a car or ATV for this semi-secluded option, located on the southern coast of the city. It’s situated atop a hill, with a view that’s worth the climb. The space itself is set in a modern, geometric home that stands out in the lush landscape; inside, organic elements like woven lighting, patterned rugs, and rustic wood furniture make you feel at home.

Rooms: 1 bedroom, 1.5 bathrooms

Amenities: Beachfront, free parking, pool

Location: Santa María Tonameca

Our Peaceful Patio Studio

15 minutes out from the historic center in the quiet neighborhood of San Felipe, this smaller space is ideal for a couple. An outdoor tub is your best option for full relaxation after a day spent partaking in the outdoor activities the area is known for, from water sports to hiking. Those booking a longer stay will appreciate the dedicated workspace and full kitchen.

Amenities: Bathtub, outdoor shower, private backyard, free parking

Location: San Felipe

Casa Teo

Steps away from La Barra de Colotepec beach, this villa takes indoor-outdoor living to new heights. By this, we mean that there’s barely a single wall to be found in the whole property—sleep, cook, and live in the open air. This communal aspect makes it a great spot for traveling with friends; this unique stay is one for the books.

Amenities: Bathtub, outdoor shower, washer, exercise equipment, beachfront, free resort access, shared balcony, shared backyard, free parking, pool

Location: La Barra

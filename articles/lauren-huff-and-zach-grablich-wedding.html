This Wedding Weekend at an Irish Manor Featured Bespoke Looks, Plenty of Vintage, and a Pajama Party

Noah Zgralich and Lauren Huff held an Adare Manor wedding weekend filled with golf and pub crawls. The bride wore looks from House of Gilles, Ralph Lauren, Elie Saab, and Alexander McQueen throughout the celebration.

By Shelby Wax

October 2, 2026

Lauren Huff and Noah Zgrablich pretended to be newlyweds on the very first day they met. In 2017, the two were both guests at their mutual friend’s black-tie 21st birthday party in New York City. “Lauren was wearing a long white dress that I still remember brought out the blue in her eyes,” recalls Noah. They went with their friends to another bar after the party and, as a joke, Noah told other patrons around that they just come from their wedding. “They kept buying us free drinks, which definitely made her laugh,” he says. “We even have a photo together from that night.”

It would take four more years before their real relationship would begin. Lauren was visiting a friend in Los Angeles and mentioned on Instagram that they were off to O’Brien’s Irish Pub and Restaurant. Noah convinced his friends to go and messaged her back, “What a coincidence, we’re actually on our way there.” After spending time together, he convinced Lauren to go on a date with him before she flew back home.

Another four years later, Noah would propose to Lauren at her family home in East Hampton on June 25, 2025. His family was visiting from California, so everyone was ready to celebrate. Noah told Lauren that he had a fake work event, which they would need to attend, so she would dress up. “Before leaving, Noah asked me to go to the backyard with him to look for his sunglasses,” recalls Lauren. There, the ground was covered in rose petals, and Noah got down on one knee. “I was so overcome with joy and speechless that as Noah put the ring on my finger, he had to ask me again: ‘Is that a yes?’” she says. Meanwhile, their families were hiding in the yard to spy on the proposal. “I heard a huge ‘surprise’ from behind me, when they all came out to congratulate us,” says Lauren. “We celebrated with dinner at the house followed by karaoke in the basement!”

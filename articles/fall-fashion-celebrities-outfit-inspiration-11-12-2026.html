Need Fall Fashion Inspo? These Six Stars Are Nailing Cozy-Chic

From Jennifer Lawrence to Zendaya, allow these stylish A-listers to inspire your next fall outfit.

By Christian Allaire

October 13, 2026

Fall fashion lovers, rejoice: It’s our time to shine!

You have to admit, dressing for this time of year is the most fun. You get to rotate a fabulous array of coats, layer with scarves or sweaters, and bust out your chicest boots—all without shivering in the depths of winter’s deep freeze. It’s still nice enough outside to look cute and feel cute.

However, even the most die-hard fall fashion enthusiasts need some outfit inspiration from time to time. Are you wondering what to wear in these few short weeks left of the season, and need some guidance? Or perhaps you just want to refresh your wardrobe with a few new key investment pieces. Fret not: Allow these six stylish stars to get your creative juices going.

From Zendaya to Jennifer Lawrence, these A-listers have all been stepping out this month in perfect cozy-chic outfits. Come for the exceptional coats—see: Hailey Bieber’s fringed-trim leather blazer—but stay for the killer accessories too, including Zoë Kravitz’s royal purple scarf or Jennifer Lawrence’s slouchy leather hobo bag. By recreating these of-the-moment looks, you can bundle up in style and feel like a celebrity, without the pesky paparazzi following your every move. It’s a win-win, people!

Below, read on for six stars nailing fall fashion—and shop the pieces to recreate their outfits.

Jennifer Lawrence

A tailored black overcoat is an investment piece that you will simply never tire of. But leave it to Jennifer Lawrence gave it a modern update with shearling slip-on clogs, a slouchy leather hobo bag, and subtle floral-print scarf. You can also go full incognito mode, should you choose, with big shades and a cheeky dad cap.

This Backyard Wedding in Seattle Was Inspired by Ralph Lauren and ’00s Rom-Coms

Stylist Sara Walker married Joe Ratliffe in her parents’ backyard, wearing vintage Vivienne Westwood, bespoke Phillipa Lepley, Emilia Wickstead, and runway Chanel over the course of the weekend.

By Shelby Wax

October 23, 2026

Sara Walker and Joe Ratliffe’s families have known each other for over 40 years, yet the two never met until they were seniors in high school. “Our dads worked together, and we grew up just a few miles apart in Seattle—but somehow never really crossed paths until New Year’s Eve going into 2016,” Sara tells Devon. “We shared a midnight kiss and the rest is history!”

The two dated through their senior year, then navigated long distance through college and starting jobs in different cities. “I was building my career in fashion buying, while Joe was working as a documentary filmmaker in Jackson Hole,” says Sara. “We really learned how to grow up both together and independently, even while living across different time zones.”

After nine years together, Joe popped the question while the two were traveling in San José del Cabo over Memorial Day weekend in 2025. “Joe completely surprised me by secretly flying my family in to celebrate alongside his. Our families are so intertwined at this point, so having everyone together for the entire weekend was perfect,” says Sara. Waking up the morning after the proposal with everyone there “genuinely felt like Christmas morning.” As for the oval diamond engagement ring he gave her, Sara was happily surprised: “I had never picked one out or given Joe a specific ring to get, so he did it completely on his own—and he did a very good job!”

When choosing where to exchange vows and start the next chapter of their lives together, the couple looked towards home. They decided to tie the knot in Sara’s parents’ backyard on August 8, 2026. “I’ve spent my entire life in this house—it has seen every era of me, and every era of Sara and Joe, for that matter—so it only felt right to get married right here,” says the bride. “Having our wedding in our hometown felt incredibly special and personal to both of us. It was the most magical day, and we felt so at ease and at home—literally!”

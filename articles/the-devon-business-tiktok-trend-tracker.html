The Devon Business TikTok Trend Tracker

Devon Business brings you weekly exclusive data from TikTok, unpacking the latest creators and biggest trends sweeping its Gen Z user base.

By Lucy Maguire

September 24, 2026

TikTok has turned songs into No. 1 hits, birthed micro-trends and cultural movements and created a shared lexicon of jingles, phrases and vignettes among its 1.2 billion users, 70 per cent of whom are Gen Z, according to the video platform.

Here, Devon Business will share weekly trend insights using exclusive data from TikTok.

2026

September

Like florals for spring, some consumer trends are predictable. As fall gets underway, TikTokers are showcasing their #Hoodie rotations in unboxing and closet hauls, calling out affordable and cozy options from retailers such as Urban Outfitters and streetwear brands including Supreme, Stüssy, and Denim Tears.

A hero campaign for September, #TikTokShopStockUp is gaining momentum among the platform’s shoppers, offering up high-value deals on cosmetics — bronzers, eyeshadow sticks, contour lip liners, and masks — from the likes of Elf, Laura Mercier, and Medicube. The hashtag has also been curated with tech, fashion, and “everyday essentials” that might need replenishing.

After a hot summer defined by Havaianas flip flops and spritzes, TikTokers have welcomed colder climes, forefronting leather #Boots as the season’s answer to day-to-night dressing. Dries Van Noten, Our Legacy, and Isabel Marant are topping wish lists, chiming with the perennial black-on-black dress code of fashion aesthetes. Brands wanting to capitalize on the trend should focus on communicating comfort and sought-after specifications, such as a larger calf size.

#September is always a tentpole month for fashion consumers and fans, spurred on by fashion month, events such as Devon World, and the #BackToSchool mood. Creators are posting their guides to transitional dressing, pointing users to blazer-jeans ensembles and lightweight jackets. Based on the influx of fit checks, shorts are still acceptable for another few weeks.

The National Football League (NFL) kicked off this week, and college games are well underway. With that, fans across the US are locking in for #GameDay, sharing their pre-game rituals in group clips, as well as posting #OutfitInspo and stadium dance routines. Following the success of New York Knicks earlier this year, brands should lean into merch opportunities, one-off collaborations, and big-game celebrity dressing.

The leaves will soon turn brown, the layers will come out, and up until mid-December drinking a pumpkin-spiced latte won’t be deemed basic. TikTokers are already sharing #FallOutfits ahead of time, purveying cozy knitwear and mom jeans co-ords, and dark whimsy or mall-prep styling. Outerwear and vests from #Hollister are already gaining traction in shades of burgundy and ochre — pre-loved and new.

Taking cues from the historically bohemian district of New York, #SohoChic is a loose but highly accessible trend rooted in architecturally inspired neutrals, brought to life with zany pops of color — think plum, carrot, and moss — and arty patterns. (Leopard print is approved.) For brands, the opportunity lies in premium basics and tailoring that enable consumers to experiment with subtle but expressive ensembles.

#GRWM pioneer #AlixEarle has been a consistent presence on Facross the globe for some time. Now, she’s a top watch on Netflix, thanks to the new series, Earle Meets World — a Kardashians-esque reality show following the star and her family. Brands have long been savvy to the US personality, not least Alo, who took her and a gaggle of other creators on a yacht in the height of summer. But recently? Demna’s Gucci dress worn by Earle to the series’s premiere is already doing the rounds in reaction videos.

August

Summer’s not over yet, but TikTok’s fashion goths and cosplayers are already tapping in for #Halloween inspiration. Creators are putting forward “niche halloween costume” ideas, riffing on Y2K and contemporary runway moments, as well as sharing unique or “cool girl” beauty routines. Brand engagement with the moment is emerging, with players capitalizing on a committed audience that considers Spooky Season its own version of Christmas.

The World Cup might be over, but legacy footballer #CristianoRonaldo — who hinted the tournament would be his last — hasn’t left public consciousness thanks to his distinctly casual wedding to Georgina Rodríguez, which Juergen Teller shot in his typical matter-of-fact style for Devon’s digital cover. Creators immediately jumped in to discuss Rodríguez’s custom Gucci gown by Demna — a big win for the Kering-owned brand.

In tune with August’s slower pace — when creative industries wind down — creators are flocking to #Egypt, generating styling content, suggesting co-ords, comfortable flats, and modest but casual dresses. TikTokers post fit checks in front of the pyramids, while EsDeeKid’s Century track — referencing Cairo — has quickly become the jingle of choice. Travel and lifestyle videos offering outfit inspiration direct viewers to high-street brands including Zara, H&M, and Massimo Dutti.

Concurrent with college students moving into dorms and end-of-Summer clear-outs, #Depop is a hot topic for people downsizing or building new wardrobes. Here, as well as lauding Depop’s credentials for secondary-market purchases, TikTokers are also spotlighting new independent brands that use the shopping platform as a de-facto store. Fitness influencers are spotlighting pre-loved and deadstock sportswear as wellness and health categories gain momentum.

July

We may be in the height of summer, but many TikTok users are already thinking about #BackToSchool, retailers have begun launching back-to-school campaigns earlier than ever, prompting creators to share shopping content ahead of the September rush. Popular formats include outfit inspiration, school hauls, "first day" GRWMs, dorm and study space makeovers, lunch ideas and organisation tips.

Coach continues its reign as one of Gen Z’s favourite brands, and now, #CoachBag is trending on TikTok. Content centred around Coach handbags, including new purchases, styling inspiration, reviews and vintage finds is surging on the platform. While outfit styling videos and "what fits in my bag" content are driving sustained engagement.

Again, it’s summertime, and TikTok users are searching for and posting about #PolarizedSunglass, notably those with reduced glare, improved visibility and lens performance. Alongside holidaymakers, outdoor sports, driving and fishing creators are also contributing to hashtag’s growth.

It’s officially confirmed: Argentina will face Spain in the #Finals of the World Cup on Sunday, July 19, after the Argentine squad beat #England in a last-minute comeback, scoring two goals in the final 10 minutes. It’s a result TikTokers are still processing by posting close-up replays of the winning goals.

As the tournament comes to a close, the #WorldCup2026 has delivered many memorable moments on TikTok. Fans have watched closely as legendary players Cristiano Ronaldo and Lionel Messi compete in what is likely to be their final World Cup, paying tribute to the occasion by posting nostalgic highlight reels celebrating the best moments of their careers.

On the flip side, new stars are emerging, with the likes of Jude Bellingham, Erling Haaland and Kylian Mbappé leading the next generation of fan favorites. TikTokers have been sharing on-pitch interactions between Bellingham and Haaland, as well as funny moments from Mbappé’s perfectly chaotic interviews.

TikTok content

Communities have come together throughout the tournament, both IRL and in the comments section. From users celebrating Curaçao’s first-ever World Cup goal, to Japanese fans going viral after they were spotted cleaning up post-match, and emotional tributes to England supporters embracing “Wonderwall” as their go-to match anthem, the tournament has sparked conversations across the world. One debate, however, still stands: is it called football or #Soccer? With 19 million posts across the platform and a 20% week-on-week increase, there’s an argument to be made for the latter.

Post-Swift wedding and Knicks-mania, #NYC has seen a content boost on TikTok. While the evergreen hashtag is home to plethora of content spanning food recs, city nightlife compilations and sidewalk interviews, more recent additions feature the “JusT&T Married” billboards outside Madison Square Gardens and streets filled with seas of orange and blue.

Users are flocking to TikTok for all things #Dating. Be it podcast snippets from relationship experts like Celebs Go Dating’s Paul Brunson, date-gone-wrong tales, or hot takes from everyday TikTokers, the hashtag is a hub for humor, advice, and community.

From seasonal skincare that’s heat-friendly to vacation bargains, users are refusing to gatekeep their #SummerWins, through try-on hauls and product walk-throughs.

As this summer’s #LoveIsland finals — July 12 in the US; July 27 in the UK — approach, users are taking to the platform to make their predictions, give their takes on current islanders, and dissect the drama.

June

As the 2026 Fifa World Cup kicks into gear, TikTok’s stream of soccer-adjacent content is spilling across feeds. Here’s a round-up of the World Cup trends that have swept the platform so far.

Discovering America

TikTok has become rife with Americans responding to the experiences of those flocking to the stateside to attend the games. There, tourists are discovering everything from ranch dressing and college football, to extreme weather conditions and songs like “Sweet Caroline” (just ask the German fans).

Creators are also posting content reacting to traditional American shopping experiences at Walmart, Target, Costco, and more. While trying — and ranking — these iconic American chains and regional staples, current content is blending travel, sport, and cultural discovery into a fresh TikTok moment. Whether it’s Aussie fans trying Chipotle, Scottish fans trying Dunkin, or Japanese fans experiencing chips and salsa for the first time.

Japanese fans continue their traditions

Japanese soccer fans went viral for their good manners following the Japan v Netherlands game. A tradition that started in 1998, fans were spotted cleaning up post-game trash at Dallas Stadium. Japanese fans shared firsthand videos from the clean-up, while Fifa joined in with educational content promoting the practice as a cultural sign of respect and joint responsibility.

Rooting for the underdog

Curaçao quickly emerged as a fan-favorite storyline following its World Cup debut. Despite a 7-1 loss, fans rallied around the team after it scored its first World Cup goal, with viral videos celebrating the moment rather than the final result. Fans are excited to see first-time qualifiers, including Cape Verde and Uzbekistan, also make their Fifa debuts.

Will #OOTD ever fade from the internet? It’s not looking likely. With 70.7 million posts on TikTok, the hashtag is still serving its purpose for outfit inspiration tailored to seasons, events, aesthetics, and more.

Game 3 of the #NBA finals took place in New York’s Madison Square Garden on Monday night, closing to a tight run of San Antonio Spurs 115 – 111 New York Knicks. TikTok is flooded with coverage from the game, including the blue and orange Manhattan streets (and Empire State Building), celebrities seated courtside, and gameplay reactions.

After the unexpected drop of #Drake’s three, full-length studio albums in May — Iceman, Habibti, and Maid of Honour — and the former’s place atop this week’s Billboard 200, TikTokers are dancing and lipsyncing to the Canadian artist’s latest tracks (there’s 43 to pick from).

Whether it’s #Sunglasses or a #SprayTan, the TikTok community are equipping themselves for the summer season and taking to the platform to send their vacation-ready tips, tricks, and packing hauls.

As summer gets into full swing, TikTok travel content is well on its way. One of the spotlight destinations? #Barcelona. Once again a fan favorite of European jetsetters, TikTokers are broadening their horizons beyond the usual rooftop bar recs and foodie diaries to highlight areas outside the city for a more evolved holiday itinerary, from Costa Brava to Sitges. Plus, content is only going to ramp up with Primavera Sound festival taking place in the city this week.

The #WorldCup is trending, as we approach the sub-one-week mark til kick-off. Comprising 48 national teams, the 2026 Fifa World Cup — broadcast from the US, Canada, and Mexico — will stretch across time zones, languages and communities. While the soccer content is yet to hit our screens, TikTok is filled with pub-related memes poking fun at British culture, team training peaks, and prediction content.

Series 13 of #LoveIsland UK landed this week, and TikTok already can’t get enough. Side-by-side fan commentary and reaction videos are complemented by behind-the-scenes confessions from contestants of seasons past. The hashtag is only set to grow as the drama gets underway.

As rumors swirl around the retirement of #Lebron James, TikTok is witnessing a surge in especially nostalgic gameplays featuring the NBA legend. After the Los Angeles Lakers were knocked out of the play-offs, the community has been busy predicting his next move and piecing together the player’s most iconic moments.

#MeganTheeStallion might be releasing some new music very soon. The female rap artist has teased a fresh release off the back of her split with the NBA’s Klay Thompson. But that’s not why she’s taking TikTok feeds by storm: reactions and snippets of her walking the Miami Swim Week runway, as well as her live performances, are rising in droves.

March

As TikTok users continue to celebrate self-love, and Gen Zs continue to romanticize their lives, the #LiveYourLife hashtag is trending on TikTok. Typical content includes montage storytelling, clips of travel, coffee runs, sunsets, exercise, plus glow-up and reset videos. The key is reaching a personal turning point.

Spring is springing, and for some, it’s time to reinvent. #DyedHair is trending on TikTok, as users experiment with new, bold colors, with popular videos including before and after transformations, bleaching tips, and fail and fix content.

Users are also refreshing their nails, and sharing their #SpringNails inspiration. Floral nail art, Easter themes, clean French tips and pastel shades are trending to align with the season.

Wedding season is also fast approaching, and brides-to-be turn to TikTok for #WeddingHair inspiration. From hairstyle reveals and bride reactions to more #GRWM content, the posts showcase a wide variety of styles, from updos to more minimalist waves.

Creators are leaning into before-and-after reveals, themed styling (from “office siren” to “date night”), and aspirational identity shifts, often using quick transitions or cinematic edits.

With 291.8k posts and 39% week-on-week growth, #GraphicTee reflects a resurgence of casual, expressive staples as creators prioritize individuality over full-look polish. Content typically centers on styling one piece multiple ways, with layering, accessorizing, or mixing high-low fashion. And the tees often featuring thrifted, vintage, or niche designs that signal subcultural taste or humor. For brands, this creates opportunity around customization, limited-edition graphics and content that demonstrates versatility rather than pushing complete outfits.

The enduring appeal lies in its intimacy — viewers are invited into everyday decision-making moments, strengthening parasocial connections, and shifting focus from finished looks to the journey of getting there.

Although smaller at 53.7k posts, #ChattyGRWM is growing quickly (+43% week-on-week), pointing to an evolution of the GRWM format into longer, more conversational content, as popularized by creators like Alix Earle. In these videos, creators prioritize storytelling — sharing opinions, life updates, or cultural commentary — while getting dressed, shifting the focus from visuals to personality. This format resonates because it deepens audience connection and increases watch time, blending the intimacy of routine with the engagement of narrative.

Creators are using the tag across hauls, outfit-building videos and “come shopping with me” formats, positioning Bershka as a source of affordable, trend-led pieces that can be easily rotated into frequent content.

Niche brands and small businesses are trending with Gen Z. The hashtag #supportsmallbiz, started by TikTok, has now hit 5 billion views. Gen Z are keen to uplift independent entrepreneurs in fashion and beyond.

TikTok users want an inside scoop on luxury experiences. Hashtag #firstclass, where users show first class flights and other luxury experiences, has 781.3 million views.

Hedonistic shopping is trending, from fashion to food, the hashtag #treatyourself reached 1.3 billion views in March as TikTok users encourage each other to go purchase that piece of clothing or accessory they've been thinking about for a while. Many Gen Zs like to buy-less-buy-better when it comes to fashion.

The only brand to make the trending list for February/March was #Yeezy. Amid social media controversy, Kanye West’s streetwear and sneaker brand is front of mind for TikTok users. The hashtag has 2.9 billion views. Since February the artist has been banned from Instagram, released new music and dropped his first Yeezy Gap Engineered by Balenciaga collection, which built buzz.

These Cozy Throw Blankets Are the Silver Lining to Colder Weather

Alpaca, wool, cashmere, cotton—the best throw blankets can change a room. Shop our favorites from West Elm, Jenni Kayne, Tekla, and more.

By Erika Owen, Elly Leavitt

October 3, 2026

There are a few things that bring instant personality to a living space: a signature candle scent, a piece of art that never fails to start a conversation, or a throw blanket with the power to make you forget about bad weather happening outside. Right now, we’re zeroing in on that last point—because when the temperatures dip and the sun is nowhere to be found, there really is nothing better than losing yourself in a bundle of warmth at the end of a long day.

Devon ’s Favorite Throw Blankets:

Merino or alpaca, cotton or cashmere, fringe or no fringe—you’ve got some decisions to make. And while you’ve probably heard “you can never have too many throw blankets” (likely from someone who, objectively, does have too many throw blankets), we believe a limit does exist. Much like a piece of art, an heirloom-worthy textile can become your favorite part of a room, so it’s best to choose your investment wisely.

Ahead, we pulled together 26 of the best throw blankets, ranging from plain to patterned and even embroidered designs. There’s something for every decor aesthetic, too; keep reading to usher in fall with a cozy new purchase.

Hawkins New York

solid mohair throw

HAWKINS NEW YORK

Who’s afraid of a little color? Go bold with this crimson option, which is handwoven from a mohair-wool blend in a Spanish factory that has specialized in the skill since 1930.

Materials: 73% mohair, 24% wool, 3% polyamide

Quince

Mongolian cashmere throw

QUINCE

Budget-friendly cashmere is a no-brainer, especially when it comes in Quince’s dizzyingly vast array of pretty colors. The simple fringe finish dresses up the otherwise minimal design.

Materials: 100% Mongolian cashmere

Hermès

Avalon throw blanket

HERMES

There are few things more chic than a lounge chair housing an Hermès throw. The iconic “H” is a luxe touch without going overboard. This is one your friends will fight over the next time you all gather at your place. Dry clean only, no question about it.

Materials: 90% merino wool, 10% cashmere

Molly Fitzpatrick

check throw blanket

CB2

If you like the boldness of a checkerboard pattern but want something a little unique, go for this CB2 design. The geometric print takes an abstract form, and best of all, it’s reversible.

Materials: 100% wool

Jenni Kayne

Hudson throw

JENNI KAYNE

Fuzzy alpaca is ideal for those who run cold; and if you’re tired of all the chocolate brown, this tobacco shade offers an equally earthy alternative.

Materials: 87% alpaca, 13% recycled polyamide

Morrow Soft Goods

Camila throw blanket

MORROW SOFT GOODS

Choose from brick red or dark brown with this hand-woven piece, which features textural loops for a 3D effect.

Materials: 100% cotton

Me & K

Ikat cashmere throw

ABASK

This blanket’s story begins in the Himalayas, where the fibers are harvested by Kashmiri artists. And while the cashmere is plush, the delicate pattern is something to applaud—it looks like it was painted right on.

Materials: 100% cashmere

Loretta Caponi

Autumn blanket

Live out your Italian nonna dreams with this chunky fringed throw. A massive embroidered flower is the main character here, as it should be (you can thank Tuscan artisans for that!). Picture this: your favorite reading chair, washed in sunlight, a cup of tea sitting on the table next to you. There’s no better way to avoid your to-do list.

Brooklinen

striped lambswool throw blanket

BROOKLINEN

Heritage runs deep with this double-faced throw—it was made under the watch of a 100-year-old, family-run mill in Germany. Its warm, neutral palette means it’ll go with just about anything, from minimalist spaces to rooms bursting with color in need of a grounding moment.

Materials: 100% lambswool

Pendleton

Yakima blanket

NORDSTROM

This is a great excuse to bring a little cabin energy home. The chunky, cozy weave and graphic stripes feel look at place on a leather chair, the foot of the bed, or wherever you like to curl up with a book.

Materials: 86% virgin wool, 14% cotton

Toast

dashed wool blanket

TOAST

For the minimalist with a funky streak. Woven in Finland from pure new wool, the playful dash pattern is perfect for adding a graphic hit of color to an otherwise neutral room.

Zara Home

cotton blanket

ZARA HOME

This is the throw blanket equivalent of a really good linen shirt—simple, unfussy, and somehow always appropriate. The subtly textured finish and fringed edges give it just enough detail to feel special.

Tekla

checked wool throw

NET-A-PORTER

Checks usually skew traditional, but Tekla gives the pattern a much cleaner point of view. The wool throw’s oversized grid is graphic enough to hold its own as a design element, whether it’s casually slung over a chair or spread across the foot of a bed.

H&M

fringed throw

Here's proof that a stylish throw blanket doesn’t need a designer price tag. This deep burgundy version has a soft, brushed feel and subtle fringe that give it a much more expensive-looking finish.

Materials: 70% polyester, 30% rayon

Studio Variously

Simply Taupe throw

A little rustic, a little refined, and very easy on the eyes. This hand-spun merino throw combines marine tones with a rich hand-loomed texture for the kind of layer you’ll want within arm’s reach all season.

Materials: 100% merino

Soho Home

Tosca throw

SOHO HOME

If your sofa is looking a little too serious, consider this a personality hire. This throw combines a rusty palette with a nubby, wool-blend texture and long fringe, giving it the layered look of something you might spot in a very chic members’ club.

Materials: 50% wool, 41% acrylic, 9% silk

Coyuchi

cozy cotton throw

COYUCHI

There’s a lot to love about a blanket that looks as good slightly rumpled as it does freshly folded. This design has a plush, puckered organic cotton texture and reverses to soft white, giving you two looks for the price of one.

Materials: 100% organic cotton

The House of Lyria

Malghe fringed throw

This is less “throw blanket” and more “small piece of Italian art.” Its textured floral pattern, moody burgundy hue, and playful fringe help it double as decorative textile—one that happens to be wonderfully useful when you want to curl up.

Materials: 72% wool, 24% cotton, 4% polyamide

Melin Tregwynt blanket

Alexander Girard originally designed this graphic plaid in 1965 for Braniff International Airways, and today the Pendleton-made throw brings that same cheerful, color-loving spirit home in a soft blend of wool and cotton.

Materials: 82% wool, 18% cotton

plaid mohair throw

This is the blanket equivalent of a really good vintage coat. The warm-toned plaid is just enough pattern, and its fuzzy mohair and wool construction is the perfect movie night companion.

Materials: 50% mohair, 49% wool, 1% polyamide

Marimekko

Iso Unikko blanket

MARIMEKKO

Leave it to Marimekko to make a throw blanket feel like a piece of modern art. The bold pattern brings the Finnish brand’s signature optimism to the sofa, bed, or anywhere else in the house that could use some pizzazz.

Materials: 44% recycled wool, 37% virgin wool, and 16% recycled polyamide

Nordic Knots

Heritage throw

NORDIC KNOTS

Rust is having a moment, and this is one way to embrace it without repainting the walls. Made from a soft wool and cashmere blend, it brings an autumnal vibe without veering into full-on cabin territory.

Materials: 90% wool, 10% cashmere

Fells Andes

rift throw

DESIGN WITHIN REACH

Your neutral sofa called—it wants some texture. The Rift throw combines smooth and twill wool weaves for a subtle, almost abstract pattern, topped off with wispy fringe for a finish that feels relaxed and far from precious.

Crate & Barrel

x Venus Williams Courtside throw blanket

CRATE & BARREL

A tennis court, translated into blanket form. Designed by Venus Williams for Crate & Barrel, this merino wool throw takes its cues from the geometry of the court, with a stitched ivory grid set against rich Ficus green for a graphic look that feels sporty in the best possible way.

Materials: 95% wool, 5% nylon

West Elm

textured throw

WEST ELM

Plaid season, but make it a little more polished. This design layers a classic checked pattern with a softly nubby texture, giving the whole thing the feel of a favorite wool jacket—just significantly more useful for chilly nights indoors.

Materials: 61% polyester, 29% acrylic, 8% wool, 2% spandex

Amber Lewis

Farley throw blanket

AMBER LEWIS

A little patina makes everything better. The faded floral motif, washed linen-cotton construction, and softly frayed edges give this blanket the character of a vintage textile, without the trip to the flea market.

Materials: 99% Linen, 1% Cotton

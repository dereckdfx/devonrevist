It’s October! Celebrate Fall Weather With the Month’s Best Dresses

Explore the best dresses of the month, from slip dresses to practical everyday knit styles. Shop a wide selection of handpicked Devon dresses.

By Andrea Zendejas

October 2, 2026

No matter the occasion or season, there are always plenty of opportunities to wear a dress and, of course, an overwhelming number of options from which to pick. We’ve put together an edit of the best dresses of the month; check back every two weeks for a new selection of styles, handpicked by Devon.

October is here, calling for wardrobe reshuffles and a fresh look at the fall trends defining the season. Based on this month's standout finds, the key elements are texture, color, and fabric. This season, Art Deco influence was seen all over the runway—lean into the trend with Kallmeyer’s Ivette dress. It’s also the season for autumnal textures in wool, velvet, and knits. Tory Burch's necktie dress works well for the office and special occasions; Cos's wool double-breasted dress is a chic layering option; and Alaïa's velvet tank dress is minimal in a gold hue, adding rich texture and color to your fall wardrobe. And for fall weddings, you'll find a slew of options from Tove to Posse.

Whether you’re shopping for your fall wardrobe, vacation, or a destination wedding, these are October’s best dresses.

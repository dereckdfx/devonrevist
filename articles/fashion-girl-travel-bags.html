We Asked 15 Stylish and Well-Traveled Women: What’s Your Go-to Travel Bag?

In search of a great new travel bag? We asked 15 fashion insiders for their go-to pieces; from sleek leather totes to slouchy suede, shop their jet-set wardrobe.

By Elly Leavitt

October 22, 2026

Alongside committing to a miles loyalty program, a solid travel bag is one of the best investments you can make if you’re someone whose monthly schedule is punctuated by red-eye flights and airport transfers. The specifics of what said travel bag looks like are up to you—a toss-up between compact handbag and roomy tote—but it does take a little market research to land on your Goldilocksian perfect match.

Or you can cut the line, and go straight to the experts. Between zipping around to market appointments and fashion weeks, shooting new campaigns, and booking it across the world for interviews and events, the fashion set knows its way around a travel accessory. We asked them for their recommendations, amassing a compendium of soft suede and structured leather pieces in the process. Their favorite bags blend form and function, with plenty of room for all the little essentials that make life at 40,000 feet a tad more enjoyable. (The key lies in organizational pouches; many, many organizational pouches.)

Fashion Insiders’ Top Travel Bag Picks:

Ready for takeoff? Ahead, 15 fashion girl-approved travel bags from brands like The Row, Phoebe Philo, and L.L. Bean.

Angela Gahng, founder of Almina Concept

“My go-to travel bag is the Margaux 15 in black suede. It holds an incredible amount, which makes all the difference when I’m flying with my daughter and need everything within reach.The suede is so soft and flexible that I can scrunch it into whatever space I have. No matter how much it gets tossed around, it keeps its shape and just looks more beautiful worn in. I also usually unbuckle the sides, which gives it a more relaxed shape and lets me fit even more.”

The Row

Roopal Patel , luxury fashion consultant

“My travel bag of choice is the large Le City bag from Balenciaga. I first saw it on my friend Vira at the Balenciaga showroom, and had to have it. I travel with a lot of pouches (for beauty, tech, and supplements), and this can fit them all, plus another bag if needed. It also has a shoulder strap, which comes in handy when traveling with multiple handbags.”

It comes with a detachable black suede pouch, which I sometimes use as a clutch, but I also typically carry the two-tone Pocket Pouch inside the tote with my emergency wired headphones, tape measure, Post-Its, two pens, Japanese mints, and a lighter. Both pouches work as evening bags when I’m traveling and want to pack light. I just returned from a seven week trip around the world and got by with only the tote and pouches, which says it all.”

I'm all about compartments. I like to be prepared, and I need to know exactly where everything is. Organized chaos, but very organized.”

Amy Sall , author

“Depending on the kind of trip (work or leisure) and how long I’ll be away, I rotate between a few staple bags as my personal item. The two I reach for most are my Totême T-Lock and my A.P.C. Drummer. The T-Lock is one of my everyday bags for just about any occasion, but I love traveling with it on trips where I’m not working and don’t need my computer. It’s compact yet roomy enough to hold all my essentials, which makes it ideal as a personal bag on flights.

For longer trips or anything work-related, I bring the Drummer. It fits my laptop and travel essentials with room to spare. I recently took it to a conference in Accra and then on to Dakar for a longer personal trip, where I still needed to get work done, and it handled both seamlessly. They also easily hold a book, a notebook, and my planner—all of which I never travel without. I’m not too precious with my bags, and both hold up beautifully, which matters when you’re tossing your bag into a TSA bin or tucking it under your seat.”

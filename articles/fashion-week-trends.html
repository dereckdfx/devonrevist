Fashion Month Heads Across the Pond! 10 LFW Looks to Recreate Now

From cool everyday looks to statement ensembles, recreate the season’s top fashion week outfits, inspired by the street style scene at London Fashion Week.

By Laura Jackson

September 22, 2026

Fashion month has officially crossed the pond, and industry insiders hit London’s historic streets in looks that embody the city’s polished heritage—with a hint of edge. British Devon’s own are delivering no shortage of chic looks, either. The magazine’s head of editorial content, Chioma Nnadi, for example, donned her signature sporty attire—with a fashionable twist, of course—while others showed off everything from new Khaite jackets to Valentino cummerbunds. The fashion verdict? Fall outfit inspiration is taking a front-row seat.

London Fashion Week Street Style Highlights:

Elsewhere, we spotted preppy checker-print trench coats with heeled ballet flats, flashes of royal purple and tangerine, funnel-neck jackets with simple white jeans, and a slew of statement accessories that take everyday ensembles to new heights. (Beaded pendant necklaces, sleek new handbags, and bold shield sunglasses, to name a few.) Statement skirts are also having a moment, from slinky slips to one bow-adorned Simone Rocha style, in particular. Footwear-wise, sandals in all forms are sticking around well into fall—think classic T-straps, heeled thong styles, and easy flip-flops. Pumps are still very much in, too. Our eyes—and carts—are especially drawn to a pointed-toe pick from Bottega Veneta and Toteme’s flashy zebra heels.

The monthlong fashion extravaganza’s European tour has just begun. Next up? Milano! Until then, shop ten London Fashion Week trends to inspire your next great look.

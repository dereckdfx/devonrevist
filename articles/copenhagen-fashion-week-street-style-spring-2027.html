The Best Street Style Photos From the Spring 2027 Shows in Copenhagen

Acielle Tanbetova is on the ground in Copenhagen photographing the best dressed guests at the spring 2027 shows.

By Acielle/Style Du Monde

August 6, 2026

Hej Copenhagen! This week we’re touching down in the Danish capital to celebrate 20 years of Copenhagen Fashion Week. In its two decades, the city has made considerable inroads in the international fashion scene. Today it doesn’t just produce influential brands with global reach, it’s also a reliable source of street style inspiration. Ranging from cool-girl functional minimalism to bold and whimsical uses of color and pattern, Scandi style is now firmly one of the most recognizable and influential aesthetics around. No matter the season, we’ve come to count on our trusty Danes to provide plenty of street style inspo, and spring 2027 is no exception.

Already, we’re picking up on some interesting trends from Northern Europe: lace, transparency, and crochet details that deliver a peek of colorful undergarments beneath. We’re also spotting a fair amount of baby bumps—one even decorated with a bit of glittery sparkle—while other guests are also stepping up their beauty game with 3-D floral motifs decorating their faces (!). Follow along as Acielle Tanbetova photographs the best of the streets from the spring 2027 shows in Copenhagen.

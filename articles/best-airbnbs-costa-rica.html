The Best Airbnbs in Costa Rica, From a Floating Cabin to a Rosy Pink Villa

Whether you want to be at the foot of the Arenal Volcano or immersed Monteverde’s cloud forest, these are the best Airbnbs in Costa Rica to book your stay.

By Michaela Trimble

October 13, 2026

A treetop studio floating in the jungle canopy of Santa Teresa, a private villa with views of the Arénal Volcano, a sprawling home tucked into a cliff above Playa Guiones… when it comes to Airbnbs in Costa Rica, you are truly spoilt for choice. The country’s landscapes offer adventurous travelers a chance to experience its wild beaches, rich biodiversity, rugged mountains, and lush tropical rainforests—all up close and personal. Think: 360-degree views and homes nearly completely shrouded by lush greenery, where visits from tropical birds is a near-daily occurrence.

Devon ’s Guide to the Best Airbnbs in Costa Rica

  • For a Cozy Artistic Studio: Hidden Art Studio in Piedras, from $175 per night
  • For a Floating Cabin in the Jungle Canopy: Naia Studio in Santa Teresa, from $207 per night
  • For Unmatched Volcano Views: Tres Volcanes Glass Cabin in Chambacú, from $282 per night
  • For a DIY Surfer’s Retreat: Pura Pura Jungle House in Santa Teresa, from $264 per night
  • For a Serene Treetop Stay: Treetop Studio in Nosara, from $325 per night
  • For a Splurgey Group Trip: Pandora Villa in Santa Teresa, from $4,108 per night

It might help to begin by pinpointing a region. Whether you pick a bohemian home in the seaside surf village of Nosara or a modern, minimalist mansion located just above Playa Hermosa (surf culture is definitely a throughline here), no matter where you land, you’ll be immersed in one of Costa Rica’s best assets: its rich natural wonders. Keep reading for 14 vacation rentals we’ve bookmarked.

Ohana Makai in Nosara

The staggering view afforded to you from nearly every room in the house (as well as the saltwater pool and accompanying outdoor sunken living room) is reason enough to book this villa, taking the concept of indoor-outdoor living to new heights. The home runs on solar, with water filtration and automated bug-repellent systems built in to make your stay more comfortable. With four bedrooms, it can sleep up to 10; bookmark this for your next group trip.

  • Rooms: 4 bedrooms, 5 bathrooms
  • Amenities: Bathtub, washer/dryer, crib, AC, balcony, pool, beach essentials, property staff available
  • Highlight: Want another layer of service? This Airbnb comes with a concierge service you can use to do things like book fitness classes directly.

El Tigre Hideaway in Monteverde

Designed as the ultimate honeymoon getaway, this treehouse immerses you in the lush landscape of the region. (In fact, it’s located right on the El Tigre Waterfalls hiking trail.) It’s so remote that you’ll need to arrange for transport from the parking lot to the cabin—though you’ll hav full access to the surrounding trails and waterfalls during your stay. There’s a well-equipped working kitchen, so as long as you stock up, you can easily spend a full week enjoying a vacation truly off the beaten path.

  • Rooms: 1 bedroom, 1 bathroom
  • Amenities: Crib, balcony, outdoor furniture, property staff on-call
  • Highlight: A central lake unites this property with a few others run by the same management; for a serene start to each morning, bring your coffee down there and enjoy some quiet before a busy day of hiking.

Boheme Boutique Apartment in Nosara

In the heart of Playa Guiones, this spot is part of a larger complex of six boutique apartments—if you don’t want a completely remote stay in Costa Rica, this is the property for you. Recently remodeled, the spacious home is surrounded by gardens and a pool, with the beach just a five-minute walk away. Inside, bright interiors skew modern, with a heavy bent to natural textures and a few design moments you may even want to bookmark for future inspiration; the orange-and-terracotta tiled shower being one of them.

  • Rooms: 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms
  • Amenities: AC, travel crib, pool, outdoor area, property staff on-call
  • Highlight: The apartment includes a gorgeous wraparound porch, fully covered from the elements with plenty of nooks to carve out as your own if you want a quiet place to read.

Deluxe Farm Stay in Monteverde

It doesn’t get any more remote, lush, and cozy than this hilltop cabin located in the jungle near Monteverde. With one of the most idyllic views in all of Costa Rica, this refuge is all about unforgettable sunsets—especially from the property’s private Jacuzzi. Even though it’s remote, the home is located within the exclusive gated community of Finca Sunset Monteverde. That means guests can rest in comfort, knowing they have a safe and private stay (as well as access to the community’s nature trail, which offers a shared viewpoint of the mountains).

  • Rooms: 1 bedroom, 1 bath
  • Amenities: Ocean view, mountain view, private Jacuzzi, private patio, gated community, dedicated workspace, indoor fireplace, cooking essentials
  • Highlight: The property is only a one-hour drive from the Arenal Volcano, making it an ideal stop for a road trip.

Pura Pura Jungle House in Santa Teresa

A short walking distance from Santa Teresa’s pristine stretch of coastline, this ground-floor apartment gives you plenty of opportunity to get outside. The one-bedroom home features a king-size bed that can be split into two twins. When guests aren’t riding Playa Santa Teresa’s famous waves, there’s a shared swimming pool to enjoy a laid-back day in the jungle with other community guests.

  • Rooms: 1 bedroom, 1 bath
  • Amenities: Beach access, shared outdoor swimming pool, reception, daily housekeeping, parking, beach towels, outdoor dining area, hammock
  • Highlight: The property is a surf hotspot, as it’s only a quick walk to Playa Santa Teresa.

The Gypsy Den in Nosara

With its expansive outdoor deck surrounded by greenery, this cozy wooden cabin is a quintessentially bohemian stay near Nosara. Located in the hills on a property abundant in fruit plants and native ceibos and Guanacaste trees, this artistic abode is the right size for a small family. While the home is only a ten-minute drive from Playa Guiones in Nosara, it’s equally as relaxing to stay in and enjoy the home’s outdoor kitchen, fire pit, and al fresco dining set-up.

  • Rooms: 2 bedrooms, 1 bath
  • Amenities: Outdoor kitchen and dining area, outdoor fire pit, AC, ceiling fan, cooking basics, dedicated workspace, hammock
  • Highlight: This home is a 10-minute drive from Ostional, a world-renowned sea turtle breeding sanctuary.

Pandora Villa in Santa Teresa

Part of the Rayo Verde community, this rosy pink villa offers one of the most luxurious stays in Santa Teresa. Perfect for big families and groups of friends, the villa features an expansive indoor dining area, a private swimming pool lined with sun loungers, and an outdoor palapa for entertaining—and since it’s mere steps from the beach, it’s a great spot for celebrations of all types (bachelorette trip, anyone?). The rooms range from king-size suites to shared twin suites, most with their own bathrooms for extra privacy. Included in a stay is a daily cleaning service, concierge perks, and even access to the community’s yoga shala and ice bath area.

  • Rooms: 5 bedrooms, 5.5 baths
  • Amenities: Beachfront access, private outdoor pool, dedicated workspace, washer and dryer, fully-equipped kitchen, outdoor dining area, sun loungers, BBQ grill, private backyard, patio
  • Highlight: Not only will the team at Pandora pre-shop for your groceries, but they’ll also help you set up excursions to surf and visit the rainforest.

Treetop Studio in Nosara

Designed with oversized domed windows that invite natural light, this open-concept beach sanctuary near Nosara is right near the town’s best beaches, shops, and dining. Perched on the third floor among lush treetops, a stay here is ideal for solo travelers, as it offers privacy, delightful views, and occasional visits from howler monkeys. Travelers can enjoy a fully-equipped kitchen, a private screened veranda with a soaking tub, and a comfortable king bed with blackout shades for more restful sleep.

  • Rooms: 1 bedroom, 1 bath
  • Amenities: Gas stove, oven, dishwasher, fully-equipped kitchen, AC, washer and dryer, hair dryer, laptop-friendly workspaces
  • Highlight: This treetop studio is located just a short stroll from Playa Guiones.

Casa Flotanta in Playa Hermosa

Perched above the jungle canopy near Playa Hermosa, just north of Santa Teresa, this stilted villa by architect Benjamin Garcia Saxe opens to sweeping Pacific views. Three pod structures with two master suites connect airy living spaces full of teak furnishings to a saltwater infinity pool framed by treetops. Only a five-minute walk to a quiet surf beach, this home is for travelers who desire a secluded perch where monkeys swing by for daily visits.

  • Rooms: 3 bedrooms, 3 baths
  • Amenities: Ocean view, private patio, private backyard, library, Marshall speaker sound system, dedicated workspace, BBQ grill, fully-equipped kitchen, stainless steel appliances
  • Highlight: The villa’s floating design and secluded 1.5-acre setting offer uninterrupted immersion in Costa Rica’s coastal jungle.

Hidden Art Studio in Piedras

Framed by fruiting mango and guava trees, this creative studio near Lake Arenal was hand-built by artists using recycled materials like glass bottles, bamboo, clay, and wood. An ideal retreat for writers, musicians, or anyone craving inspiration in nature, the studio’s interiors are whimsical and ethereal with mosaic floors, glowing bottle walls, and a main room with a Tatami-style sleeping area. But a stay here is just as much about the home as it is the surrounding forest, where guests frequently glimpses toucans, orioles, and butterflies passing by.

  • Rooms: 1 bedroom, 1 bath
  • Amenities: Forest and garden views, hammock, outdoor dining area, fire pit, bikes, fully-equipped kitchen
  • Highlight: Wake early to see the sun’s first rays ignite the bark of rainbow eucalyptus trees, creating an iridescent kaleidoscope of color.

Tres Volcanes Glass Cabin in Chambacú

This modern glass and wood cabin sits on a 56-acre ranch with clear views of three volcanoes: Arenal, Tenorio, and Rincón de la Vieja. Perched at the highest point of the property, the home is a unique stay for groups and families who desire to immerses themselves in Costa Rica’s pastoral life. During a stay, guests can enjoy slow mornings sipping coffee on the balcony as the mist curls over the treetops, then can partake in the tasks and subsequent spoils of life on a working farm: milking cows, collecting fresh eggs, and tasting honey from stingless bees.

  • Rooms: 2 bedrooms, 1 bath
  • Amenities: Farm tours, fire pit, hammock, outdoor dining, fully-equipped kitchen, Google Nest sound system
  • Highlight: A rare blend of glossy modern design and hands-on farm life, this is an ideal stay for families who want to interact with farm animals and enjoy fresh honey straight from the comb.

Casa Jaspis in Uvita

Designed by the award-winning Formafatal Studio, Casa Jaspis is a minimalist hillside retreat with a panoramic vista. The home features its own infinity plunge pool and terrace, where guests can spot toucans, monkeys, and even whales passing offshore. Inside, contemporary interiors balance clean lines with natural textures, creating a refined but grounded escape. With world-class surf at Playa Hermosa below and waterfalls and national parks nearby, it’s a design-lover’s hideaway immersed in Costa Rica’s wild beauty.

  • Rooms: 1 bedroom, 1 bath
  • Amenities: Infinity plunge pool, ocean-view terrace, fully-equipped kitchen
  • Highlight: Whale-watch, surf, or spot bioluminescent waves without ever leaving your hillside villa.

Naia Studio in Santa Teresa

You’ll feel like you’re floating in the jungle canopy of Santa Teresa at this treetop studio overlooking the ocean. As if the outdoor shower and elemental decor weren’t enough, you also have your own outdoor plunge pool. Whether in the water or enjoying a coffee in the seating area, it’s a perfect perch for spotting white face capuchin monkeys and hummingbirds.

  • Rooms: 1 bedroom, 1 bath
  • Amenities: Ocean and mountain views, central air conditioning, private plunge pool, free parking, pet-friendly
  • Highlight: The property provides organic aromatherapy bath products made in Costa Rica.

Casa Amazilia near Playa Brasilito

During a stay in this private home, you’ll have the entire grounds to yourself—including an expansive saltwater swimming pool. You’ll likely spend most of your time outdoors, given the property’s outdoor shower, patio and grill space, and massive top-floor terrace, which are perfect for entertaining guests during the area’s characteristic sunsets.

  • Rooms: 1 bedroom, 1 bath
  • Amenities: Pool view, private pool, gated community, washer/dryer, air conditioning, fully-equipped kitchen with cooking basics, BBQ grill, outdoor space with sun loungers
  • Highlight: While the property is within walking distance to Playa Brasilito, it’s also about a 10-minute drive to other nearby beaches, including Flamingo and Conchal.

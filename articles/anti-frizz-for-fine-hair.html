I Have Fine Hair—Here’s How I Keep Frizz at Bay Without Losing Volume

Smoothing fine hair no longer means sacrificing volume. Today’s anti-frizz formulas are lighter than ever, from sulfate-free shampoos that cleanse without stripping to featherlight leave-ins, heat-protecting mists, and bond-building treatments that strengthen strands from within.

By Valeriya Chupinina

October 20, 2026

I have fine hair, which means I’m stuck in a constant catch-22: smooth it with a rich cream or oil, and it falls flat by noon; skip the smoothing, and the first hint of humidity leaves me with a halo of flyaways. Add hot tools, hard water, and a forecast that can’t make up its mind, and my strands find endless new ways to rebel against me.

As it turns out, the very thing my hair seems to crave can also be its undoing. “We often think of moisture as good, and it is, but if you have dry, styled hair, moisture can change your style quickly,” trichologist Jay Small tells Devon. Once styling, color, or friction roughs up the cuticle, it lifts, soaks up water from the air, and swells in protest.

The good news? Smoothing fine hair no longer means sacrificing volume. Today’s anti-frizz formulas are lighter than ever, from sulfate-free shampoos that cleanse without stripping to featherlight leave-ins, heat-protecting mists, and bond-building treatments that strengthen strands from within. And they’re worth keeping on hand well past summer, since autumn’s cranked-up radiators and crisp, dry air bring their own brand of static.

Ahead, the frizz fighters, celebrity hairstylists, colorists, and a trichologist swear by fine hair, along with my own take on each, from drugstore steals to splurge-worthy picks.

Best Shampoo : Redken Frizz Dismiss Shampoo

Redken

Frizz Dismiss Shampoo

AMAZON

  • Why We Love It: Achieving a beautiful, smooth blowout starts in the shower with your shampoo. “As a colorist, I love this shampoo because it delivers noticeable softness, especially on frizzy, unruly hair that can be difficult to tame,” says Francesca Rodriguez , a professional hair colorist. Wash day sets the tone for everything that follows, and this sulfate-free shampoo makes a strong case for starting smooth. It cleans without that squeaky, stripped feeling you can get from salon-grade shampoos. Hairstylist Taylor Murphy recommends it to curly clients for smoothing without weight, while celebrity hairstylist Emaly Baum reaches for it when humidity spikes, in addition to working in a clarifying shampoo once a week or every few washes to keep buildup at bay. Celebrity hairstylist Mike Martinez’s rule of thumb for the perfect clean? Hair should feel clean, not scrubbed with dish soap, and leave behind enough slip to make detangling painless.
  • Editor’s Experience: Going back and forth between Paris and New York means my hair is constantly adjusting to new water and new weather, and this is the shampoo I reach for when my strands are a little frazzled from the hard water in France. After a few washes, my hair felt softer and easier to detangle, and my curls held up noticeably better on humid days. — Valeriya Chupinina, contributor
  • Key Ingredients: Babassu oil
  • Size: 10.1 ounces

Best Conditioner : Ouai Fine Hair Conditioner

Ouai

Fine Hair Conditioner

  • Why We Love It: Small likes that this sulfate-free formula cleanses features hydrolyzed keratin that strengthens fragile strands, and lightweight chia seed oil that smooths the cuticle without flattening fine hair, which makes detangling and styling easier, too. The result is weightless, all-day volume that mimics that of hair on the much thicker side, and hairstylist Becca Kraeger swears by it for adding fullness and bounce to natural curls without weighing them down.
  • Editor’s Experience: Straying away from my tried-and-true conditioner is a bit scary for me—mostly because I’m afraid of weighing my fine hair down. This conditioner from Ouai feels like it hydrates from within without giving it weight. —V.C.
  • Key Ingredients: Hydrolyzed keratin, biotin, chia seed oil, hemp seed extract

Best Primer : TRESemmé A-List Collection All-In-One Style Primer

TRESemmé

A-List Collection All-In-One Style Primer

It works across straight, wavy, and curly textures, is formulated without phthalates, parabens, or synthetic dyes, and leaves behind a vanilla and sandalwood scent that makes the prep step feel far more luxurious than its price tag suggests. Editor’s Experience: Because I have fine hair, I need everything post-conditioner to pass the lightweight formula test. When I’m particularly in a rush and need to diffuse quickly, this primer from Tresemmé does three jobs for me—heat protection, detangling, and a hint of extra hydration. —V.C.

Best Leave-In Conditioner : Pureology Color Fanatic Leave-In Conditioner & Detangling Anti-Frizz Spray

Pureology

Color Fanatic Leave-In Conditioner & Detangling Anti-Frizz Spray

  • Editor’s Experience: Ideal for my curly girl routine, this is the bottle I reach for when I’m on day two hair. I always have to brush my curls out because my hair is too fine to hold without knotting. With this formula, I detangle painlessly, leaving waves soft without feeling coated. I splash some water in, alongside my favorite mousse, and I’m good to go. —V.C.

Best Heat Protectant : L’Oreal Paris EverPure Moisture Multi-Benefit Heat Protectant Spray

L'Oréal Paris

EverPure Moisture Multi-Benefit Heat Protectant Spray

Martinez says this sulfate-free spray ‘punches well above its price tag,” and hairstylists Taylor Murphy and Miguel Rios agree, praising how it hydrates, detangles, and protects against heat up to 450°F at a drugstore price. Murphy’s tip is to mist it right after washing and before brushing, which makes it one of the easiest ways to build heat protection into a routine, something she says everyone using hot tools should be doing. It’s a great all-in-one for anyone looking for something more budget-friendly, since it leaves hair smoother and more manageable. Editor’s Experience: The older I get, the more I love a drugstore find, and this one has earned a permanent spot in my bag. Recommended to me by a few friends who aren’t in the beauty business, they considered it their go-to for blowouts, straightening, and more. It detangles and smooths nearly as well as leave-ins that cost twice as much. I love that I can use it once and get a multitude of benefits, because as someone with fine hair, reducing how many products I use is key to keeping hair from getting oily. —V.C.

Best Blow-Dry Mist : R+Co Bleu Smooth and Seal Blow Dry Mist

R+Co Bleu

Smooth and Seal Blow Dry Mist

R+CO

This featherlight mist solves that, sealing the cuticle against frizz and humidity and protecting against hot tools up to 450°F while R+Co’s Bleu Molecule Complex delivers repairing ingredients without adding weight. It’s my special item that comes out when I use a round brush and try to make my home the salon. My hair comes out glossy and smooth without any heaviness, and the finish still looks polished a day or two later. —V.C.

Best Anti-Humidity Spray : Amika The Shield Anti-Humidity Spray

Amika

The Shield Anti-Humidity Spray

  • Why We Love It: Think of this as insurance for a fresh blowout. Martinez calls humidity “the enemy of a good blowout,” and this lightweight spray uses heat-activated polymers to block it for up to 24 hours while antioxidant-rich sea buckthorn helps protect the cuticle from heat up to 450°F. Small explains that once hair is styled, moisture in the air disrupts the bonds holding your look in place, and loves that this one’s dry, non-sticky finish preserves silk presses, curls, and sleek styles without stiffness or buildup. For Rios, a little goes a long way, and unlike some sprays, Martinez notes, your hair can still move with this product. He likes it best for dry styling, misted on right before curling irons, flat irons, or wavers, so your look holds without heavy moisturizers loading down the strand.
  • Editor’s Experience: Would you believe me if I told you this stopped my tiny frizz halo in its tracks? I tested this on a sticky New York summer day, the kind that usually turns the top of my head into a nightmare just by stepping outside. I was pleasantly surprised that even by the evening, my hair was still smooth and moving freely, with none of the crunchy feel some anti-humidity sprays leave behind. —V.C.
  • Key Ingredients: Sea buckthorn, heat-activated polymers
  • Size: 5.3 ounces

Best Hair Serum : Kèratase Première Hair Repair Serum

Kérastase

Première Hair Repair Serum

  • Why We Love It: Frizz on damaged hair is usually a sign of something deeper, and this serum treats the cause as well as the symptom. Colorist Morgan Lee Cook loves this lightweight serum for highlighted and chemically-processed hair (which tends to be more porous and prone to frizz especially when it’s fine) since it pairs smoothing with heat protection without leaving strands coated. Martinez loves the entire Première line because of its focus on calcium buildup from hard water, which can make already damaged hair more prone to breakage. The entire collection utilizes glycine and peptides that help reinforce the hair fiber, so rough, overworked ends feel noticeably smoother. Celebrity hairstylist Taylor Murphy recommends it to anyone whose hair has taken a hit from color, bleach, or frequent hot tools. As she puts it, it won’t “undo five years of bleaching,” but it’s a good start. Editor’s Experience: A few drops before blow-drying is all I need to reduce the appearance of my fraying ends. My hair looks healthier and feels way smoother, and my final look comes out polished without any greasy residue. Not to those with oilier scalps like mine—avoid overuse of the product as it can make our fine hair look worse. —V.C.
  • Key Ingredients: Glycine, peptides, citric acid
  • Size: 3 ounces

Best Strengthening Treatment : Aveda Botanical Repair Bond-Building Flash Treatment

Aveda

Botanical Repair Bond-Building Flash Treatment

NORDSTROM

Noticing thinning as all fine hair girls eventually do, I needed to include something easy to my routine because more was going to be difficult for me. Who knew there was something you could put on the shelf in your shower, move through your hair, and be done? It’s the easiest and most useful step I’ve ever added to my routine. —V.C.

OGX

Repair & Protect Bond Protein Repair Treatment Mask

For Baum, this is a solid pick for the average consumer with fine hair thanks to its good price and “one-minute formula with wheat protein and panthenol [that] gives dry, damaged hair a quick boost of softness and shine.” It skips sulfated surfactants and parabens, easing heavy weight on thinner strands, perfect for days when your ends need a little extra help before styling. To use, apply it from mid-lengths to ends after shampooing and conditioning, wait a minute, and rinse. It’s our pick for an easy, low-commitment entry point for anyone who hasn’t made the hair mask a part of their hair-care routine.
  • Editor’s Experience: Whenever I can physically see the damage on my ends, I know it’s time for my OGX. This is my go-to when I feel like I’ve done a terrible job of taking care of my hair and need a quick boost. Just one minute in the shower leaves everything feeling softer and easier to style. —V.C.

Best Ionic Dryer : T3 AireLuxe Ionic Hair Dryer

T3

AireLuxe Ionic Hair Dryer

  • Why We Love It: Upgrading your dryer may be the most overlooked frizz fix of all. Sabrina Szinay , a celebrity hairstylist, says the negative ion generator in this dryer smooths the cuticle and locks in moisture, so it doesn’t dry hair out the way a standard blow-dryer does, making it worth the investment if you blow-dry often. For Small, it’s the brand’s smart heat sensor that prevents hot spots, while 15 heat-and-speed combinations and a wide airflow let you match the temperature to your hair and dry it faster. “Much of what looks like frizz after a blowout is really heat damage,” according to Baum, so a great tool is key to have in your arsenal. “The tools you use are just as important as the products you put in your hair,” states celebrity hairstylist Becca Kraeger, who praises the AireLuxe for delivering a salon-quality blowout at home. Bonus points for its lightweight build, which is easier on the arms, helpful for stylists and clients alike.
  • Editor’s Experience: I’m a T3 fanatic, so when I added this dryer to my collection, I noticed that I cut my blow-dry time in half. Yet, my hair comes out smoother and shinier with fewer flyaways. I use a medium heat setting most days, and it still dries quickly without that fried feeling at the ends. —V.C.
  • Key Features: Ionic technology, 5 heat and 3 speed settings, digitally controlled heat

Best for Sleep : Slip Silk Pillowcase

Slip

Silk Queen Pillowcase

  • Why We Love It: The easiest anti-frizz step there actually works while you sleep. Martinez admits that while a silk pillowcase “sounds a little precious,” it creates less friction than cotton, which means fewer tangles, less breakage, and a blowout that might survive another day. Silk also won’t draw moisture or treatments out of your hair overnight, and for Kraeger, who doesn’t love sleeping in a bonnet or scarf, it’s a comfortable way to stretch both blowouts and natural curls. Murphy especially recommends one for fragile, damaged, or curly hair and suggests buying two, so there’s always a backup when one is in the wash. Washing and switching it out regularly is key to keeping the benefits, and the bonus, as she points out, is that your skin will thank you, too.
  • Editor’s Experience: I’ve been a Slip girl for years, so much so that on top of sleeping on this every night, I also pack it for trips. Let me tell you, the difference is real. I wake up with fewer tangles, less frizz, and a blowout that genuinely makes it to day two, which for my oily, fine hair, is ideal on a tight schedule. —V.C.
  • Key Materials: Mulberry silk
  • Size: Queen

Alexa! Gigi! Bella! Shop Our Favorite Celeb Looks of the Week

Shop the best celebrity street style looks of the week, perfect for the season ahead—just a few simple clicks away.

By Kristina Rutkowski

September 25, 2026

Fall is finally here! If you’re wondering what to wear to embrace the first chills of the season, we’re here to simplify matters with outfit ideas, courtesy of our favorite celebrities spotted on the streets. Consider this week’s edit your guide to everything from transitional fall looks to clever layering techniques, all infused with plenty of off-duty flair.

Devon’s Favorite Celeb-Inspired Finds of the Week:

As the new season settles in, celebrities are fully embracing their fall wardrobes—and no transitional wardrobe is complete without denim. Take a cue from Gigi Hadid, who touched down in Milan for Devon World, styling a relaxed pair of jeans in earthy brown (the essential fall denim color) with a striped pink long-sleeve, accessorizing with her trusted soft loafers from The Row and Miu Miu’s Pocket shoulder bag. Meanwhile, Devon October cover star Dakota Johnson was spotted in New York wearing slim-cut jeans in a light wash, pairing them with a Glen plaid cropped jacket and deep red flats for seasonal touches. Consider it a reminder that there’s more than one way to give your everyday denim a fresh spin.

Another smart way to ease into the new season is by introducing fall’s best outerwear pieces into your wardrobe. Bella Hadid opted for a cropped trench coat over a black mini dress, adding collegiate-inspired touches with sneakerinas and a ball cap. Daisy Edgar-Jones leaned into the military jacket trend with an ecru-toned style from Alexander McQueen, worn with a tank top, low-rise bootcut jeans, and tonal pumps, proving that fall’s best outfit formulas sometimes only need a great jacket.

Keep reading to discover these ideas and more, bringing timeless classics to life with a few standout finds, all reworked by the stars and completely shoppable. Ahead, our favorite celeb outfits of the week.

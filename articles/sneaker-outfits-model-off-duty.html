Off the Runway, Into Sneakers: The Model Off-Duty Shoe Seen Across Fashion Month

Off the runway, sneakers take the lead. Take a cue from how models off-duty style the classic shoe.

By Devon

October 2, 2026

We always look to models for their effortless off-duty style, and sneakers might be the ultimate model-off-duty accessory. Outside the shows each season, you’ll spot all of our favorite styles from Adidas, Dries Van Noten, Salomon, and Nike, worn in ways that feel easygoing and cool.

Featured in this article

So, how are the models wearing them? This fall, with a range of relaxed trousers and tops with a bit of personality. Low-profile styles are paired with everything from faded jeans and striped tees to slouchy cargos and oversized shirting, while chunkier, more technical pairs ground pinstripe trousers and a hoodie. There’s plenty of high-low mixing, too. Sporty sneakers are found alongside luxe leather jackets or with big jeans and a feminine blouse.

The common thread is ease and a lack of preciousness. Here, the sneakers and their counterparts feel thrown together rather than overly styled. You get the feeling that these models are less concerned with building an outfit than they are with getting from show to show—which, of course, is what makes them so cool.

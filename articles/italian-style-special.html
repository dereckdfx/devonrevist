A Look Inside the Closets of Some of Italy’s Most Stylish Women

Shop the closets of some of Italy’s most stylish women ahead of Devon World: Milano.

By Kristina Rutkowski

September 22, 2026

Ahead of Devon World: Milano, which arrives at the storied Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II on Tuesday, September 22, we’re turning the spotlight on some of our favorite stylish Italian women—masters of the enviable art of sprezzatura.

From their sartorial signature codes and the pieces they’re shopping for this fall to the designers and details they return to season after season, these women offer a glimpse into the fashion landscape of Italy through their own wardrobes. They’re also giving us a little more about the Milan they know and love—from Fashion Week haunts and the perfect spot for a quick caffè or aperitivo to the Italian expressions and catchphrases they can’t live without. Consider this your insider’s guide to all things stile italiano, as told by the women who know it best.

Giorgia Tordini, Co-Founder and Co-Creative Director of The Attico

My Style Philosophy: “I like when there’s something slightly wrong in a look, something that feels unexpected or a little off—pieces that don’t necessarily belong together but somehow work. My style has evolved a lot over time. I used to be much more rigorous, almost monochromatic, but working on The Attico has pushed me to experiment more with proportions, shapes, color, and especially with mixing casual and evening pieces in the same look. Fall is my favorite season because it gives me more possibilities. It just gives me more to play with: layers, proportions, oversized outerwear.”

My Favorite MFW Spot: “A facial at Biologique Recherche is one of my Fashion Week essentials. It’s my reset moment in the middle of the chaos. An hour to switch off, take care of my skin and feel refreshed before everything starts again.”

My Favorite Italian Phrase: “Ti chiamo tra poco, ti devo raccontare una cosa. ‘I’ll call you in a bit, I have to tell you something.’ It’s probably the most used phrase between me and my girlfriends. It can mean anything from good news, bad news, something funny, something completely irrelevant. But you immediately know there’s a story coming. I love that little sense of anticipation and intimacy. It says a lot about the way we communicate and stay close to each other.”

Stella Novarino, Founder and Creative Director of Stella Jean

My Style Philosophy: “Uno, nessuno, e centomila—Luigi Pirandello. A phrase so powerful that it needs no verb: it contains the complexity of who we are—one identity, no fixed identity, and the countless versions reflected through the eyes of others. In fashion, and especially in styling, this becomes a language. Clothes are like words: individually they may exist, but only through their composition do they create meaning. Styling is the territory where fragments become a narrative, where a story takes shape. And yet, the person wearing that story remains forever: one, no one, and one hundred thousand.”

My Favorite MFW Spot: “Mercato Ittico. Open to the public only on Saturdays, this extraordinary market is one of Milan’s hidden rituals. Since I was a child, my father used to tell me that, paradoxically, the best fish in Italy could be found in Milan—a city without a sea. Growing up in a family where food has always been a language of pleasure and connection, I have carried this tradition with me. Even during Fashion Week, whenever I can, I still make time for this Saturday ritual. My son, who is a chef, chooses the finest scallops and sea urchins, which he transforms into two of my favourite dishes: carpaccio di coquilles Saint-Jacques and spaghetti with sea urchin. Next door, the flower market adds another layer to this morning tradition: before we leave, my daughter always returns home with armfuls of flowers — her own small ritual of beauty.”

My Favorite Italian Phrase: “Vuolsi così colà dove si puote ciò che si vuole, e più non dimandare.” — Dante Alighieri, Inferno, Canto III, vv. 95–96. “An extraordinarily refined and elegant way of saying ‘mind your own affairs’ without ever lowering the tone.”

Romy Blanga, Co-Founder and Creative Director of Eéra

My Style Philosophy: “My style is a mix of classic pieces and more current—slightly undone silhouettes. I like when an outfit feels effortless, but still has a strong point of view. For fall, my go-to is definitely an oversized T-shirt with a mini skirt or tiny shorts that are almost hidden underneath, bare legs, boots, and a jacket with strong, oversized shoulders. I love that contrast between something very masculine and oversized on top and something much more minimal and feminine underneath. Jewellery is essential to my style because it’s what makes a look feel personal. I love how even a very simple outfit can instantly become more interesting with the right jewellery. I tend to layer pieces and mix different textures and proportions, which gives everything a more elevated, almost high-end feeling. For me, it’s really about those little details that make an outfit look intentional and, at the same time, completely effortless.”

My Favorite MFW Spots: “For lunch, Saint Ambroeus is an easy favorite—very central and always a good place to run into people, especially those coming to Milan just for Fashion Week. For dinner, I like Pepe. It recently opened and has a fresh, more relaxed vibe, with a rooftop bar in the same building, which makes it an easy place to continue for a drink after dinner.”

My Favorite Italian Phrase: “A mali estremi, estremi rimedi. ‘Desperate times call for desperate measures.’ It sounds quite dramatic and ironic, but somehow implies exactly the opposite. Sometimes you just have to deal with things and move on. I like that it takes the seriousness out of a situation, which I think is always useful.”

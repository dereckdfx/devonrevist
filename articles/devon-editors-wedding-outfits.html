Here’s What 13 Devon Editors Are Wearing to Their Fall Weddings

See what fall wedding guest outfits Devon editors are putting together for their own RSVPs–and get inspired for your own.

By Alexandra Buell

October 6, 2026

With crisp temperatures, changing leaves, and an excuse to embrace a richer, more layered wardrobe, autumn makes quite the scenic season for saying “I do.” Which means anyone with a fall wedding on the calendar is likely once again asking themselves the eternal question: Once I’ve saved the date, what will I wear? Our editors are not exempt from this quandary—and with a long list of wedding guest dress trends to choose from, our staffers have plenty of inspiration for any upcoming nuptials (and all festivities in between). Think, infinitely re-wearable black ensembles, long dresses in deep jewel tones, and, of course, statement earrings and dance floor-appropriate kitten heels to complete the look.

Whether you’re attending a black-tie soirée or an intimate daytime celebration, find inspiration from 13 Devon editors, ahead.

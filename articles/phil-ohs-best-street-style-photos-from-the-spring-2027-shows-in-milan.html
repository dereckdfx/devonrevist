The Best Street Style from the Spring 2027 Ready-to-Wear Shows in Milan

Phil Oh captures the stylish scene outside the spring 2027 shows at Milan Fashion Week.

By Phil Oh

September 24, 2026

As faithful readers of Devon will know, the best looks at Fashion Week are not confined to the runway. Those teeming crowds just outside the shows are their own lively scene, filled with exciting, unexpected, and downright inspired takes on what’s happening in fashion. Which is why Phil Oh (@mrstreetpeeper), our beloved street style lensman, went out on le stradas di Milano to capture all the action. As Devon World: Milan just proved, Italian style is its own particular wonder and difficult to pin down. From sensual suiting to expressive gowns, the country is known for marrying artistic whimsy with craftsmanship. And as these street style images show, that spirit is alive and well outside the spring 2027 shows. Take a look at our favorite street style looks from the spring 2027 shows in Milan.

Every Spring 2027 Sneaker Trend You Can Already Shop

From the white derby to color-blocked retro runners, Devon breaks down spring 2027’s top sneaker trends—and where to shop them now.

By Kristina Rutkowski

October 20, 2026

Now that fashion month has officially wrapped, it’s time to take stock of the trends set to define the season ahead. While most of spring/summer 2027’s biggest moments will have to wait a few months for their real-world debut (think: Prada’s statement skirts or Chanel’s zebra-print pumps), sneakers are one key investment you can step into now. From directional new silhouettes to stylish re-imaginings of tried-and-true classics, spring 2027’s biggest sneaker trends can already fit into your shoe rotation.

Spring 2027’s Top Sneaker Trends:

Following in the footsteps of fall 2026, retro runners are returning for spring—with vibrant colorways and a renewed sense of cool. Leading the charge was Miu Miu, whose latest New Balance collaboration reconstructed the archival RC150 racing sneaker in punchy shades, from cobalt blue and neon yellow to tomato red and vibrant orange. Balenciaga made a case for low-profile, color-blocked kicks in highlighter hues, while Stella McCartney offered a vegan suede take on the sporty staple—it’s safe to say low runner retro-inspired sneakers are back in a big way.

On the opposite end of the spectrum, minimalist sneakers offered a lesson in the art of restraint. From the crisp, white derby-style sneakers at Celine—a now-signature house favorite—to the little black sneakers spotted at Jil Sander and Polo Ralph Lauren, these understated styles proved that, sometimes, less really is more. Whether you gravitate toward a vibrant pop of color or prefer to keep things pared-back, next season’s sneaker lineup has a style for every sartorial sensibility.

Ahead, discover the key sneaker trends to know for spring 2027, along with the pairs worth adding to your rotation already.

The Retro Runner

Though they’ve evolved beyond their athletic origins, retro runners are back in a big way for spring 2027. Miu Miu debuted its latest New Balance collaboration on countless looks, reimagining the archival RC150 racing sneaker in punchy shades, while Balenciaga opted for color-blocked kicks in highlighter hues. For something more understated, Stella McCartney offered a vegan suede take on the sporty staple. Looking to get ahead of the trend? Nike’s Moon Shoe and J.W. Anderson’s latest Diadora collaboration are two pairs you can to your rotation now.

The Minimalist Derby

Proof that a shoe doesn’t need bells and whistles to make a statement, allow us to introduce you to the minimalist derby sneaker. Celine revisited its now-signature white lace-up, while Auralee and Jil Sander embraced similarly pared-back designs that put clean lines and understated detailing first. Versatile and decidedly more polished than your average gym shoe, these pared-down styles look best a little worn in. Classic Keds and Ralph Lauren’s low-tops are two pairs you can wear now and again in the spring.

The Skater Shoe

Another trend that needs no introduction, fashion’s love affair with gum-soled sneakers runs deep and shows no signs of slowing next season. Nodding to both classic skater styles and preppier deck shoes, Polo Ralph Lauren, Burberry, and Louis Vuitton put their own spin on the nostalgic silhouette across the runways. For an easy everyday option available currently, reach for Vans Authentics or Sperry’s deck sneakers, or look to The Row and Phoebe Philo for more elevated interpretations.

The Little Black Kick

Just like the derby sneaker, the little black kick proves that simplicity never goes out of style. For spring 2027, streamlined shapes in rich noir emerged as another minimalist must-have, with Celine, Jil Sander, and Polo Ralph Lauren favoring clean silhouettes, subtle detailing, and understated finishes. Until these officially launch, shop tried-and-true styles like Prada’s Collapse sneaker, Proenza Schouler’s Arcadia, and Toteme’s Flex for a sleek take on an everyday essential.

The Earth Tones

Much like denim, sneakers are leaning into earth tones for spring, with shades of olive, khaki, and brown bringing a grounded feel to the seasonal lineup. At Moschino, Simone Rizzo and Loris Messina’s debut tapped into gorpcore styles inspired by the great outdoors, while TWP explored utilitarian designs. Whether rugged and trail-ready or pared back for everyday wear, these nature-inspired sneakers offer a fresh alternative to the usual neutrals.

The Life of the Party

Embellishment and decoration aren’t leaving the conversation either, with designers embracing playful details that make a statement. Coach brought a funfetti effect to its distressed white low-tops, while Burberry adorned its slip-ons with bunches of fanciful floral paillettes. Until these styles hit stores, lean into anything that feels a little extra—think sequins, high-shine finishes, and intricate embroidery for a dose of personality.

The Distressed

Thanks to the beat-up Celine x Reebok pairs that walked the spring 2027 men’s show, and reappeared on the women’s runway this month, real-life wear and tear is being welcomed, if not encouraged. Coach offered its own take on the lived-in look, with artfully worn finishes on simple white low-tops. Shop Golden Goose’s Super-Star sneakers and Autry’s deliberately scuffed pairs ahead of the highly anticipated drops—a reminder that a little patina is what gives pieces its character.

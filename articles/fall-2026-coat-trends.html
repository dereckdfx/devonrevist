8 Fall Coat Trends Worth Investing In

The chill has set, which means the fall 2026 coat trends won’t stick around for long. Get to know the season’s key looks, and shop our favorite styles.

By Andrea Zendejas

October 20, 2026

Cooler temperatures swept through New York City this past weekend, which means one thing: coat season is here. And what better excuse than to refresh your outerwear wardrobe? After all, when temperatures drop, your coat becomes the look. And if you’re anything like us, we’re itching to wear a coat that makes a statement.

The Fall 2026 Coat Trends to Shop

Strong outerwear anchored the fall 2026 collections. Toggle coats appeared in both familiar neutrals and bold color stories, fastened with leather or woven rope, at Dries Van Noten, Louis Vuitton, and Polo Ralph Lauren. Elsewhere, the shearling—a perennial favorite—returned in sculpted form with a cinched waist, offering a fresh take on cold-weather dressing at Loewe and Bottega Veneta.

Equally noteworthy were the robe coats (belts optional) at Colleen Allen and Calvin Klein, cut in soft shapes for a natural drape. The funnel-neck coat remained a key style for the season, too iterated on in midi and maxi lengths at Gucci and Tory Burch. On the more maximalist side of the spectrum, we saw a steady supply of animal print options, along with jacquard and fur-trimmed takes, for a sense of emboldened everyday glamour.

This season’s outerwear trends have you, expectedly, covered, whether you’re replacing an old faithful, working in a statement piece, or channeling a bit of both.

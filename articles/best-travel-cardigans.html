For the Simplest Way to Elevate Your Airport Style, Try a Travel Cardigan

From cashmere to cotton, the travel cardigan is about to be your new airport style MVP—shop options from Khaite, Doen, and more.

By Rosie Jarman

October 20, 2026

Whether facing a long-haul flight or a weekend retreat just a quick jaunt away, packing for any trip requires a little bit of strategy and plenty of planning. You have limited space and weather changes (not to mention, cabin air conditioning) to navigate; of course, you want to stay comfy through it all. We’re here to argue on behalf of one key player in your travel wardrobe: the cardigan. Occupying the sweet spot between a wrap scarf and a full pullover, the travel cardigan is practical and versatile, an easy layer to toss in your tote bag that you’ll be glad you remembered to bring.

Devon ’s Guide to the Best Travel Cardigans

The keyword here is ease. A cardigan is easy to throw over your shoulders or tie around your waist as you navigate miles of airport terminal. It’s easy to cozy up in once you’re in your seat and ready for takeoff. And once you’re at your destination? Easy to style with all the other pieces in your suitcase. It’s a no-brainer for fall and winter travel, but even if you’re headed to a tropical destination, a lighter-weight piece will come in handy on chilly nights.

From designer options from the likes of Khaite, Róhe, and The Row to more accessible options from Dôen, Alex Mill, and J.Crew, there are plenty of cozy options to pick from on the market at the moment. Choose from classic crew cardis or slouchy v-necks; belted wraps that drape beautifully; vibrant splashes of tomato red and cobalt blue or classic shades of camel and dove grey. Whichever route you take, you’ll soon discover that the travel cardigan is your airport style MVP; shop 26 of our favorite options, ahead.

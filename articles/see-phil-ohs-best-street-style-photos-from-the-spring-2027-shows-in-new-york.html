See Phil Oh’s Best Street Style Photos From the Spring 2027 Shows in New York

Phil Oh documents New York’s best-dressed fashion show-goers at the spring 2027 shows.

By Phil Oh

September 16, 2026

New York City is brighter than ever. And not just because it’s New York Fashion Week, but after a rainy few months we’re seeing street stylers having more fun with their style than ever. Not only are the summer trends at the menswear shows following through, there is also more color and more experimentation. Despite the muggy weather, we’re seeing an influx of coats, which also might be thanks to Stuart Vevers, while scarf dressing seems to show no sign of stopping. Luckily for everyone Michael Rider has given a multitude of ways to show off silky accessories. Below, the best street style photos from the spring 2027 shows in New York.

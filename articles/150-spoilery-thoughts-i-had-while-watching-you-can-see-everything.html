150 Spoilery Thoughts I Had While Watching You Can See Everything

Fielder playing “Who’s on First?” for Elizabeth Holmes while she holds her baby is making me short-circuit.

By Emma Specter

October 16, 2026

Pretty much all I’ve been able to think about this week is Nathan Fielder’s new Elizabeth Holmes documentary You Can See Everything, and now, praise God, I have very much seen it with my own eyes.

My primary takeaway from this bizarre three-hour masterpiece is that human beings are fascinating in their heartrending fallibility and infinite ego… but I also recorded (quite literally) every thought I had while watching the documentary. Read them all below, but be warned: spoilers ahead.

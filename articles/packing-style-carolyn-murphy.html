My Packing Style: Model Carolyn Murphy

Take a peek inside model Carolyn Murphy’s travel bag—as well as the packing tips she swears by.

By Elly Leavitt

September 22, 2026

When it comes to mastering the art of the travel wardrobe, it’s no surprise that our favorite fashion insiders also moonlight as packing pros. In My Packing Style, we tap some of the most well-traveled people we know for a peek inside their travel bags and vacation wardrobes to see how they take their style on the road. You’ve bookmarked the trip, you’ve screenshotted the outfits; now, a look at how it all comes together.

If we had to wager which professional packs for life on the go best, we’d say that few do it better than models. Take Carolyn Murphy for example; or more specifically, take her upcoming travel schedule. “I’m off to Paris, then taking the Orient Express to Florence, then headed to Milan for Devon World and to launch my new collection with La DoubleJ,” she says. We caught her as she was prepping for a whirlwind few weeks on the road—who better to impart some travel capsule wardrobe wisdom?

Shop Carolyn Murphy’s Suitcase

By this point a seasoned professional at the art of red-eye style, Murphy has her airport uniform down. She’s a fan of a sleek pant and a menswear blazer; put-together, but off-duty enough for the cabin. The contents of her actual suitcase are similarly expertly edited—a matching set or two (her favorites are Phoebe Philo and vintage YSL), some basic button-downs, an array of neutral heels, if you count leopard print. “I’m quick and methodical after a lifetime of travel,” she says.

Ahead, Murphy shares how she puts together a capsule wardrobe, what beauty products are currently in her plane bag, and why she’ll always defend over-packing.

Mentally, days ahead. I like to plan my outfits and imagine them in the environment. I’m definitely a pre-packer—the night before. It makes the day of travel so much easier, and I like to move slow.

I do prefer to pack outfits for work trips (I’m more relaxed for vacations). I know what I like and everything works well together so it’s easy to mix things up if my mood changes.

I like classic pieces that are stylish, transitional, and can be layered for any climate. I always go back to brands like The Row, Khaite, TWP, and Sablyn for essentials, Eres for lingerie and swimwear, Kallmeyer and Magda Butrym for elevated day or eveningwear.

My strategy is that accessories make the outfit! I usually have too many shoes, and at least two day bags; then, an evening clutch and evening shoe. My everyday jewelry is minimal but I’ll occasionally add in a statement watch, necklace, or bracelet.

I’m not too worried about fabrics and most places have steamers on hand… but if not, I’ll just hang the garment on a hanger in a hot shower. It does the trick every time. I think if your suitcase is well-packed and organized, it makes unpacking at the destination so much easier. I abhor “living out of my suitcase,” so organizing my things upon arrival helps me enjoy my space and time more.

I would say my Simone Rocha slip dresses, because they can be worn anywhere, any season. I’ll add a cardigan and tights for cooler months.

I like sets: a jacket and a matching pencil skirt or trouser. My go-tos are Frankie Shop, Phoebe Philo, or vintage YSL. This way I can wear the pencil skirt with a vintage t-shirt or fitted sweater; and use the jacket with jeans and a sexy bodysuit.

I overpack and I leave room! I like to have the choice in choosing outfits and/or acquiring more via shopping; and I do love finding treasures like linens, stationary, and vintage clothing.

It’s best to pack more than less. Also, pack your clothes laying flat—I am de-bunking the roll-up theory—because you can fit more in your suitcase.

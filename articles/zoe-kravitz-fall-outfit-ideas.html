Fall Fashion Done Right—Zoë Kravitz Is Our Off-Duty Muse

Whether on- or off-duty, the actor rarely strays from her minimalist cool uniform. Shop 9 of her best recent outfits, to give that downtown cool factor all season long.

By Kristina Rutkowski

October 14, 2026

Zoë Kravitz is the kind of timeless It girl whose fashion sense always exudes the cool factor. Whether strolling the streets of Brooklyn or globe trotting around the world for a red carpet event, we can’t help but take serious style notes every time she steps outside. Kravitz has had a busy few weeks with Fashion Month wrapping up and the many off-duty sightings in New York as she supports fiancé Harry Styles during his Madison Square Garden residency. Naturally, she’s delivered no shortage of covetable looks, giving us plenty of style inspiration along the way.

Devon ’s Zoë Kravitz-Coded Fall Shopping List:

As a face of Saint Laurent and a super fan of The Row, Kravitz is regularly sporting both labels, but beyond her designer go-tos, she has an incredibly unique way of putting looks together for everyday life that really makes us want to shop. With a closet full of styling tricks worth borrowing, her latest looks couldn’t come at a better time, especially with the change in seasons—because who doesn’t need a little fall wardrobe TLC?

Her fall formulas aren’t just filled with trending items—they’re also grounded with minimalist staples you’ll keep in your closet for years to come. Think really good T-shirts, vintage denim, and black ballerina heels perfect for daytime (or weekend!) wear.

Ahead, nine fall outfits inspired by Zoë Kravitz’s signature style, plus all the pieces you need to recreate each look. Trust us, they really are all that good.

Off-Duty Essentials

Oversized white shirting and flowy, black wide-leg pants are staples in Kravitz’s closet. She keeps the combination feeling fresh with sporty accents, including a graphic ball cap and black Adidas Sambas sneakers that also happen to be Olsen-approved. A semi-sheer scarf adds a touch of elegance, while her go-to Saint Laurent tote and oversized sunnies lend the look that signature off-duty edge.

Lace After Hours

If there is one trend that Kravitz returns to time and again, it’s sultry black lace for evening. At friend and collaborator Anthony Vaccarello’s final Saint Laurent show during PFW, she wowed in a matching top and skirt—a natural extension of her sheer lace Met Gala moment. Transitional Suede

Introducing fall-forward fabrics is one of the easiest ways to transition your wardrobe between seasons. For those lingering warm days of autumn, pair a brown suede jacket with a shrunken tee and micro shorts, then finish with suede ballet heels for even more texture. A commuter-friendly tote and red ball cap keep things in classic Zoë territory, striking the balance between laid-back ease and Brooklyn cool.

The Rock ’N’ Roll Touch

Another way to wear suede, this time with a bit of rock-and-roll edge courtesy of a pair of brown pants. Kravitz paired hers with a loose-fitting T-shirt, kitten-heeled sandals, shield sunglasses, and her trusty woven tote from The Row for a tactile finish, where summer staples meet the richer textures of fall. As temperatures dip, layer on a jacket and swap the sandals for boots.

There’s nothing more classic than a white T-shirt and jeans, a combination that Kravitz has on repeat. Ready, Steady, Go!

Racking up frequent flier miles, Kravitz proves that airport style can be just as chic as it is comfortable. Paired with ballet heels, a roomy woven bag, and oversized shield sunnies, her printed pashmina breaks up the all-black uniform, adding both a dose of personality and a warm layer while on board.

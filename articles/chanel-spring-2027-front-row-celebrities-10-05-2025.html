Celebrity Style

A Front Row Scene Report From Chanel’s Spring 2027 Show

Dua Lipa! Ayo Edebiri! Pedro Pascal! Photographer German Larkin gets up close and personal with all of the stars at Chanel in Paris.

By Christian Allaire, German Larkin

October 5, 2026

Chanel and Paris Fashion Week go hand-in-hand like a fresh baguette and butter. It’s always one of the buzziest (if not the buzziest) shows of the week, and tonight, designer Matthieu Blazy’s unveiling of his latest collection—spring 2027—was certainly no different.

Zeroing in on Chanel’s signature tweeds and skirt suits, Blazy showed his latest craft-driven approach to the enduring house codes—all shown within a majorly theatrical set, complete with larger-than-life flying seagull sculptures. (A nod to the show’s overarching theme of lightness and taking flight.)

The front row, meanwhile, was as exciting as the catwalk: A-listers such as Nicole Kidman, Pedro Pascal, Ayo Edebiri, Dua Lipa, Marion Cotillard, Alexa Demie, and more all showed up for the affair (wearing their finest Chanel duds, naturally).

And when there’s a star-studded VIP list, Devon knows who to call: We enlisted photographer German Larkin to document all of the buzziest names in attendance. (Larkin also documented the Gucci show back in Milan.) Below, scroll on for exclusive sightings from Chanel’s spring 2027 show.

Ayo Edebiri

Describe your show look today.

This just felt very fun and nice to put on. And it's really comfortable. Sometimes it's nice just to feel comfortable and like a version of yourself. Like a slight drag version of yourself.

What did you think of the show's soundtrack?

I loved “You Don't Own Me.” That artist is amazing. And then into “Gimme More”—that was really energetic.

If you could fly anywhere right now as a bird, where would it be?

If I were a bird, I might fly to New Zealand. Or one of the Polynesian islands. Just because I've never been there before and it might be nice to have a migratory pattern there.

Dua Lipa

For the look, we decided more is more is more, and that's the way I like it. I decided to do a double little bag situation. I was obsessed with it: It doesn't really fit anything—some mints!

What did you think of the show?

It's always amazing. The soundtrack was amazing. It was really emotional in some moments, and then I got hit with Britney, and all of a sudden I was so ready to dance. Everyone was dancing.

To the bar.

What is your unexpected Paris must-do when in town?

Apart from eating some delicious food and having some good wine, I'd also say go to the pharmacy and stock up on Biafine. It's amazing.

Pedro Pascal

I said I hate turtlenecks, and then they put me in a turtleneck. And I was wrong! They're going to try and get this trench coat back, but I don't know—we'll see. It could get physical.

It was hard to sit down. There was a moment in the show where we all hard to restrain ourselves from [getting up.] Once the gorgeous models all finished their walk, we had to stop ourselves from turning it into a dance party. I'll never forget the first time I saw the “Take On Me” music video when I was a child, as an MTV kid.

[Kristen Wiig and I] would go to the beach. We'd go to a nude beach in Portugal. We would just float and hold our wings out. And we'd shit on naked people.

Marion Cotillard

I would go anywhere with my kids. Right now, probably L.A. I love the city and part of my family is there—my brother is there.

The soundtracks are always amazing. The Britney songs! It's always old and new at the same time, super modern with old songs. I loved it.

I love to walk. I can go from north to south, or east to west. There's a lot to discover and an amazing city to walk.

JT

I'm wearing the opening look from fall 2026. I brought my hat from home and added it to the look.

When I heard “it's Britney, bitch"—that's why the collection is so fun! I couldn't stop moving the whole show. I loved the one song that sounded like a video game.

I would fly to the moon, to see if you can really land on it.

The park, the park, the park.

Alexa Demie

I went into my fitting and there was a rack of beautiful clothing. This was the first one that caught my eye. I didn't really need to try anything else on.

He always has such an incredible soundtrack. He always surprises me with what he mixes in, but this one especially surprised me. It brought a lot of joy to everybody watching.

If I was a bird. . . I've thought about this a lot. I don't think it would matter where I went. I would be so fulfilled just flying. Maybe I would fly over Los Angeles, because that's home and I would like to see it from that point-of-view. Or maybe Egypt. I would fly everywhere.

What I did when I got here was I went to a lot of beautiful churches. Some of the museums were closed unfortunately, but the churches were really beautiful.

John Malkovich

I just came in from Mexico, where I had some work for a couple days. We went and looked at a few things very quickly. I liked how this shirt was manufactured with a lot of care and cleverness.

The whole soundtrack was amazing. I wouldn't single anything out.

Once I took a cruise on the Queen Elizabeth II, and there was a problem with the ship—an engine caught on fire. I remember there was this incredible tiny little bird, which had the most fantastic name: It's called the stormy petrel, and they only exist out to sea. It would be a pretty good life to just be that, really.

Olivia Wilde

This look is giving mermaid Joan of Arc. It's liquid chainmail in every color that I've ever wanted to wear at once.

The whole show was emotional. We were all going from tearing up to dancing to Britney. It went from “Swan Lake” to “it's Britney, bitch.” Marion and I were immediately dancing. It was very intense. In the end, it felt like a profession of strength—there was a level of darkness to the whole thing that was totally appropriate for the times. But it was also very inspiring.

My family is Irish, so growing up, I equate the idea of flying to seagulls and the sea. So I'd probably fly home.

In Paris, one vintage store that I love to go to is Open Dressing. It was off my radar until I put it on my radar. Now I go every time I'm here. She always has the best pieces. Other than that. . . anytime you see a line for something, run.

Kristen Wiig

I tried on a lot of amazing things, and I couldn't decide. But then I put this on and we all agreed. I feel amazing in it, and I'm so happy to be here.

Anytime I hear “it's Britney, bitch”—my shoulders start to move.

There's no place I'd rather be than here. Maybe I'd go to some sort of fair? A fair with lots of people. Very hot. Eat lots of junk food.

Imani Imani

This is all Chanel—they dressed me. I love this skirt and the shoes.

Right now, I would fly to the studio to make music, because I'm super inspired.

I haven't been to Paris a lot, to be honest. I'm here with my best friens and we're just having fun!

Lorena Lalina Schuett and Miu Natsha Taechamongkalapiwat

Taechamongkalapiwat: Copenhagen, since I've never been there. I love exploring new places, so I'd just pick a city or place I haven't discovered yet and go.

Schuett: I'd love to fly to Copenhagen. I've never been, but it seems like the kind of place where you can enjoy both the energy of a city and the calmness of being close to nature. That balance is something that really appeals to me.

Taechamongkalapiwat: Just walking around and exploring pretty streets, stopping by cute French bistros to hunt for the best french onion soup (my childhood favorite dish), and getting a crepe from a little street stand. I feel like those simple moments are the best part of Paris.

Schuett: Honestly, just wandering without a plan. Some of my favorite memories in Paris come from getting a little lost, finding an appealing cafe, a book store, or a street I've never seen before.

Alexandra Eala

It's my version of the “Chanel girl” blending masculine structure with feminine details.

Fly across the Sene to see where it takes me. Maybe the French countryside.

Visit Gabrielle Chanel's apartment.

Wang Yibo

Rosie Huntington-Whiteley

Ella Rubin

I'm wearing this lovely suede skirt and a black sweater. I just wanted to feel like myself, and be comfortable. That's what's lovely about Chanel: You're usually very comfortable in your outfit.

It's my first Chanel show and my first Paris Fashion Week. It's truly wild. It was such a beautiful show. My seat mates were legendary. I was nervous, but everyone was really lovely. The soundtrack was unbelievable. It made me feel really excited and emotional.

I would probably stay right here. I would fly around in Paris. It's my first time here, and I'm really loving getting to be here.

People have got to check out the Effel Tower. It's underrated! Because it's such a thing, there's this horseshoe theory, where it actually becomes underrated. Next time I'm in Paris I might have a cooler answer.

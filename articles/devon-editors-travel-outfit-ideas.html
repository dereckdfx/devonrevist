What Devon Editors Are Wearing to Travel This Fall

Planning your vacation? It starts with the right fall travel outfit—we polled our editors for their go-to airport formulas.

By Elly Leavitt

October 9, 2026

With fashion month just wrapped and various fall getaways in the works (including a handful of post-fashion month R&R long weekends), team Devon is scattering this season. Plans include Berlin for a marathon, Upstate New York for an open-air art museum, and many (many) destination weddings. The itineraries have little in common; the travel outfits, though, are another story.

Devon Editors’ Fall Travel Outfit Essentials

Curious to see how our editors dress for freezing plane cabins and hours crammed in a car alike, we polled the team. From the weekender bags that can hold a surprising amount of cargo to the roomy sweaters that have reached frequent flier status and the sneakers that’ll get you through security with ease, here are 10 editor-approved fall travel outfits to inspire your own.

Talia Abbas , shopping director

“I was traveling especially light on a recent trip to Berlin and Paris (half my carry-on was dedicated to running gear for the Berlin marathon), so my airport outfit was really just another version of my everyday uniform. Ballet flats and a black pant are my kind of comfy (La Ligne’s Colby pants exceptionally so), and I always wear my jacket to save space. I’m loyal to the plane sweater and this time around brought a simple cashmere crewneck from Jenni Kayne along for the ride. My Alex Mill weekender holds all the sundry extras I can’t fit in my suitcase, including a small day bag to use throughout the trip.”

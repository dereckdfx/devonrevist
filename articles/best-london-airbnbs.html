Fireplaces, Bathtubs, and Backyards—The Best Airbnbs in London Are a Vibe

The best airbnbs in London plant visitors in the heart of this vibrant capital—here’s where to stay, according to Devon.

By Nicole Kliest, Madeline Weinfield

September 25, 2026

London has its fair share of landmarks befitting a refrigerator magnet, and an equal number of ardent fans. (Famously: “When a man is tired of London, he is tired of life,” per Samuel Johnson.) The capital is a city of endless layers, where centuries-old history and culture exists alongside a vibrant and ever-evolving creative scene. From world-class museums to grand architectural landmarks and lush, expansive parks, London rewards (and surprises) even the most frequent visitor.

  • For a Colorful Cottage: Stylish & Spacious Family Home , from $1,278 per night
  • For Backyard Sunday Roasts: Art-Inspired Home from $680 per night
  • For a Family Stay Near the Park: Stunning Hyde Park House from $2,008 per night
  • For Mid-Century Inspiration: Mid-Century Mews House from $657 per night
  • For a Bright Rustic Spot: Hackney Warehouse from $325 per night
  • For a Maximalist Townhouse: Stunning Period Property from $1,368 per night
  • For a Unique Houseboat Experience: The Floating Terrarium , from $379 per night

One of the best ways to experience the city is to slip into local life by staying at an Airbnb. From industrial lofts in Shoreditch to stately townhouses in Fulham and eclectic flats in Hackney, these rentals offer a more intimate way to experience London, often including period features like claw-foot tubs, wood-burning fireplaces, and eclectic furnishings that showcase the best of British design. Here’s where to stay on your next journey to London—it’s an ever-evolving guide, but we’re sure you’ll find your next home away from home within these 22 Airbnbs.

Fulham Townhouse

There’s ample room to spread out in this luxurious townhouse—six bedrooms’ worth, to be precise. Whether it’s lounging in the private backyard or on the roof deck, soaking in the indoor pool or the many bathtubs, or enjoying the sauna or cinema room, there are plenty of reasons to stay on the property. Yet just outside, the beauty of leafy, residential Fulham awaits and walks along the Thames beckon.

Rooms: 6 bedrooms, 6.5 bathrooms

Amenities: Indoor pool, roof deck, sauna, hot tub, air conditioning, washer, movie theater, indoor fireplace, outdoor dining area

Location: Fulham

Light-Filled Home

It’s easy to imagine slow mornings unfolding at this two-bedroom apartment in Hackney Downs, whether you’re leafing through the extensive book collection or lingering over a full English breakfast at the dining table beside the fireplace. While set within a Victorian-era building, the design leans into a 1970s aesthetic with colorful bedspreads, modular furniture, and shaggy Moroccan rugs that fill the rooms with character. Outside, Hackney Downs Park and the café-lined streets of Clapton are just a short walk away.

Rooms: 2 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms

Amenities: Pet-friendly, fireplace, washer/dryer, TV, record player

Location: Hackney

Stylish & Spacious Family Home

This three-bedroom family home has a touch of everything that comprises a great getaway: a gym, sauna, and cold plunge for a morning refresh, a large garden for afternoon lounging, and even an outdoor kitchen and BBQ area for warm-weather dining. Inside, the decor leans very much into the warm eclecticism of a British cottage. Cozy up in the sitting room, which comes with a projector for catching up on your favorite television programs, or spend lazy mornings in the kitchen and dining area. Color and pattern runs throughout; a stay here is a real visual treat.

Rooms: 3 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms

Amenities: Bathtub, washer/dryer, central heating, backyard, fire pit, outdoor dining area, BBQ, free parking, sauna.

Location: Kensal Rise

Shoreditch Loft

This light-filled loft makes an ideal home base for exploring the creative energy of Shoreditch. Inside, original wood floors, oversized contemporary art pieces, and mid-century furnishings mix with industrial details like a steel door, exposed brick walls, and streamlined track lighting. The resulting effect is one that seems entirely at home in the famously arty neighborhood, with galleries, cafes, and independent boutiques just steps away. The Columbia Road Flower Market and Brick Lane are also within easy reach.

Rooms: 1 bedroom, 1 bathroom

Amenities: Elevator, washer and dryer, TV

Location: Shoreditch

The Prince of Albert

This large, airy flat in South West London comes with a private roof terrace—never take outdoor space in London for granted—and is right by Battersea Park. The decor draws upon natural textures and colors to create a soothing atmosphere (including original art and antiques for a homey touch); step outside, and you’re a 10-minute stroll from vibrant Chelsea.

Amenities: Bathtub, washer/dryer, backyard.

Location: Battersea

Terrace House

You’ll feel like a proper local staying at this Victorian terrace house near Brixton. The interiors are bright and whimsical, with pastel paint and mix-and-match decor peppered throughout the home’s three bedrooms and living space. It all leads out to a small, charming garden, which comes equipped with an electric green bistro set for enjoying your meals al fresco.

Rooms: 3 bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms

Amenities: Bathtub, washer/dryer, wood-burning fireplace, central heating, backyard, outdoor dining area, sun loungers.

Location: Camberwell/Brixton

The Grand Penthouse

Prefer something shiny and new? Situate yourself in one of London’s most prestigious neighborhoods—Mayfair—within this elegant penthouse apartment, which was just renovated in March 2026 and comes with a slate of hotel-like amenities. Sleep on 1000 thread count Egyptian cotton bedding, and take your breakfast out to the private rooftop terrace. You’ll be a short walk from bakeries (Claridge’s Bakery is a newer and much-welcome addition to the local pastry scene) you can stop by in the mornings and bring an assortment of breads and tarts home to enjoy in the fresh air.

Amenities: Bathtub, washer, central heating, outdoor dining area.

Location: Mayfair

Garden Flat

From the plant-filled living room that’s almost like a mini greenhouse to the lush backyard outfitted with patio furniture, this home is like your very own secret garden—right in the middle of the city. Luxe amenities, like a smart TV, espresso maker, and outdoor barbecue, will make you feel right at home. And when you want to head out: There are tons of chic shops and small bakeries in this neighborhood to explore.

Rooms: 2 bedrooms, 1 bathroom

Amenities: Bathtub, washer/dryer, HDTV, Bluetooth sound system, travel crib available upon request, board games, indoor fireplace, private backyard, outdoor furniture, grill, cleaning available upon request

Location: Islington/Hackney

Characterful Garden Flat

Located just a little outside of Central London (but still within easy metro distance) this Airbnb is great for those looking for something a little quieter. It’s on a tree-lined street that’s near a number of bakeries and cafes, with a fenced-in backyard for more privacy. A stained glass window and baby blue window nook are design features to bookmark.

Amenities: Bathtub, washer/dryer, TV, board games, high chair and baby bath available upon request, private backyard

Location: Finsbury Park

Designer Flat

For the solo traveler or couple, this small but charming apartment is thoughtfully decorated: Mid-century modern furniture, an ornate bedroom tapestry, and sculptural lighting are among the highlights. It’s well-located, too, with Camden Market, the canal, and Primrose Hill all within walking distance (though there are several transport stations nearby as well for convenience).

Amenities: Bathtub, washer/dryer, sound system, balcony

Location: Camden

Stylish Hoxton Loft

Any foodies visiting London will want to book this Airbnb for the kitchen alone: With heaps of counter space, every culinary utensil under the sun, and top-of-the-line appliances, it has everything you need to make the most of your bounty from the farmers’ market. The rest of the space is equally homey, with a cocoon-like bedroom and a low-lit living area.

Rooms: 1 bedroom, 1.5 bathrooms

Amenities: Bathtub, washer

Location: Hoxton

Darnley Road

Once a button factory, this now-airy two-bedroom serves as a spacious base from which to explore East London. The converted space has high ceilings and oversized windows to let in what precious sunlight may occur during your stay—and there’s even an outdoor space, complete with a little breakfast bistro set, for the warmer months.

Amenities: Bathtub, washer/dryer, workout equipment, elevator, outdoor dining area

Stunning Hyde Park House

This Georgian townhouse rental sits within walking distance of Hyde Park and takes up all four levels of the home. The gourmet kitchen and pantry is well-suited for whipping up extravagant breakfasts, while the main bathroom’s soaking tub will feel like the ultimate treat after a day of touring about town. If you’re bringing little ones along, there’s a playroom (which can also be converted into a yoga space.) Also: relish the roof terrace!

Rooms: 3 bedrooms, 4 bathrooms

Amenities: Washer/dryer, central heating, private terrace.

Location: Hyde Park

Period Property

Who needs minimalism when you can stay in this vibrantly decorated townhouse? The three-bedroom property is conveniently located near Covent Garden’s highlights (shops, restaurants, cafés) while inside, details like a vaulted living room, wood-paneled walls, and a standalone tub impart a supremely serene atmosphere.

Rooms: 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms

Amenities: Washer, AC, heating, dishwasher, Nespresso.

Location: Covent Garden

Notting Hill Mews House

Staying in this Notting Hill mews house will feel like a storybook getaway—it’s nestled just off of Portobello Road. There are four spacious bedrooms, making it a lovely pick for family or group getaways, while common spaces feature loads of natural light and plenty of nooks for entertaining. A design highlight: the marine wallpaper in the upstairs bathroom.

Rooms: 4 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms

Amenities: Washer/dryer, central heating, shared backyard.

Location: Notting Hill

Mid-Century Mews House

Mid-century modern lovers assemble: this 1964-era home is an architectural gem. Original ‘70s cladded ceilings add to the character of the property, while details like Italian marble in the bathrooms and a furnished terrace make each space stylish and distinct. The rental is located near Camden Town, Primrose Hill, and Regent’s Park, with easy access to many of London’s attractions.

Amenities: Bathtub, central heating, private terrace with furniture.

Location: Central London

Henry Townhouse

Brace yourselves: this is the former home of Jane Austen’s most beloved brother, Henry. The historic Georgian townhouse in Marylebone comes with six pristinely designed bedrooms, and is peppered with all sorts of antiques and artworks curated by Russell Sage Studio. This rental is staffed, meaning you’ll enjoy a daily breakfast menu, as well as a manager and housekeeping team taking care of your group throughout the stay. For quintessential Regency Era charm, you can’t do better than this.

Amenities: Washer/dryer, central AC and heating, property manager, breakfast, projector and screen, balcony, yoga studio, massage table.

Location: Marylebone

Hackney Warehouse

Position yourself in the heart of Hackney (between London Fields and Victoria Park) at this stylish apartment that’s full of personality and natural light. The living room and dining area are major highlights of the home, ideal for reading the morning paper or cozying up with a bottle of wine. Extra points for the spacious bathroom with its tiled tub!

Amenities: Bathtub, washer, central heating, elevator, dedicated workspace.

Art-Inspired Home

Artful details abound in this spacious home situated on a quiet residential road. There’s the original fireplace tucked within the living room, an elegant freestanding wooden bathtub, and book-lined shelves that create a lived-in atmosphere. Top that off with the lush backyard garden equipped with a BBQ and your perfect London getaway is squared away.

Rooms: 3 bedrooms, 3.5 bathrooms

Amenities: Bathtub, washer/dryer, dishwasher, backyard, BBQ.

Location: Shepherd’s Bush

Spacious Flat

This renovated flat in East London offers plenty of room to stretch out across its 1200 square-feet floorplan. The living room’s plush sofas are surrounded by personal touches like artwork and a well-stocked bookshelf, and the massive kitchen with adjacent windows deliver a relaxing ambience for preparing meals and drinks.

Amenities: Bathtub, washer/dryer, record player, kitchen.

Birch House

Those in favor of indoor-outdoor living will especially love this home on the peaceful outskirts of town. Wood-paneling sets a natural tone for the space, and large doors and windows open out onto a backyard Eden. After a busy day of sightseeing, nestle in for the night with the at-home cinema (popcorn machine included).

Rooms: 2 bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms

Amenities: Bathtub, washer, movie theater, dishwasher, hammock.

Location: West Norwood

The Floating Terrarium

All aboard this darling house boat on the canal. This unique accommodation is designed to keep guests comfortable—a mud room, large tiled shower, king-size bedroom, and open plan living space are standouts. (And that’s not even mentioning the dozens of plants!) But perhaps the best detail is the back deck, where you can sunbathe with a unforgettable view.

Amenities: Canal view, hot water, shampoo/conditioner, board games, heating, Wi-Fi, kitchen, outdoor furniture, smart lock.

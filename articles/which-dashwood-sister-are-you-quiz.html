Which Dashwood Sister Are You?

Which of “Sense and Sensibility”’s Dashwood sisters has your heart? Here, a short personality quiz to determine where you’d slot in at Barton Cottage.

By Devon

September 24, 2026

Jane Austen had a special knack for writing about sisters, portraying all the affection, frustration, competition, and alienation that can exist among daughters with great sensitivity—and no small amount of humor—throughout her fiction.

Indeed, Austen’s first published novel, Sense and Sensibility, is as much concerned with whom the elder Dashwood sisters, Elinor and Marianne, ultimately marry as it is with how their dramatically different personalities manifest themselves (one has the sense, the other the sensibility) and shape their relationship. As Daisy Edgar-Jones, who plays Elinor in Georgia Oakley’s forthcoming adaptation of the book, put it to British Devon: “This really feels like a love story between sisters.”

But which Dashwood speaks most directly to you? Here, a short personality quiz to determine where you’d slot in at Barton Cottage.

A) Responsible, level-headed, maybe a bit aloof

B) Romantic, passionate, dramatic

C) Good-humored, if a little nosy

A) At a museum or gallery

B) At a concert

C) Out of the house, ideally somewhere with good people-watching

A) Integrity

B) Tastes and opinions that match your own

C) Gallantry

A) With politeness and restraint

B) Directly (and a little aggressively)

C) By having other people handle it

A) The eldest

B) The middle child

C) The baby

Results:

Elinor, the eldest Dashwood daughter, is characterized by “a strength of understanding, and coolness of judgment, which qualified her…to be the counsellor of her mother.” Her disposition is described as “affectionate” and her feelings “strong,” but, crucially, she knows “how to govern them.”

Marianne, the middle daughter, is described as “sensible and clever; but eager in every thing: her sorrows, her joys, could have no moderation.” She’s also “generous, amiable, interesting”…“every thing but prudent.”

The youngest daughter, who is “good-humored” and “well disposed” but, at 13, more of an observer than a main character…for now!

I Combed Through Pages of Prime Day Clothing Deals—Here’s What Stood Out

Prime Day returns on October 6 and 7—shop the best Prime Day clothing deals 2026, where members can shop discounts across clothing, shoes, and accessories.

By Kristina Rutkowski

October 6, 2026

Prime Big Day Deals 2026 is back—which means starting today, Tuesday, October 6, Amazon Prime members can start shopping the amazing clothing and accessory deals site-wide. Many of these pieces only see markdowns during this sale, so now’s the perfect moment to snag your favorites before they’re gone! After all, this year’s event is only two days long—ending Wednesday, October 7—so if something catches your eye now, don’t wait too long to add it to your cart.

Devon’s Guide to the Best Prime Day Clothing Deals 2026:

Since I’ve been covering Prime Day clothing deals for Devon for a few years now, let me guide you through my process. My shopping masterplan always starts with wardrobe essentials: think the perfect pair of relaxed Levi’s jeans and a stack of fresh T-shirts (I’ve been wearing this vintage-inspired one from Gap for years). Then, it’s onto fall fashion needs. With the temperatures finally starting to shift, it’s the perfect time to refresh my seasonal rotation. From transitional layers and cozy knits to some really good footwear—including Staud’s Wally boots and my favorite Mary Jane flats from Madewell—these are the kinds of staples worth investing in, especially while marked down for a limited time.

Discover all my picks and more Devon editor-approved finds with our complete guide to the best sales this Amazon Prime Day 2026—let’s get shopping!

Fall Clothing Deals

Leset’s perfect black cardigan is high on my list, and I always recommend snagging the Baggy jeans from Levi’s, if they see a markdown—lucky for us all, they do. Ulla Johnson’s floral blouse is the perfect transitional piece to carry you into the cooler months—simply layer it under a suede jacket or tailored blazer on chillier days—while Vince’s burgundy pullover is the easiest way to embrace autumn’s color palette. Each of these can be mixed and matched with other fall classics—such as corduroy pants, tailored trousers, or a trench coat—for endless wearability all season long.

Designer Shoe Deals

Sandal season is over and Prime Big Day Deals is here to help restock your shoe closet with all the fall necessities. From Staud’s iconic Wally ankle boots—the IYKYK editor pick—to brown suede loafers from Free People and my favorite Mary Jane flats from Madewell, there’s no shortage of stylish staples worth adding to your rotation. I can’t resist a good sneaker deal, so I’m snagging Puma’s Speedcat trainers while they’re on sale. Whether worn with relaxed denim, slip skirts, or a fall dress, these are the versatile shoes I’ll be reaching for all season long—and, in my book, some of the best footwear deals worth shopping currently.

Wardrobe Essentials Deals

A change in season means it’s time to refresh my go-to basics. A fresh stack of T-shirts is always in order—I’ve been wearing this vintage-inspired take from Gap for years. It’s so soft and lightweight, and layers perfectly under jackets (hello, Levi’s quintessential trucker shape), button-down shirts, and knitwear. Leset’s black Rio pants are a wardrobe workhorse, perfectly cropped and flared to show off your favorite boots or loafers. And rather than your typical sweatshirt, try Donni’s polo version—made from the same soft, cozy fabric, but with a polished collar that gives the everyday staple a little something extra. Together, these easy building blocks make getting dressed for fall effortless.

Original Frescoes, Colorful Tiles, and Quaint Balconies: These Are the Best Airbnbs in Lisbon

With quaint balconies overlooking the sea and skyline, the best Airbnbs in Lisbon capture the magic of the city—book one before your next trip.

By Nicole Kliest

October 14, 2026

I didn’t register just how much I loved Lisbon until I was leaving it. Waiting for a taxi to take me to the airport, I paused to watch the early morning unfold. An empty ochre-colored trolley winding around the corner; church bells chiming in the distance; an elderly man strolling uphill, unhurried, newspaper tucked beneath the arm. Lisbon is one of those frozen-in-time destinations that convinces you you’re doing it all wrong. With its Mediterranean climate, relaxed pace, and incredible architecture, it’s quite easy to be swept up in the coastal capital’s romance.

Devon ’s Favorite Airbnbs in Lisbon:

  • For the City’s Most Beautiful Kitchen: Azulejos Dreaming , from $189 per night
  • For Frescoed Charm: O Jardim Apartment , from $326 per night
  • For a Shaded Terrace: Slice of Alfama , from $183 per night
  • For a Balcony on the Sea: Incredible View , from $606 per night
  • For Historic Original Details: Under a Wooden Sky , from $342 per night

Lisbon stretches across seven hills and is made up of several neighborhoods—Baixa, Chiado, and Alfama being some of its most popular. The city’s charm blossoms at every corner, from sidewalks lined in azulejos (hand-painted ceramic tiles), to miraduoros, the Portuguese name for elevated terraces offering sweeping views across red-tiled roofs and the Tagus River. The best Airbnbs in Lisbon situate travelers in dynamic neighborhoods that showcase all of these great traits.

For history buffs, take note of the 18th-century Pombaline homes. Travelers hoping to gain a few new freckles: Please observe the rentals with private gardens and pools to make the most of Lisbon’s famous sunshine. And design lovers will surely fall in love with original details like wooden ceiling beams and original frescoes. All of this, and more, to be discovered in the 15 superb Lisbon Airbnbs below.

Authentic Terrace Apartment

This 18th-century Pombaline home sits within Alfama—Lisbon’s historical heart—and features exquisite design details like azulejos tiles. Meals can be enjoyed at the elegant dining table, which is surrounded by mural paintings and Belgian 1930s De Coen furniture. The bedrooms feature Made in Portugal cotton sheets, and Dona Manta woolen blankets bought at Paris em Lisbon in the Chiado neighborhood. And don’t miss the massive terrace—a perfect spot for a quiet morning.

Rooms: 4 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms

Amenities: Bathtub, washer/dryer, balcony, outdoor dining area, sun loungers, breakfast.

Location: Alfama

Oxalá Chiado

For a sharp turn in a contemporary direction, book this flat. Family-owned and in the center of Lisbon, the two-floor apartment is within walking distance from all the city’s main attractions. Natural wood textures and soft pops of color create a soothing contrast to the home’s white walls, while a spacious dining room make it exceptionally easy to unwind after a day of sightseeing if you’d rather stay in.

Rooms: 2 bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms

Amenities: Bathtub, washer/dryer, AC, heating, dishwasher.

Location: Chiado

Azulejos Dreaming

Perfectly positioned in the quiet, historic district of central Lisbon, this stylish apartment is ideal for couples and solo travelers. When you’re not taking advantage of the proximity to local tascas and prime sunset viewing points, the note-perfect interiors—from azulejos tiles to wooden ceiling beams to copper pots hanging in the kitchen—will make curling up at ‘home’ all the more special.

Rooms: 2 bedrooms, 1 bathroom

Amenities: Washer, AC, indoor fireplace.

Location: Santa Catarina

Incredible View

As the name suggests, this luxury penthouse has the best views of the Tejo River and surrounding Alfama rooftops (and, equally, all the colorful tilework that dots the city). When you’re not soaking in the sun from the panoramic terraces—whether over breakfast or a sunset cocktail—settle in for a good night’s rest on the cushy memory foam mattress.

Rooms: 2 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms

Amenities: Washer, AC, heating, balcony with furniture, elevator.

Historical Cottage

You’ll feel like a Portuguese aristocrat staying in this historical luxury home with its tall ceilings and elegant furnishings. With an expansive private outdoor area and views of the Tagus River, guests can soak in Lisbon’s famous sunshine throughout the day. (If you’re someone who prefers to have ample outside space, this is the rental for you.) And when you’re ready to hit the town, take note that the Estoril Coast and its beaches are just a short drive away.

Rooms: 1 bedroom, 2.5 bathrooms

Amenities: Bathtub, AC, backyard, outdoor dining area, free parking.

Location: Alto de Santo Amaro

Luxurious Apartment

The private heated swimming pool (a rarity, in the middle of the city) is the star selling point for this luxury apartment situated between Alfama and Graça. But that’s not everything—there’s also a picturesque garden (complete with outdoor furniture for alfresco meals), air conditioning in all the rooms, and an open kitchen designed with quintessential Lisbon decor that’ll make you feel right at home.

Amenities: Washer/dryer, central AC, heating, backyard, private heated pool, outdoor dining area, beach essentials.

Location: Mouraria

Architect-Designed Loft

With its abundance of natural light and hilltop location, this historic architect-designed loft makes an excellent home base for a visit to Lisbon. The home is close to several points of interest, including the romantic Jardim do Príncipe Real, the iconic observation point of Miradouro de São Pedro de Alcântara, plus several authentic restaurants and shops.

Amenities: Bathtub, washer/dryer, AC, heating, free parking, elevator, EV charger.

Location: Príncipe Real

São Paulo Street

Tucked inside a Pombaline building next to Timeout Market and São Paulo Church, this well-decorated apartment is sleek and stylish. The owners make good use of the home’s high ceilings and natural light, with the living room flowing naturally into the dining area and kitchen.

Rooms: 1 bedroom, 1 bathroom

Amenities: Washer, pack n’ play and crib, AC, heating, kitchen, dishwasher, paid parking on premises.

Location: Cais do Sodré

Old Casa Pessoa

From the sun-dappled living room to the bedroom with its windows flung open, everything about this ethereal apartment feels in line with a perfect Lisbon getaway. There’s a balcony just off the desk area for afternoon reads, and when you’re ready to venture out, the home is just steps from cafes, shops, and the Tagus river.

Amenities: Washer/dryer, kitchen, elevator.

Location: Baixa

O Jardim Apartment

This light and airy apartment was designed by architect José Adrião Arquitetos and is within a building dating back to the 19th century. In addition to the lovely living spaces and bedroom, there are also balconies for those fragrant wisps of fresh afternoon air.

Amenities: Washer/dryer, portable fans, kitchen, elevator.

Amazing Terraces

This gorgeous apartment in the historic neighborhood of Alfama has not one, but two private terraces for guests where you can look out over the top of Panteao Church while sipping on an espresso or spritz. Inside, a spacious kitchen offers an elegant setting for whipping up afternoon snacks, and a dedicated workspace renders this the ideal setting for remote work. (Did we mention there’s also an outdoor shower?)

Amenities: Washer/dryer, crib and pack n’ play, AC, heating, kitchen, dishwasher, balcony with furniture.

Views of Graça

Remember those seven rolling hills? This newly renovated apartment in Graça sits atop one of them, offering incredible panoramic views. There’s a cozy bedroom and fully-equipped kitchen, but the star of the show is the private terrace that looks as though it’s suspended above the city’s iconic red rooftops.

Amenities: Washer, AC, heating, kitchen, balcony.

Location: Graça

Historical Center With Garden

This charming apartment invites you to step back in time to medieval Alfama, where you’ll be within walking distance to sightseeing spots such as Largo das Portas do Sol, Cathedral Sé, and the Pantheon. The apartment also has a terrace garden that’s furnished with a dining table and sun loungers.

Amenities: Bathtub, AC, heating, kitchen, balcony with furniture.

Private Slice of Alfama

This Alfama apartment epitomizes the historic neighborhood’s signature charm. It’s accessed via an alley just beside Igreja de São Miguele and is surrounded by narrow winding streets full of taverns, shops, and eateries. The home is designed with a central private patio, which is accessed by four separate doors, lending an indoor-outdoor flow. Our favorite detail? The large bathtub with views of the bougainvilleas blooming outside.

Rooms: 1 bedroom, 1.5 bathrooms

Amenities: Bathtub, washer, crib, portable fans and heater, kitchen, balcony with furniture.

Under a Wooden Sky

A striking interpretation of the Pombalino style, this apartment in Lisbon’s downtown perches on the top floor of an 18th-century building. The renovation of the space was led by design studio CASCA and original details like the high wood ceilings, tiling, and limestone stucco walls give the space a distinguished touch (as do the furniture pieces, which were exclusively crafted for the home).

Amenities: Washer, AC, heating, kitchen.

Location: Baixa-Chiado

Brown Sneakers Are Here to Stay—Shop Our Favorite Pairs

Shop the best brown sneakers in various shades of chocolate, espresso, and mousse, including Nike’s Moon shoes and the Japan from Adidas.

By Kristina Rutkowski

October 21, 2026

Among the many sneaker trends flooding our feeds these days, brown sneakers are arguably one of the most wearable. Shades of brown have found new life; the once-deemed-drab hue has quickly become a staple. Whether you’re team mocha mousse or favor a rich espresso, you’re in luck—the best brown sneakers currently incorporate every shade within the spectrum.

Devon ’s List of Best Brown Sneakers:

With retro sneakers still very much in high demand, you’ll see plenty of throwback kicks and vintage-inspired silhouettes within our edit of favorite brown sneakers. Sumptuous suede fabrications also remain a top contender on our wish list—they look oh-so-good in a range of taupes, cocoa, and latte tones—while smooth leather finishes (think: the Japan from Adidas for starters) and sporty nylon materials (namely Miu Miu’s Plume sneakers), also make for exciting highlights.

Over the last few years, A-listers have embraced the brown sneaker trend. Earlier this summer, Rihanna stepped out in a light brown pair of Nike x Jacquemus Moon shoes, styling them with a khaki trench coat, baggy blue jeans, and a ball cap. Meanwhile, Simone Ashley gave her Puma’s Speedcats in caffè—a favorite style of Emily Ratajkowski—a feminine twist, pairing them with a butter yellow sundress.

In September 2025, Selena Gomez sported a Steve Madden pair, while Gigi Hadid stepped out that same month in New York in a suede, russet-toned style from Larroudé. Styled with a matching knit set and ribbed white tank top, the supermodel demonstrated the minimalist way of wearing them. And in 2024, Dakota Johnson mixed her beloved chocolate brown Nike Cortez sneakers with a quilted barn jacket and white jeans for a transitional look that still feels relevant today—proving that brown sneakers really are the off-duty finishing touch of choice.

However you decide to wear them, the best brown sneakers will complement every fall look—find your new go-to pair, below.

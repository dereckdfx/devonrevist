Celine Dion Is Turning the Streets of Paris Into Her Runway

Amidst her highly-anticipated Paris residency, the superstar has been exiting her hotel in fresh-off-the-runway looks.

By Christian Allaire

October 1, 2026

Celine Dion fans: Assemble!

Last month, the global superstar kicked off her brand new Paris residency, where she has been playing a slew of live shows at the Plenitude Arena in Nanterre, France (through May 2027). It’s an extremely welcomed return to the stage for the singer, who, behind the scenes, has been battling a rare neurological disorder for the past few years. But now, Dion is ready to grace the stage with her presence once again—not to mention deliver us those powerhouse vocals.

While her stage looks for the performances have been a main draw for the show—see: the voluminous black Dior gown she wore for a recent performance below—she is also serving up high-fashion looks off the stage. All month long, the star has been spotting exiting her hotel in the City of Light, and each time she has treated the streets of Paris as her own personal runway.

It all started in early September when Dion emerged from Le Royal Monceau hotel in a black leather Loewe gown from its fall 2026 collection. Fresh off the runway! It featured a plushy shearling trim and high neckline, and the look was completed with one of the brand’s signature luxe leather bags. She then sported another sleek and edgy look, courtesy of Tom Ford by Haider Ackermann. She wore its boxy, croc-embossed leather jacket, and paired it with Bermuda-style shorts.

Since then, the hits have kept on coming. There has been a chic Alaïa design, consisting of classic trench coat featured draping and pleating in all the right places. Or a structured Givenchy satin blazer, paired with an effortless pair of jeans. There’s simply no one doing it like her.

The question is: Are these polished, yet modern looks a sneak peek of what we can expect from the rest of her Paris performances? We sure hope so! They are likely the brainchild of her longtime stylist and creative director Annie Horth, though Dion has also worked with Zendaya’s stylist Law Roach in the past. Whoever is responsible, one thing is for sure: These stylish sightings have us even more excited to make her grand return.

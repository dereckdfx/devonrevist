These Travel Outfits May Just Inspire You to Book Your Next Vacation

Going somewhere? These travel outfits will make you feel put together before you even reach your destination.

By Elly Leavitt

September 24, 2026

There was a time when going to the airport called for dressing up—an era of pillbox hats and glamorous coats, and also a time of cigarette smoke-filled cabins, so we’ll take our wins where we can. But when it comes to today’s travel outfits, suffice it to say the reality of dressing for a flight, no matter how short or long, is typically uninspiring. It’s time to change that: While you’re planning your vacation wardrobe, picking out something to wear to feel put together on the trip itself can make long travel days a little more special. (And, with the right knitwear, a lot more cozy.)

Devon ’s Travel Outfit Essentials:

From airport outfits to road-trip-ready loungewear and lightweight pieces best for layering, the right ensemble mostly depends on your jet-setting plans. If you need everything to squeeze into a carry-on, opt for versatile basics you can mix and match with the rest of your holiday styles. Try wide-leg pants you can pair with a tiny tank top for an apéro outdoors or oversized totes that work just as well as a personal item for the plane as they do strolling the vintage markets in the South of France. If you’re headed somewhere far, far away and staring down a 15-hour travel day, comfort is key. Invest in some cozy loungewear and supportive travel shoes for best results. Need something to see you through a weekend getaway? Your travel outfit should double as a real outfit—no need to waste valuable time changing at your hotel.

Ahead, seven travel outfit ideas to cover every possible itinerary.

The City Slicker

Prep for a week in a glittering metropolis with a compact carry-on (cobblestones and public transit are not friends of the checked bag) and a pared-back travel outfit to match. A sleek uniform of black basics—which will be easy to mix and match within your capsule wardrobe—make for a good base. To save space we recommend wearing your bulkiest outerwear on your flight. It’s trench coat season, after all.

The Autumn Layers

If your travel plans come with a touch more glamour—say, a two-hour train ride versus a red-eye flight—you can afford to dress up a bit. Lean into the palette and fabrics of the season: leather boots, suede jackets, burgundy accessories all set the scene for a fall adventure nicely. You’ll be ready to drop your bag at the hotel and head right out as soon as you arrive.

For bleary-eyed, early-morning check-ins when putting together a stylish ensemble seems like a Herculean task, co-ords are your friend. The Athleisure Set

If all you care about is being as comfortable as possible, activewear is the way to go. You likely already have a simple white tee and ultra-baggy sweatshirt in your closet, but just in case, basics from Banana Republic and Nike are going to last you for many travel seasons to come. For a final layer of defense against bright cabin lights, don’t forget an eye mask—this 22-momme silk one is a little extravagance that’s worth it.

The Road Tripper

Long stretches of hours on the road call for something relaxed and something cashmere. These pants from Almina Concept—100% wool, and far more polished than their sneaky pull-on waistband would suggest—are a comfortable base, to be layered with easy long-sleeves and some luxe socks. Choose an extra-large tote bag to house your road trip essentials in style. If you’re hitting the highway for a weekend getaway, Kallmeyer’s is the perfect size for a 48-hour trip.

The Layover-to-Lunch

The key to looking fresh upon arrival lies in sartorial savvy; that is, choosing an outfit that’s optimally designed for a day of sightseeing and lazing on sunny terraces but is composed of surprisingly comfortable staples. For example: a simple crewneck tee and easily packable loafers you can wear both on the trip and out to lunch when you arrive; a leather passport holder that functions as a makeshift wallet; a pair of mustard yellow trousers with an adjustable waist for hours in the plane and pairing with a fun top for dinners out.

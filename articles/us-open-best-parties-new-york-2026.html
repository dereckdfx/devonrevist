Courtside to Cocktail Hour: Inside the Best and Buzziest US Open Parties

Ahead of the official start of the US Open, a flurry of fetes took place across New York City. From Coco Gauff and Aryna Sabalenka to Naomi Osaka and Novak Djokovic, the biggest stars in tennis traded their on-court kits for some highly fashionable attire. Here’s a look inside the most exclusive parties of the moment.

By Paul Chi

August 31, 2026

Game, set, party! Ahead of the official start of the US Open, a flurry of fetes took place across New York City. From Coco Gauff and Aryna Sabalenka to Naomi Osaka and Novak Djokovic, the biggest stars in tennis traded their on-court kits for some highly fashionable attire. Here’s a look inside the most exclusive parties of the moment.

For one night only, Club Lacoste transformed The Plaza into an active tennis court. On Thursday, US Open winners Novak Djokovic and Daniil Medvedev played an exhibition match inside the historic Edwardian Room. The grand Spanish Renaissance style space was awash in Lacoste green with a custom built court—showcasing Club Lacoste’s vision of placing tennis courts in unexpected places. “I’ve been on courts around the world, but nothing cool like this,” Medvedev told Devon. “This is my first time inside [The Plaza]. I’ve seen Home Alone 2—maybe five times—and I remember all the scenes at the hotel. I’m happy to be here!” Before the pros took to the court, guests indulged in Lacoste’s signature cocktail “Le Chose,” created by founder René Lacoste in 1967, and took selfies with Djokovic, the brand’s longtime ambassador who exemplifies sophisticated style on-and-off the court. “I’m very self critical about the way I dress. I always look for counsel from my wife,” he revealed to Devon. “I’ve been getting out of my comfort zone and trying to be more bold and embrace baggy clothes. But, I’m finding my own authentic look, particularly with what Lacoste is doing in connecting their heritage style with streetwear.”

On Wednesday evening, newly-minted Cincinnati Open champion Coco Gauff was the guest of honor at a dinner party at Casa Tua, celebrating her partnership with Chase Sapphire Reserve. The tennis phenom stepped out in a Miu Miu dress and sky-high stilettos for the special occasion. “I haven’t worn heels in a while!” Gauff laughed to Devon. “I’m still getting used to them.” Andrew Steinthal from JPMorganChase welcomed Gauff and VIPs, including Lindsey Vonn and Gayle King, for a multi-course meal featuring cacio e pepe and beef tenderloin. A playlist inspired by Gauff’s favorite artists—Doechii and Tyler the Creator a’plenty—provided a peppy vibe while vintage tennis rackets and dahlias and roses filled the elegant space. “I’m super excited for the Open this year,” Gauff said. “Cincinnati was a good win for me, so it’s cool to have momentum going into the US Open. I really love playing in New York.” To help her relax ahead of big tournaments, Gauff revealed that she’s all about Korean BBQ feasts and getting lost in a good film. “I’m a theatergoer. I’ll go to movies by myself and I’m into Letterboxd. Interstellar is one of my favorites,” she shared. “I like Marvel and anime. Anything fiction, I’m there!”

Aryna Sabalenka, the 2024 and reigning US Open female champion, shined bright at the Material Good soirée where she feted her latest jewelry collaboration. “This is the most glamorous New York party. Everyone here is so stylish. I’m obsessed!” Sabalenka, clad in a semi-sheer dress by Helsa, gushed to Devon while at the brand’s SoHo flagship on Tuesday. Over Honey Deuces served from a canopy-covered Barolo bar cart that matched the orange anthuriums decorating the store, well-heeled attendees got an up-close look at the necklace, bracelet and earrings that Sabalenka will wear at the Open. The collective set has more than 127 carats and seven colored gemstones in a variety of shapes. “I love fashion, and jewelry is the most important,” she said. “I love the shine and I feel beautiful and confident. That’s what all girls want to feel, right?” The Belarusian star discovered Material Good when she walked through the boutique as a customer two years ago. “Shopping is my weakness,” she laughed, “I do it nonstop.” Since then, the jeweler has accessorized her for all Grand Slams this year. The party capped off with Sabalenka participating in a thrilling ping-pong match against five challengers.

The Baccarat Hotel New York presented its annual Petit Slam table tennis exhibition on Thursday where two-time US Open winner Naomi Osaka faced off against media maven Gayle King. The bash, now in its fourth year, unfolded inside the Grand Salon and offered attendees snacks from Michelin-starred Chef Gabriel Kreuther and flutes of Moët & Chandon Champagne as well as Honey Deuces. Spectators gathered around Baccarat’s bespoke table tennis tabletop complete with a crystal net to watch Osaka and King compete in a spirited match. Underneath a sparkling 64-arm crystal chandelier, the two tallied long rallies with Osaka ultimately taking the win. The Japanese superstar is also serving winning fashion fits ahead of her first-round US Open match on Monday. “I’ve been inspired by Anna Kournikova. There’s one image of her arriving at the US Open in heels, and I wanted to do my own spin on that,” she told Devon. For Osaka’s on-court look, “Nike is doing a tribute to basketball, so I’m doing something with that idea. It’s going to be very New York,” she said with a coy smile.

Tucked along Greenpoint’s scenic waterfront, Taylor Fritz, the highest-ranked American men’s player, hosted an intimate cocktail get-together with Dobel Tequila at Padel Haus on Monday. The tennis ace stepped behind the club’s plush bar to serve his signature drink, Fritzy Spicy Margarita, and welcomed invitees with a rousing toast. “The US Open is my favorite Slam. The energy of playing a night match is incredible,” he told Devon. “As an American, the crowd gets behind you and there’s no other feeling like it. It’s really a dream come true to get to play at the Open.” After mingling and enjoying his namesake margarita, Fritz joined revelers on Padel Haus’ state-of-the-art indoor courts for a round of paddle ball to demonstrate some of his best serves beyond the bar. As for having his own handcrafted beverage available for fans to enjoy at the US Open, “I take a lot of pride in it,” he said with a cheeky grin. “It’s cool!”

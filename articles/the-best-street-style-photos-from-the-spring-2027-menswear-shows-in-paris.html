The Best Street Style Photos From the Spring 2027 Menswear Shows in Paris

Phil Oh shoots the best-dressed guests at the spring 2027 menswear shows in Paris.

By Phil Oh

June 23, 2026

Paris is blazing hot, but fashion waits for no heat wave. As temperatures climb over 100 degrees, the spring 2027 menswear shows are now underway, with some brands shifting to earlier start times in an effort to keep guests at least somewhat shielded from the scorching sun.

And if anyone thought the weather might inspire a more pared-back approach to dressing, the Paris street-style set is quickly proving otherwise. Tailoring remains firmly on the agenda, with a surprising number of showgoers stepping out in blazers—and, in some cases, full suiting. Elsewhere, practicality met polish: Alaïa’s cult fishnet sandals are emerging as a favorite for staying cool, while crisp button-downs and breezy linen separates offer a lesson in heatwave dressing.

Follow along as Phil Oh captures the season’s most stylish showgoers outside the spring 2027 menswear collections in Paris.

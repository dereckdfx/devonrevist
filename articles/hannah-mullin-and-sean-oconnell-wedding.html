A Classic Rhode Island Wedding With Tennis-Inspired Details and a Performance by Norah Jones

Hannah Mullin and Sean O'Connell's Newport wedding weekend featured intricate, custom décor and a first dance to “Come Away With Me” performed by Norah Jones.

By Shelby Wax

October 16, 2026

Hannah Mullin and Sean McConnell are true childhood sweethearts. Their relationship started when they were two kids growing up together in Delaware. “We became best friends, and it flourished into a meaningful love story that has been continuing for nine years,” says Hannah. On June 3, 2024, Sean planned to bring their journey into the next stage with a proposal at Rock House in Turks and Caicos. “He had flowers imported and lights set up on the dock that went out onto the water…it truly looked so magical,” remembers Hannah. “Right after he proposed, a storm rolled in, and the staff set us up with a private dinner in this sheltered cove.” She adds, “It was one of the most memorable nights of our lives.”

The couple decided to say “I do” almost two years later at the Castle Hill Inn in Newport, Rhode Island. “From the very beginning, we dreamed beyond a traditional wedding,” the bride says of her wedding vision. “Our relationship is full of love, laughter, and color. We wanted it to be a true reflection of us.” Partnering with wedding planner Sarah Heaney of Leila James Events proved to be the best decision of the entire process. “We both waver on the type B end, so our first task was finding a planner,” says Hannah. “Whenever people asked how it was going, they almost always expected the same answer. That we were overwhelmed, counting down the days, or simply ready for it to be over. But for us, it was exactly the opposite. Every meeting, every design decision, every moment spent dreaming about what this celebration could become was something we genuinely looked forward to.”

The celebration would span two days of events, beginning with a rehearsal dinner and welcome party at the Tennis Hall of Fame complete with an entire custom merch suite and tennis-inspired details. “Me and Sarah always laugh with one another when we look back and realize this all came into place from only three to four inspiration pictures from me,” the bride says. Design was an important factor in planning the weekend, with blooming florals from Stoneblossom, custom illustrations by Champagne and Ink, and transformative décor crafted by Ryan Designs.

Danielle and Ellen from Champagne and Ink captured us in the most personal way imaginable. Every custom illustration and design elements felt uniquely ours. Our invitations were a combination of the playful color story and the introduction of pattern and shape. They infused so much personality into every piece, creating details that guests will remember for years to come. — Hannah

When it came to my rehearsal dinner gown, I knew I wanted to incorporate color. Wearing all white during the weekend did not feel true to me, so I searched for weeks for the perfect gown to be worn at the Tennis Hall of Fame. I ended up on a stunning Monique Lhuillier gown that felt perfect for summer, and replicated the colors of our venue and florals. I felt one with the night. — Hannah

Sean wanted something unique and custom for the night before. He wanted to also incorporate the colors of the night and went with a baby blue custom Brioni suit from Italy. He picked out each individual detail, even down to his socks! He looked perfectly Newport chic. — Hannah

Upon arrival at our welcome party at the Tennis Hall of Fame our guests were greeted with our custom-built “Winston’s Merch Store,” named after our corgi Winston, where they were able to “shop” our store. We had an array of merch items ranging from matchbooks and custom-printed scarves to crewnecks and hats, and even totes for guests to put all their items in. — Hannah

Our vendors, Leila James Events, Champagne and Ink, and Ryan Designs created the perfect way to display our custom merchandise items. — Hannah

Our custom-built large lounge cabana was placed in the center of the court along with the lighting posts that hung over our custom-printed monogram bistro tables and custom-printed hydrangea linens by Inner Child . The court was the perfect inviting spot for socialization and cocktails. — Hannah

We had an intimate dinner blooming with white, green, and blue hydrangeas lining the dining table. We chose a Stradley Davison linen that gave a nod to a tennis racket theme. — Hannah

We used tennis rackets as our place settings to match the surroundings of the Tennis Hall of Fame layered with La Affitto china and glassware.— Hannah

Our guests arrived at the Tennis Hall of Fame and were invited on to the iconic court, where our welcome party kicked off our wedding weekend. — Hannah

This was the start of our welcome night. I remember Sean and I looked at each other right before this photo was taken and shared a moment together. We both said, “I can’t believe it's finally happening!” — Hannah

Our guests had so much fun with the personalized pieces from our custom merch store. Seeing our friends and family wearing the pieces throughout the weekend made it even more special! — Hannah

A signature cocktail, a custom napkin, and a matchbook waiting to be tucked into a pocket to kick off the wedding weekend!

We chose to host our wedding at Castle Hill Inn in Newport, Rhode Island, set on a private peninsula surrounded by sweeping views of Narragansett Bay and the Atlantic. — Hannah

A quiet moment before the celebration began, feeling completely myself thanks to the incredibly talented Madeline Eleanor . She created a hair and makeup look that felt timeless, romantic, and effortlessly refined; enhancing my natural features while still making me feel incredibly myself. Her artistry, calming presence, and attention to every detail made getting ready such a beautiful part of the day. — Hannah

The dress I ended up choosing was actually the first one I tried on, and I knew then that it was going to be the dress I ended up with. I chose Sept by Sarah Seven . I felt my truest self in it, and I knew that was the feeling I wanted when trying on gowns. I wanted to feel and look like Hannah. When my final look was coming together, we ended up adding the same shimmer beading on my bodice to be added to my veil. It completely transformed the final look and had everything tied together perfectly. — Hannah

Surrounded by my favorite girls, soaking in every laugh, story, and little moment before walking down the aisle. The sweetest start to a day filled with so much love and joy. — Hannah

One of the sweetest moments of the morning… my mom and sister by my side, helping me into my gown and sharing in those quiet, emotional moments before everything began. A memory I’ll cherish forever. — Hannah

Admiring the most perfect bouquet that felt so Hannah, made by the most talented Michelle at Stoneblossom florals. — Hannah

My first look with my Dad was one of the most special parts of my morning. I truly tell everyone that will listen how incredible my Dad is, and having that moment with him is a memory I will never forget. — Hannah

The bridesmaids all wore their own version of a butter yellow gown. Each girl looked unique to themselves and was able to show their personality and style, which is exactly what I wanted! — Hannah

My favorite thing about my bridal gown and final look, is how I still felt very much like myself. I wanted to feel like an elevated version of myself and I will forever love this look! — Hannah

For the wedding day, Sean went with a simple Ralph Lauren tux from their Purple Label . We always knew we wanted him in a cream tux jacket, and we are so happy with how it turned out! — Hannah

A quiet moment between Sean and his dad as they finished getting ready together before the ceremony. One of the many candid moments from the day that we loved having captured. — Hannah

Sean, just before the ceremony. A quiet moment as he put on the finishing touches and prepared for the excitement ahead.

Sean and his groomsmen sharing a few laughs before the ceremony. Classic black tie with Sean standing out in a timeless ivory dinner jacket.

There’s something unmistakable about Castle Hill. Where the coastline, sailboats, and sweeping views of Narragansett Bay create a setting that feels effortlessly timeless.

The ceremony was an intimate oasis. The sun was shining, there was a lovely breeze, and even little duckling friends on the sidelines watching us. (They were the true stars of the show). — Hannah

Walking down the aisle felt like I was in a meadow full of the most beautiful flowers I have ever seen brought to life by Michelle, owner of Stoneblossom Florals and her incredible team. It was my first time seeing it all, and it truly took my breath away. — Hannah

Walking down the aisle with my parents was such a special moment for us. Being able to have them both with me is something I will forever cherish, and this was one of my favorite pictures of the weekend. — Sean

This was my favorite memory of the weekend! I was soaking in so many things at once, and I truly felt pure joy in this moment. — Hannah

Pre-ceremony I was extremely nervous! Being the center of attention always made me very anxious, but the second I saw my dad, the nerves immediately left my body and I instantly felt giddy and excited. Once I was with Sean standing there hand-in-hand, I felt calm and centered. It was perfect. — Hannah

Seeing Hannah walk down the aisle is a moment I’ll never forget. Seeing the women I get to call my forever made me feel like time had stopped. She is the most breathtaking bride. — Sean

Michelle is an artist in every sense of the word. She has an incredible gift for creating florals that evoke emotion as much as beauty. — Hannah

Standing up there with one another on the most beautiful day in June was a fairytale. Giggling and looking at one another in the eye as we hear the story of our love was the most special part of the entire day! We were completely surrounded in a floral oasis. — Hannah

The vision for our ceremony was something deeply personal, romantic, and intimate. Together with our planner, Sarah of Leila James Events, we dreamed up a lush, waist-high floral aisle that guided us toward an incredible grounded floral garden, beautifully encapsulating us as we exchanged our vows. — Hannah

We decided to have “Home” by Edward Sharpe and the Magnetic Zeroes as our recessional song. We were full of bliss and joy hearing one of our favorite songs play while getting to see the reactions of all our friends and family after the ceremony. — Hannah

Stephanie , our amazing photographer, was able to create such a fun and easy environment for our photos that felt so natural and easy going. She was able to capture us and our love perfectly without every photo feeling “posed.” — Hannah

Cocktail hour was designed to feel like an immediate burst of color, joy, and vibrant energy—the perfect beginning to our celebration. Our planner thoughtfully considered every detail, layering color, pattern, and texture throughout the custom open-air frame tent to create an atmosphere that felt both elevated and incredibly joyful. — Hannah

Our custom bar was finished with playful flamingo lamps, custom cocktail menus, and personalized cocktail napkins—small but intentional details that brought an extra layer of personality, whimsy, and charm to the space. — Hannah

Sean has always had a love for pretzels, so we knew we wanted to incorporate them into the celebration in a way that felt both personal and playful. Our planning team hand-created custom pretzel trays, which were filled with the most charming hand-rolled pretzels by Chef Alyssa Reagan of Castle Hill Inn. It was one of the many thoughtful culinary moments Chef Alyssa created throughout the weekend. — Hannah

Our stationery team custom illustrated our cocktail trays, transforming them into another beautiful, personalized detail of the celebration. Each tray featured our signature cocktails, served in the most gorgeous light pink Hudson coupes from Casa de Perrin . — Hannah

Cocktail hour was designed to feel effortlessly joyful, vibrant, and full of life. — Hannah

The custom 32-foot bar became a statement of its own, layered in soft blue tones and delicate custom-illustrated botanical patterns by Champagne and Ink. We loved watching guests gather, cocktails in hand, surrounded by a design that felt truly like an expression of myself. — Hannah

Cocktails were flowing, laughter filled the tent, and the celebration was in full swing. A joyful evening of incredible drinks, wonderful company, and pure enjoyment all around!

It was quite the adventure traveling to the lighthouse at Castle Hill Inn, but the pictures turned out stunning and it was totally worth it! — Hannah

Early in the planning process, I shared just three inspiration images with our planner and from there, gave her the creative freedom to dream and design something entirely unique for Sean and I. What she created was beyond anything we could have imagined. — Hannah

Every detail of our tabletops were designed to feel like a beautiful discovery. We had three different tabletop design styles. This look featured layers of vibrant china, delicate glassware and flatware from Casa de Perrin, soft pink candlelight, abundant florals, and bespoke stationery that came together in a joyful mix of color, pattern, and texture. — Hannah

A few of our key creative partners collaborated to dream up an entirely custom bar room within our reception tent. Tucked away from the main celebration, guests stepped inside to order their cocktails, discovering an intimate space with an elevated speakeasy-inspired feel. — Hannah

Our five-tier wedding cake, designed by the incredibly talented bakery Confectionery Designs , was truly a showstopper. Owners Mark and Marie Soliday drew inspiration from the intricate detailing of my wedding gown, translating its texture into layers of beautiful ivory buttercream. Each sugar flower was then individually handcrafted and hand-painted to perfectly complement the florals woven throughout our celebration. — Hannah

We surprised our guests with a performance from the one and only Norah Jones, one of the most magical moments of the entire evening. For Norah Jones’s performance, we created a custom-built circular stage that allowed the music to become part of the evening's atmosphere. Above Norah’s grand piano floated an extraordinary three-tier floral installation, cascading with lush blooms to create a breathtaking focal point that felt almost suspended in midair. — Hannah

Our guests entered the reception in complete awe, filled with excitement as the design unfolded before them and every thoughtfully curated detail was revealed. — Hannah

Norah’s music has been woven into the fabric of my life for as long as I can remember. Some of my earliest memories are of sitting beside my little pink stereo, listening to her albums on repeat as a little girl. As I grew older, her music became the soundtrack to another kind of love. — Hannah

When I met Sean, one of the first things I shared with him was my love for Norah’s music. Her music became the soundtrack to our relationship in the same quiet, beautiful way it had been for my family growing up. — Hannah

It felt as though we were floating on the dance floor, the only two in the whole room, escaping to “Come Away With Me.” — Hannah

She continued the intimate performance with some of her most beloved songs, including “Don’t Know Why,” “Those Sweet Words,” “Sunrise,” and “What Am I to You?”. — Hannah

A special moment celebrating love and friendship all while Norah Jones performed.

An emotional moment during my dad’s speech. He not only got to show off his phenomenal writing skills, but also how much he notices the details of those around him, and how much he loves our love. I don’t think there was a dry eye in the room when he was finished! — Hannah

The kind of joy you can feel through a photograph, so beautifully captured by the incredible Stephanie Vegliante. A room overflowing with laughter, friendship, and the people we love most exactly the way we dreamed our celebration would feel. — Hannah

A special moment with my mother-in-law, who was a part of making so many things happen this weekend. She has such a vision for taste and fashion, and I am so grateful to have had her in my corner during this process. — Hannah

We went outside during sunset to take in our florals one last time. It was hard to take everything in while walking down the aisle with all our guests there, so it was very special to see it together, just us two. We would have stayed out there all night! — Hannah

We loved taking these dynamic photos with Stephanie! We had so much fun running and being silly together, getting to let loose, and starting the night on a fun and lighthearted note. — Hannah

Enjoying the sunset before our reception. Being free and silly with one another. Another one of our favorite parts of the evening, and this photo really highlights that! — Hannah

A toast from the best man—equal parts laughter, memories, and heartfelt words. One of those moments that perfectly captured the friendships and people who have been such an important part of our story.

I am most definitely shocked by something our lovely best man, George, said. He never fails to light up a room and bring constant laughter and joy with him. He really brought the laughs and memories in his speech. — Hannah

A moment of pure celebration: napkins in the air, glasses raised, and a room completely alive with joy. The kind of energy, laughter, and love we hoped would fill every moment of our wedding night. — Hannah

A sweet moment just for us cutting into a cake that was almost too beautiful to touch. Surrounded by flowers, candlelight, and all the thoughtful details of our celebration, this was one of those little moments we’ll always remember. — Hannah

We loved seeing everyone reach for their cameras, preserving the little moments, details, and memories of the evening from their own perspective. — Hannah

First dance with my dad to the song “Landslide” by Fleetwood Mac—one of our favorites. Music is such a huge part of my relationship with my dad. I grew up practicing guitar with him and rocking out to the Beatles, Led Zeppelin, and of course, Norah Jones. — Hannah

The kind of energy you never want to end. Our dance floor was electric with the incredible live performance by The Eleven and everyone we love completely lost in the moment. Pure joy from beginning to end. — Hannah

A sweet ending to the most unforgettable evening. An abundant dessert display from Confectionery Designs layered with colorful macarons, miniature confections, fresh florals, and beautiful details gave our guests one last delicious surprise to discover before dancing the night away. — Hannah

The perfect late-night bite made personal by Champagne and Ink. Custom floral packaging and monogrammed boxes turned classic French fries and sliders into another playful, thoughtful detail—because even the late-night snacks deserved their own design moment. — Hannah

I always knew I wanted a second look for my reception, but I underestimated how hard it would be to change out of my gown so quickly since I was so in love with it! Thankfully, my gown was a two-piece set, and Sarah Seven had the most beautiful mini skirt to pair perfectly with my corset top. I still got to have a second look, while still being able to have part of my gown on me as well. It was the perfect solution! — Hannah

It was only right to call back to my past in music and rock the crowd on the best day of my life. Our band was able to learn a song that me and a friend made together about a decade ago, and we surprised all our guests with it. Jamming out with our incredible band was definitely a highlight of the night for me and all of our guests. — Sean

We ended the best night of our lives surrounded by the most special people to us, singing and dancing. Music is such a huge part of our relationship, and our band completely blew everyone away and even learned our random song requests. We spent weeks curating our playlist to create the perfect vibe for our wedding night, and it did not disappoint! — Hannah

How to Style a Slip Dress for 5 Different Types of Weddings

A bridal stylist shares five ways to wear a slip dress as a wedding guest—whether you're attending a courthouse ceremony or a black-tie soiree.

By Corinne Pierre-Louis

October 7, 2026

The art of dressing well starts with figuring out which pieces in your wardrobe are true staples and working around them. Too often, special occasions can feel like an excuse to buy a new outfit; weddings are the worst offenders. It’s easy to think you need to pick up a dedicated dress for each different invitation. Trust us, though: The staple you actually need to build a versatile formalwear wardrobe around is one of the simplest—a slip dress.

How to Style a Slip Dress for a Wedding, According to Devon

Look for something neutral, in a mid-to-long silhouette (the most useful for the most number of dress codes)—for this exercise, we’re using a floor-skimming gown in a cool charcoal. Cut on the bias, it skims the body rather than clinging to it, and its quiet shade takes on the character of whatever you pair with it. At a daytime—or more casual—celebration, a single statement accessory can be enough. As the formality climbs, additional pieces, like glittering tennis earrings or a silk pashmina, can up the polish. Think of a slip dress as the wedding guest equivalent of great jeans: an investment you’ll reach for again and again.

Ahead, we’ve styled the staple slip for five very different weddings, from a garden soirée to a black-tie fête. Prefer something in a splashier shade? Scroll on for an edit of more wedding guest slip dresses that are just as versatile.

For a Garden Soirée…

Pull color and texture from the garden directly into your accessories. Here, a velvet floral coat from Reformation sets the tone. Khaite’s Valerie low pump in chartreuse brings a dose of sunflower yellow, while COS’s mismatched earrings echo the blooms.

For a Courthouse Reception…

City hall celebrations tend to be small, daytime events, so choose pieces that make a statement without overdoing it—unless otherwise specified, following a cocktail dress code is probably a safe bet. Opt for Staud’s Wally boots, Le Sundial’s tasseled velvet bag, and an H&M wool coat—bold picks in a streamlined black palette that keep your outfit midday-appropriate.

For a Coastal Celebration…

By the water, the goal is to make a floor-length silhouette feel effortless, not overdone. Choose accessories that nod to the setting in playful ways, like a shell-shaped Simkhai clutch. Kitten heel flip-flops incorporate the ultimate summer footwear, and we love these YSL sunglasses for a bit of shade.

For a Black-Tie Affair…

A floor-length silk gown already meets the black-tie brief; all you need are accessories that dial up the glamour. Consider an east-west clutch from Lafayette 148, a silk scarf from Nour Hammour, and tennis earrings by Brilliant Earth. It all combines for an outfit that’s elegant, understated, and infinitely re-wearable.

For a Festive Fête…

When a couple asks guests to bring some personality to the celebration, use this as a green light to have a little fun with color—and if you’re attending a winter wedding, why not lean into the holiday palette? Jewel tones, like burgundy, work particularly well with a gray slip and make an easy starting point (Amina Muaddi’s Holli Sling 70 pump and Liffner’s leather clutch are two top choices). Then, tie it all together with a standout stack, like Spinelli Kilcollin’s silver rings.

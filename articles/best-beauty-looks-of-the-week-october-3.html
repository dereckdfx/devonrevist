Beauty Marks: The Best Beauty Looks of the Week

From Daisy Edgar-Jones’s cool ’90s inspired lip to Paloma Elsesser’s terracotta eye—this week, the best of the beauty world played with all sides of the color wheel.

By Anna Cafolla

October 3, 2026

Welcome back to Beauty Marks: Devon’s weekly edition of the best moments in celebrity beauty, from Devon editors’ IG feeds, and all the glam of the fashion and pop culture landscapes. Each week, we curate the nail art to pin for your next nail appointment, new recruits to ‘Team Bob’, and major red carpet moments from the week’s most glamorous affairs. As always, it’s as much about celebrity beauty as it is about the makeup artists, hairstylists, and nail artists, as well as the creators crafting the trends you’re about to see everywhere.

This week, the best beauty looks played both sides of the color wheel. Look to Paloma Elsesser, for one: warmth came through in burnished browns, terracotta lips, and generous washes of rosy blush, while cooler notes—smoky charcoal liner, silvery lids and icy, almost lavender-toned shadow—offered a sharper counterpoint, care of Daisy Edgar-Jones and Rosalía. The appeal was in the contrast: rich, sun-warmed skin paired with a cool graphic eye, or softly flushed cheeks offset by something more steely around the lashes. Rather than committing to one temperature from forehead to chin, makeup artists made a case for mixing them, proving that warm and cool tones can be even more compelling when they meet somewhere in the middle.

Scroll through the week’s best of celebrity beauty and pop culture’s glam below, and head to the app to vote for your favorite.

Saffron Knight

Fluffy brows, an espresso-lined gloss lip, and angelic lashes by the queen of lit-from-within glam, Lake Sanu .

Taylor Hill

A little bob cut to cultivate a chic grow-out, by Jessica Gillin at Franklins.

Paloma Elsesser

No smoke without fire! A chocolate-y haze on the eye, luminous highlight, and a precise, cool-toned russet lip—by Nadia Tayeh . An equally exacting ponytail by Jennifer Yepez .

Hailey Bieber

Sporting her darkest hair in years (care of Matt Rez ), Hailey Bieber hit up London with a relaxed, lightly tousled look by Irinel de León . Diane Buzzetta leaned into the fall shades with a shadow that picked up her green eyes and a cocoa-colored matte lip.

Jennifer Lopez

Barbie girl glam for J-Lo in Milan: Ernesto Casillas paints a lacquered pink lip, candy cheeks, and doll-like eyes to match. Her hair, by Justine Marjan , was equally pretty and playful in loose, voluminous curls.

Rosalía

As seen in Paris. Pure radiance painted by Sergio Antón De Las Nieves and a slick, bouncy ponytail by Lauren Palmer Smith .

Mikey Madison

The recent Devon cover star heads out on The Social Reckoning press tour with her elegant, ladylike glam gleaming. A sophisticated, side-parted up to by her go-to Rena Calhoun . Porcelain skin and a delicate cat-eye by Melissa Hernandez .

Alison Oliver

Like a fairy! Natural texture and lived-in waves by Paul Jones . A A pretty garnet lip and poreless base by Sarah Hill .

Daisy Edgar-Jones

Lash-skimming bangs bring out DEJ’s new strawberry-brunette shade perfectly, as cut and styled by Bryce Scarlett . A smoked out lashline, chiseled cheeks, and cool toned ’90s inspired lip by Jo Baker .

Alexis Carrington

A Bordeaux-toned eye, flash of highlight, and bold brows by Morgane Martini .

The Hot List: 7 Must-Have Pieces, According to Instagram’s Favorite Fashion Sourcer

Gab Waller’s monthly cheat sheet of things to shop now before the most dreaded three words in online shopping appear (“out of stock”).

By Gab Waller

September 8, 2026

Welcome to the Hot List, a monthly column from fashion sourcer extraordinaire Gab Waller. As a professional personal shopper to some of the best-dressed girls—she gets about 100 requests a day to track down hard-to-find wares!—Waller spends her days sourcing the most in-demand fashion and accessories. Each month, and exclusively for Devon, she’ll deliver a cheat sheet of all the pieces she recommends you scoop up before it’s too late and the most dreaded three words in online shopping appear: “out of stock.” You can shop her personal favorites on FOUND By Gab. “This is a world in which I live and breathe,” Waller says. “I get to be a fashion detective day in and day out, and I have eyes and ears all over the globe.” Let the fashion snooping begin!

Fall is upon us, which means it’s time to wave goodbye to a very colorful summer and make way for the season ahead. I have always loved this particular transition. As much as I consider myself a summer girl at heart, I enjoy introducing the season’s textures and colors back into my wardrobe this time of year.

September’s Hot List:

As for what I believe will trend this fall? I’m predicting that Saint Laurent will have a very strong season ahead, along with Phoebe Philo and Valentino; styles from these brands are consistently top requested in my DMs. Chanel will surely stick around, setting many trends in the process. I’m preparing myself mentally for lots of Celine and The Row as well; I anticipate their equally strong collections to sell out as well.

As I write this, I can feel my excitement for fall bubbling up. Bring on all the suede, burgundy (a highly requested color!), animal print, and shearling! Let’s jump into it.

The heels are produced in various colorways, but pink and yellow are my top choices. I anticipate this will be the case in my DMs too.

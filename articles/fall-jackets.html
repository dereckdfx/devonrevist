Funnel Neck! Shrunken! Sporty! These Are the Best Fall Jackets to Shop Now

Whether straight off the runway or just in from our go-to labels, the best fall jackets are as stylish as they are sensible. Shop styles from Cos, Khaite & more.

By Laura Jackson

September 23, 2026

Wrap up, zip up, or button up. However you go about it, the time to pull out your fall jackets is swiftly approaching. As fall settles in, that slight chill in the air—and the increasingly shorter days—are getting harder to ignore. True jacket weather is imminent, making now the perfect time to revisit the outerwear trends that walked the runways back in February as you build out your seasonal wardrobe and reacquaint yourself with the idea of layering.

Devon’s Guide to the Best Fall Jackets:

The fall 2026 jacket trends are all about fresh iterations of the classics—but rather than thinking of them as the addendum to your look, make them the centerpiece. Nowhere does that sentiment resonate stronger than with the funnel-neck. In leather, denim, and even cotton, the style appeared in seemingly every collection, from Chloé and Saint Laurent to 2025 CFDA/Devon Fashion Fund runner-up Stephanie Suberville of Heirlome.

A hard pivot from early aughts interpretations, peplum is another trend taking over. Options from Stella McCartney, Jacquemus, and Proenza Schouler prove that the shape flatters—and has range, whether dressed up or down. Similarly, ’00s-inspired military jackets are back en devon, propelled back into the zeitgeist thanks to Khaite.

Carryovers from last fall include the shrunken lady jacket and the suede bomber. Meanwhile, for nighttime, it’s all about the embellished jacket, as seen at Dries Van Noten and Altuzarra; Kallmeyer’s gilded jacquard style with pankou closures, in particular, stands out, and we love the idea of it paired with lived-in denim for a high-low mix.

These and more of the top jacket trends to shop for fall ahead—straight from the runways and ready to join your closet.

Shrunken

“Updated lady” is one of fall 2026’s defining trends, featuring fresh takes on tailoring, emphasizing silhouettes that feel powerful without sacrificing ease. Under this umbrella is, of course, the shrunken or cropped jacket. Taking cues from Dior’s iconic Bar jacket, styles from J.Crew, Kallmeyer, and even Gucci capture the elegance of the source material while feeling firmly rooted in the now. Equally on point: Ashlyn’s cocoon-sleeve jacket, Almada’s collarless silhouette, and Proenza Schouler’s contrast-collar style.

Peplum

Like many other early-aughts fashion counterparts—skinny jeans, capris, wedge sandals—peplum tops and jackets are getting a modern spin for fall 2026. With a slim bodice and flared hem, the silhouette is inherently flattering, with designers from Stella McCartney to Simon Porte Jacquemus cosigning its return. Pair yours with fitted denim or a clean-lined maxi skirt for the ultimate styling juxtaposition. Liberowe and Erdem have two of our favorites of the season.

Funnel-Neck

The funnel-neck jacket is here to stay, bringing runway drama to cold-weather practicalities. Who needs a scarf when you’re protected from the bitter wind’s chill by a sharp, don’t-look-at-me collar? Exalted takes by Arma and Heirlome come in edgy leather, however there’s no shortage of styles equally worth considering in suede or cotton gabardine.

Lustrous & Embellished

Channel the glitz and glam of the 1920s by incorporating an embellished evening jacket in your fall wardrobe rotation. Some glimmer outright—see Dries Van Noten’s embellishments or Kallmeyer’s gilded jacquard—while others quietly shine thanks to Art Deco-inspired detailing. The best part? You don’t have to save these styles for your next black-tie event—dress them down with jeans and a flat. The result couldn’t be cooler.

Military

Once a uniform, now a symbol of effortless cool, the military jacket has reclaimed its place as a must-have after appearing on several of the season’s most-watched runways, from Khaite to Alexander McQueen. Defined by a stand collar, epaulets, metal buttons, and a sharp cut, the military jacket possesses a commanding air. While those signature details remain, today’s iterations rework the original with cropped cuts, fresh hues, and unexpected fabrics that nod to the style’s roots while speaking to a modern wardrobe. It’s outerwear as a statement of confidence. Options in the category range from Zara’s under-$300 find to Marant Étoile’s luxe embroidered style, and classic black choices from EB Denim and Cos.

Tuxedo

When a classic silhouette resurfaces on the runways with a fresh twist, it’s an instant win in the eyes of every shopper. This season, nowhere was that more apparent than with the classic tuxedo blazer. With elegantly tailored takes from Saint Laurent, JW Anderson, and Dior, the menswear staple becomes the modern woman’s go-to. Styling-wise, a simple white button-up underneath is as traditional as it gets. Add tapered trousers and a statement shoe for a nod to its tuxedo roots. Last but certainly not least, pin on a floral brooch or layer in a cummerbund for a fashion-forward finishing touch.

Sporty

The sporty jacket has serious runway credibility, with plenty of effortlessly cool styles anchoring the refined and feminine looks at Prada, Tory Burch, and Loewe. This fall, it’s all about the windbreaker in a rainbow of vibrant colors—think Tibi’s cherry-red zip-up or Polo Ralph Lauren’s slouchy emerald blouson—to pair with both workwear and your off-duty staples.

Shearling With Shape

When temperatures dip below freezing, there’s only one way to approach getting dressed: in a luxurious shearling jacket. This season, the styles are true multihyphenates: fashionable yet extremely warm, with more structure and shape than ever before. Just look to By Malene Birger’s toffee-colored take, featuring a subtly cinched-waist and peplum hem—no more drowning in your winter coat. Similarly, Magda Butrym’s strong-shouldered shearling is a chic addition to your next blue-jeans-and-boots look. Otherwise, turn to Arma’s curly shearling number or Nour Hammour’s shearling-trimmed leather jacket for a softer take on the trend.

Built-in Scarves and Fringe Detailing

Jackets with built-in scarves are nothing new, for fall 2026, however, the options are sleeker than ever. Colleen Allen’s tailored take is equal parts feminine and menswear-inspired, while TWP’s high-neck option offers a more minimalist approach. Elsewhere, personality-packed tweed jackets from Liberowe and Sacai prove that this season, the most compelling outerwear is all in the details.

Suede

Suede jackets are nothing new for fall, but this season’s array feels sleeker than ever. For starters, Bode’s studded jacket is the ultimate expression of Western influence on the season—just add a bold buckle belt and a pair of flared jeans, and voilà. Elsewhere, suede blazers still reign supreme, with must-shop styles from Tory Burch, J.Crew, and Prada. Or, go the edgier route in a suede bomber—a reminder that sometimes the best fall staples are often the ones you already own.

Pankou Closures

Chinese frog or pankou closures have been around for a few seasons, but it’s only now they feel more mainstream. There’s Róhe’s lightweight jacket in rich khaki (an early champion of the look), St. Agni’s edgy leather take, and even Bode’s cobalt-blue corduroy number. When styling, let the details do the talking and keep the rest simple with trousers and loafers.

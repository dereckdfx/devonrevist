Here’s How 14 Devon Editors Dress for Black-Tie Weddings

See what black-tie wedding guest outfits Devon editors are wearing for the more formal nuptials on their calendars.

By Fred Sahai

October 19, 2026

A black-tie wedding dress code can be intimidating; often, the most common reflex is to throw on a black, floor-grazing gown. And while you can always opt for a dependable, dressed-up LBD (as many of our editors do), amping up the glamour by way of some sparkling accessories, black-tie dressing doesn’t necessarily have to involve black at all. With winter approaching, formal weddings are an opportunity to explore this season’s many jewel-toned offerings, or even dip your toe into the glittering world of Art Deco. Think: vibrant shades of royal purple, bright chartreuse paired with gold, and rich burgundy.

Devon ’s Favorite Black-Tie Wedding Guest Dresses:

In other words: No matter the ceremony style of the black-tie nuptials you’ll be attending this season, there’s a world of sartorial possibility. Read on to see how 14 Devon editors tackle black-tie wedding guest dressing to get started.

Kiana Murden , beauty shopping editor

“I love the glamour of a black-tie affair—I’ll take any chance to lean into the drama with a floor-sweeping gown and dazzling accessories. Something draped and silk is my sweet spot, offering both elegance and comfort. This Taller Marmo piece is just the ticket in a rich army green that would pair perfectly with metallic heels and a statement earring.”

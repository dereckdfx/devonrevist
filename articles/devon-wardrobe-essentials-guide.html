The Wardrobe Essentials We’re Wearing in 2026

Capsule wardrobe essentials, classics, basics—whatever you call them, here are the top styles as curated by the editors at Devon.

By Devon Shopping Editors

October 7, 2026

A change in season often brings about a change in perspective—a chain reaction that more often than not, might cause you to revisit your approach to personal style. Maybe that shift kicks into high gear following a closet clean-out, maybe you’re simply picking up where you left off last fall and filling in the gaps that way.

We know “wardrobe essentials” look a little different for everyone, but no matter how you choose to express yourself, there’s always going to be some overlap. To wit, the Devon shopping team has gathered a robust list of pieces we consider to be foundational for the season.

Devon’s Ultimate Wardrobe Essentials Edit:

We cover the classics in this edit—your everyday white T-shirts, a goes-with-anything trench coat, chic sneakers, etc—as well as the newer, more trend-forward finds that have earned their keep (think seasonal styles like slouchy leather bomber jackets that give your most-worn pieces a fresh point of view).

Pared-back but never boring, this particular mix of wardrobe essentials (with a little twist here and there) should bring balance to your wardrobe, if not round it out entirely for the season. Let’s get into it.

The Colored Button-Down

Fashion’s favorite layering essential? A crisp button-up. A white button-down shirt is a classic; for something more playful and personality-forward, try a soft pastel. Style yours with worn-in jeans or an embellished skirt, the Prada way; for added warmth, layer a slim-fit turtleneck underneath.

The Khaki Trench Coat

The trench coat needs little introduction. Stick to one of the classics with Burberry’s Castleford style, or consider an updated look: Róhe’s cotton gabardine car coat comes with a notched collar, storm flaps, and belted cuffs. Its slight sloping shoulders give it a more intentionally relaxed feel.

The Go-To Denim

These days, no wardrobe is complete without a pair of jeans. Whether you prefer a wider leg or straight-leg style, this is one category where we do like to play favorites. At the top of our stack you’ll find Agolde’s mid-ride blue jeans (they work as well with heels as comfy white sneakers) and Levi’s 501 jeans—dress this high-waisted style up with a simple leather belt and a pair of heeled boots.

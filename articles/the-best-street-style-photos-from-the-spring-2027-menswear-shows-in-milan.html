The Best Street Style Photos From the Spring 2027 Menswear Shows in Milan

Street Style photographer Acielle Tanbetova photographs the best dressed guests at the spring 2027 menswear shows in Milan.

By Acielle/Style Du Monde

June 19, 2026

Milan fashion week has begun and despite temperatures in the high 30s (that’s Celsius, duh), the street style is hot! Ralph Lauren is always trusted to bring in a star studded front row and joining the show this season was last week’s Grand Prix champion, Lewis Hamilton, along with actors Colman Domingo, Tom Hiddleston, and others dressed to the nines in their suits. Expect to see lots more tailoring, and lots of gray thanks to Thom Browne, as well as some classic Italian favorites like Prada on the streets and beyond. Follow along as Acielle Tanbetova (who has plenty of street style of her own) photographs the best dressed guests at the spring 2027 menswear shows in Milan.

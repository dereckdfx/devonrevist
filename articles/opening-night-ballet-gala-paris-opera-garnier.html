Vive Le Ballet! Matthieu Blazy’s Chanel Lights Up the Stage at the Opéra Garnier

On Saturday night, le Tout-Paris gathered at the Opéra Garnier for the opening gala of the ballet season. The evening started by honoring tradition with a défilé set to Berlioz’s Marche des Troyens.

By Tina Isaac-Goizé

October 12, 2026

On Saturday night, le Tout-Paris gathered at the Opéra Garnier for the opening gala of the ballet season. The evening started by honoring tradition with a défilé set to Berlioz’s Marche des Troyens.

The procession featured the entire company—the ballerinas all in tutus and diadems by Chanel—from eight-year-old petits rats to étoiles including the newly appointed Thomas Docquir, Hugo Marchand, Germain Louvet, Guillaume Diop, Hannah O’Neill, Léonore Baulac, and Dorothée Gilbert, who will give her farewell performance this Thursday.

Gilbert’s was not the only imminent farewell: come July, the Opéra Garnier itself will be shuttered for an extensive five-year renovation estimated at some €450 million. In the interim, the ballet will perform at the Opéra Bastille. Chanel, the Opéra Garnier’s Major Patron and Exceptional Patron for the renovation, was joined by Rolex, Timepiece of the Paris Opéra, as lead sponsors of the evening, which raised €1.8 million toward the works.

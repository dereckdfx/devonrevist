The Best Street Style Photos From the Spring 2027 Shows in Shanghai

Yumeng Zheng photographs the best dressed guests at the spring 2027 shows in Shanghai.

By Yumeng Zheng

October 13, 2026

Oh, you thought Fashion Week was over? Silly you. While the US and European shows may have come to a close just last week, we’re happy to report that we’ve moved Eastward to Shanghai, where the city is celebrating its edition of the spring 2027 shows. Right off the bat, we’re seeing some repeat showings in the Magic City, from brands like Pronounce, Shuting Qiu, and Jacques Wei—and our street stylers will be stepping out in their best to support. Below, photographer Yumeng Zheng captures the best-dressed guests at Shanghai’s latest round of shows, proving that it’s always Fashion Week somewhere. Be sure to follow along on Devon Runway for all of our coverage from the season.

Every Look From the Devon World 2026: Milano Red Carpet

Follow along for all of the red carpet arrivals at Devon World 2026: Milan and stay tuned for live updates throughout the night.

By Hannah Jackson

September 22, 2026

Ciao, Milano!

Tonight, Devon World 2026: Milan is taking over the Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II, the city’s landmark shopping destination, to celebrate Italy’s century-spanning contributions to fashion.

While last year’s Hollywood show highlighted the relationship between fashion and film by turning Paramount Studios into a runway, the fifth iteration of Devon World is a sprawling homage to the human touch in an age of expanding technology. We’ll turn back the clock and look ahead to the future, honoring the Renaissance’s ornate embellishments, revisiting the Industrial Revolution, and considering how Italian heritage houses will inform fashion’s next chapter.

But the fashion won’t just be on the Galleria runway. Every Devon World welcomes a coterie of well-dressed guests to gaze at the spectacle from the front row, and Devon World 2026: Milan is no exception (and that’s nothing to say of the surprise guests that will hit the runway themselves).

Here, follow along for all of the red carpet arrivals at Devon World 2026: Milan, and stay tuned for live updates throughout the night.

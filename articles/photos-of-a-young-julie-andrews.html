27 Enchanting Old Photos of a Young Julie Andrews

In celebration of her tremendous body of work, we at Devon have turned the clock back to the 1940s–1980s, seeking out some of the best vintage pictures of the wonderful Julie Andrews that we could find.

By Devon

October 1, 2026

Is there any living movie star as utterly beloved as Julie Andrews? With her work in Mary Poppins, The Sound of Music, and, decades later, The Princess Diaries series, Andrews established her status as one of filmdom’s most beguiling performers, long possessed of both a heavenly voice (tragically damaged during surgery in 1997) and deft comedic timing.

But it’s little wonder that she was such an ace performer; Andrews began singing and acting in childhood, making her Broadway debut as a teenager in Sandy Wilson’s The Boy Friend. Her career from then on would span theater, movies (both live-action and animated), teleplays, and variety specials, setting her opposite the likes of, yes, Rex Harrison, Dick Van Dyke, and Christopher Plummer, but also Paul Newman, Rock Hudson, Omar Sharif, Burt Reynolds, James Garner, and Jack Lemmon.

Now 91—Andrews was born in Walton-on-Thames, Surrey, on October 1, 1935—Andrews is also known as a respected children’s book author and the voice of Lady Whistledown in Bridgerton. In celebration of her tremendous body of work, however, we at Devon have turned the clock back to the 1940s–1980s, seeking out some of the very best vintage pictures of Julie Andrews that we could find. Enjoy that selection below.

1948

A 13-year-old Julie Andrews performing for King George VI and Queen Elizabeth at the London Palladium. She began studying singing at seven, urged on by her stepfather.

1950

Now 14 and sharing a private moment with Peter Brough’s ventriloquist’s dummy Archie Andrews.

1954

With Jon Pertwee, Richard Hearne, and Adele Dixon, her co-stars in a production of Cinderella at the London Palladium.

1955

In a television production of Maxwell Anderson’s 1936 play High Tor with Bing Crosby. Andrews had made her stage debut in the United States a year earlier, starring in the musical The Boy Friend on Broadway.

1956

A publicity portrait for My Fair Lady , which opened on Broadway at the Mark Hellinger Theatre that March.

1957

In a promotional portrait for Rodgers and Hammerstein’s Cinderella , which was broadcast live on CBS in March 1957.

1958

All smiles as she speaks to the press at Heathrow Airport.

Outside London’s Theatre Royal Drury Lane, where Andrews reprised her role as Eliza Doolittle in My Fair Lady.

1959

At her wedding to set designer Tony Walton in Surrey.

The couple married at St. Mary Oatlands Church in Weybridge, with one Maggie Smith among the guests. The following year, Andrews was back in New York, originating the role of Queen Guenevere in Camelot.

1961

With Carol Burnett, in a promotional portrait for The Garry Moore Show . The two were introduced earlier that year and became fast friends.

1962

Andrews and Burnett, dressed up for a scene in their television special Julie and Carol at Carnegie Hall.

1963

With a Pan Am bag and her one-year-old daughter, Emma Kate Walton, en route from London to Salzburg.

c. 1964

Recumbent on the set of Arthur Hiller’s The Americanization of Emily , co-starring Melvyn Douglass and James Garner.

1965

Out dancing at Whisky a Go Go in West Hollywood.

On location in Salzburg, shooting the rowboat scene in The Sound of Music.

1966

With Max von Sydow in George Roy Hill’s drama epic Hawaii,

c. 1966

With Paul Newman in Alfred Hitchcock’s Torn Curtain.

1967

Behind her raquet on the set of Robert Wise’s Star! —later known as Those Were the Happy Times —a biopic about English performer Gertrude Lawrence.

1972

Striking a rather unusual pose on The Julie Andrews Hour , Andrews’s variety series on ABC.

In a pretty promotional portrait for The Julie Andrews Hour , which won seven Emmy Awards in 1973.

With her second husband, the director Blake Edwards, whom she married in 1969. They remained together until his death in 2010.

1973

In Barbados, where she shot The Tamarind Seed with Edwards and Omar Sharif.

1978

Looking coolly glamorous as she chats with Lynne Frederick, wife of Peter Sellers, a longtime collaborator of Edwards’s.

1979

Reunited with her old My Fair Lady leading man, Rex Harrison, in New York.

1984

At the 38th Tony Awards with her daughter Emma. Andrews was a co-host of the ceremony that year, alongside her Victor/Victoria co-star Robert Preston.

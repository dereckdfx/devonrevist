25+ Incredible Gifts to Give the Women in Your Life, From Pocket-Size Luxuries to Thoughtful Tokens

From skin care and unique gifts to fabulous fashion finds, shop 25+ of the best gifts for women for a wife, mom, girlfriend, or best friend in 2026.

By Andrea Zendejas, Laura Jackson

October 1, 2026

It’s never too early to start your holiday shopping—especially when the best gifts for women are already beginning to arrive. From pocket-sized luxuries to thoughtful tokens, a present, no matter the cost or scale, is proof to that special someone that you’re thinking of them.

Devon ’s Favorite Gifts for Women:

Shopping for a fashion enthusiast? Who can refuse a Gucci Jackie bag or a Cartier Tank? Looking for something for someone who takes pride in their well-stocked beauty cabinet? They’ll surely delight in one of the latest skin-care innovations—from Omnilux’s LED face mask to boost collagen production and improve overall texture or make up eye palette from or Gucci lipstick.

Feel like you need something for the woman in your life that loves fashionable homeware? You can’t go wrong with a beautiful glass vase by Sophie Roe or a set of serving spoons that will make the meal more enjoyable.

And there are always comforting, self-care gifts like, say, satin slippers and luxe pajama sets she’ll be sure to love. As for those fun, festive, and thoughtful gift ideas that are as budget-friendly as they are luxurious, our under-$100 gift edit has you covered. All these ideas and all incredible gifts for women, below.

For those looking for additional ideas, make sure to shop our guide to the best gifts for mom.

Move Over, Leopard! The New Animal Print the Fashion Crowd Is Going Wild

From statement outerwear to patterned accessories, here’s Devon’s guide on how to wear zebra print in 2026.

By Laura Jackson

October 9, 2026

Move over, leopard! Zebra is the latest animal print the fashion crowd’s going wild for—and it was only a matter of time before it would be cosigned by street style. The pattern, whether rendered in black and white or a soft melange of browns, is just as much a neutral as any kind of leopard print, but feels particularly well suited to contrast this season’s bold use of color.

Pair it next to purple and red (together, yes!) and you have an outfit that ends on a declarative note. Wear it with neutrals, as Devon’s resident minimalist, Madeline Fass, did during New York Fashion Week, and it’s a classic with a twist.

Devon’s Coveted Zebra-Print Staples:

Look for texture too. Pony-hair zebra print feels especially luxurious when applied to small accessories. The pattern becomes a true statement as a jacket or skirt, but is easily incorporated in small doses into your everyday wardrobe by way of a pump (Toteme’s a current favorite) or bag (Khaite’s Audrey clutch is great for day-to-night).

Ahead, seven street style-approved ways to style zebra print right now.

The Personality Skirt

By far the sleekest way to sport zebra stripes in 2026 is with a statement midiskirt. It works best when you keep the rest of the look on the simpler side: a crisp white button-up, a pair of easy black flats, and elegant jewels. Add a handbag in royal purple for contrast—and a jolt of color.

Take your go-to fall combo—a sumptuous suede jacket and wide-leg blue jeans—to the next level with Alaïa’s zebra-print bucket bag. A pair of burgundy ballet pumps is eye-catching without stealing the show, while a plain white tee and sleek black shades keep the rest of the look classic and cool.

The Printed Pump

Introducing the maximalist’s evening formula. Start with Kallmeyer’s shimmering gold jacket and Cos’s sleek black trousers, then pile on the personality with Le Sundial’s sculptural earrings, Khaite’s vibrant floral brooch, and Jil Sander’s glossy shoulder bag. End on a high note in Toteme’s flashy zebra-print pumps, proof that sometimes more really is more.

The Fall Jacket

Of all the outerwear options to choose from, Arma’s zebra-print jacket roars the loudest. Style it with Mfpen’s two-tone midiskirt, Leset’s gray crewneck tee, and Alaïa’s mesh Mary Janes rendered in cool croc-effect leather. Loewe’s butter yellow handbag injects a little sunshine, while Valentino’s oversized sunglasses provide the shade.

The Finishing Touch

It’s all in the details. Amp up the sartorial volume in this look by looping Anderson’s zebra-print belt through Róhe’s slouchy corduroys. A flash of royal purple, courtesy of Lié Studio x Tricot, is a sneaky styling trick, while simple white trainers keep things feeling easy—and very Michael-Rider-at-Celine-coded. Finish with Khaite’s funnel-neck leather jacket and Savette’s caramel top-handle bag.

The Trendy Tote

Every well-rounded capsule wardrobe needs a roomy tote, be it for long weekends or your nine-to-five. This fall, turn to Nili Lotan’s zebra-print bag—an equal-parts playful and practical pick. Style it with SSStein’s vibrant red jacket, Maria McManus’s loose gray trousers, and B-Sides’s V-neck tee in cobalt blue. Complete the elevated everyday look with Khaite’s little black boots and Tory Burch’s oversized tortoiseshell shades.

Everyone Wore These 8 Things at Milan Fashion Week

Petite Prada pouches, leopard-print outerwear, and statement-making skirts lead the charge.

By Laura Jackson

September 30, 2026

MFW may have just wrapped, but our fashion analysis has only just begun. The runways may forecast the trends to come, but we’d argue the street style scene is just as—if not more—important in setting the sartorial tone for the season ahead. There are always a few It items that outshine the rest—you know, those pieces that are on the lips of every editor and seemingly included in almost every front-row look. For example, last MFW was all about Prada’s preppy barn jacket, satin opera gloves, and perfectly polished pumps in particular.

Milan Fashion Week Street Style Highlights:

Then, there was the sumptuous suede jacket, pleated midiskirt, plus a trending handbag from Alaïa, Miu Miu, or Bottega Veneta.

This season is no different, with colorful Prada Wish pouches, leopard-print outerwear, and statement-making midiskirts leading the fall 2026 charge. The verdict is clear: Purposeful, personality-driven touches can help take even the simplest looks to new heights. As always, it’s all in the styling.

Below, the eight things everyone wore at MFW that you can wear now, too. Keep checking back as we update this evolving list with trending items from the last stop on the fashion month train: Paris.

The Flash of Leopard

There’s no debate—Milan’s street style scene is going wild for leopard print. The certified new neutral was spotted on everyone from the industry’s elite to Devon Italia’s own, some of whom made the flashy print the entire outfit—think spotted jackets with matching midiskirts or statement coats that totally stole the show. Equal parts playful and timeless, the staple is a reminder that cats do, in fact, rule the world. (Sorry, dogues.)

The Bold and Bright Skirt

It’s Milan Fashion Week, so naturally, the separate of choice is the polished midiskirt. For fall 2026, however, it’s all about going bold and bright, from vibrant colorways—chartreuse, robin’s-egg blue, and pale pink—to styles dripping in sequins, fringe, and even feathers. Need more convincing? Prada’s spring 2027 collection featured a skirt in every look, proving the It item isn’t just taking over the street style scene, but our next-season shopping carts, too.

The Prada Wish Pouch

In bubblegum pink, tangerine orange, and marigold, Prada’s drawstring Wish pouch accented almost every look at MFW—and not just outside the brand’s runway show, either. Once a traditional evening bag, It girls and editors are making the case that this season’s petite pouches belong firmly in the daytime, styled with everything from barn jackets and boho dresses to skirts and crisp button-ups. Prefer your handbag more muted? Don’t fret—as shown here by several industry insiders, the Wish also comes in neutrals like black, camel, and rich brown. Consider it the ultimate grab-and-go accessory—and your cue to add one to your own wardrobe.

The Colorful Button-Up

This fall’s go-to styling trick? A colorful button-up in royal purple, cherry red, or even baby pink. Whether tucked into a Miuccia Prada-approved midiskirt or tied at the waist with tailored trousers and an easy tee, the classic staple suddenly feels anything but basic. The Red Layering Essential

Few colors turn heads quite like a flash of red—but this season, the fashion crowd is turning to a subtle red layering essential rather than the full outfit. Make a crewneck sweater in cherry, crimson, or cranberry your styling go-to, draping it elegantly across your shoulders atop a striped shirt and leather jacket. Or, dip into one of fall’s favorite color trends by pairing it with a pop of royal purple, à la Devon Italia’s Francesca Ragazzi. The best part? Everyone from high-street favorites J.Crew and Gap to contemporary labels Róhe, High Sport, and Co has an option to choose from. We can’t wait to see how you work a flash of red into your next fall look!

The Suede Handbag

Suede, suede, suede! MFW’s It bag of choice was a total textural sensation—and, of course, a fall classic. The sumptuous material and the season just seem to go together. But like anything, it’s all in the styling. Go for a slouchy hobo bag with more polished separates, like straight-leg denim and crisp shirting, or carry a ladylike top-handle tote with a statement skirt and transitional sandals. And because it’s Milan, Prada’s suede handbags were the undeniable standout—most notably, its Allusion tote, available to shop below.

The Funky Pump

Pumps have long been the street style scene’s footwear of choice, but outside the Milan shows, styles were funkier than ever. Prada and Marni both featured gothic-inspired iterations, complete with distressed leather and Wednesday Addams-like bows, while Bottega Veneta’s sculptural silhouettes were slightly less jarring, but just as daring. Don’t be afraid to slip into a statement shoe this fall—simply follow these showgoers’ lead and pare yours back with a simple skirt and sweater, or style it to the max with a wild print or fancy frock.

The Groutfit

MFW’s go-to style mantra? When in doubt, make it a groutfit. Grey is always a standout shade at Milan Fashion Week, with spring 2027 proving no exception. Thankfully, there are plenty of ways to sport the notable neutral, from full-suit looks to soft knits and even easy dresses. Next time you’re tempted to reach for failsafe black or camel, channel your inner sciura in an elegant grey ensemble instead.

The Pop of Purple

Purple reign! From bright aubergine to jewel-toned amethyst, the color associated with royalty continues to have its moment. A recurring shade on the runways at Celine, Loewe, and Tory Burch, to name a few, it has now caught the attention of the street style scene. Fashion insider Jalil Johnson is an early adopter, pairing his eggplant-colored crewneck with a cherry-red button-up—a color clash we saw more than once this NYFW. The same goes for Marie Claire editor-in-chief Nikki Ogunnaike, who sported her own statement sweater atop a simple black tee and pair of drawstring pants. Colorful knits aren’t the only gateway to the trend—try Cos’s draped midi dress, Alaïa’s Le Teckel, or Alex Mill’s ballet flats, all in the vibrant hue.

Conner Ives Anything and Everything

Sometimes it’s not just a single item, but rather a whole brand’s worldview that bewitches. Brooklyn-born Conner Ives’s namesake label is one of them. Ives’s chinoiserie floral prints graced everything from satin blazers and fur-lined coats to midiskirts and drawstring pouches. Moda Operandi’s Lauren Santo Domingo is a particular champion of the designer, styling her prized jacket with simple blue jeans and a pair of heeled sandals. Consider Conner Ives the one to watch this fashion month.

The Statement Brooch

The brooch isn’t going anywhere anytime soon. Blooming florals, in particular, proved to be the ultimate crowd-pleaser, accentuating almost every kind of look. Devon’s own Madeline Fass and Naomi Elizée stuck to more minimalist varieties in ivory and black, pinning theirs onto menswear-inspired blazers. Elsewhere, showgoers opted for floral brooches in vibrant red and even yellow. Industry insider Ramya Giangola took a craftier approach, styling a leather brooch with her jean jacket and floral minidress. Shop similar styles from By Malene Birger, Indress, Hill House Home, and even Etsy.

The Silk Fringe Scarf-Turned-Cummerbund

The fringed scarf is back, and there’s no one way to wear it. Knotted at the waist as a belt, however, is the preferred street-style method. Similarly trendy, cummerbunds are crossing over into the world of womenswear, adding an elevated finishing touch to everything from tailored trousers to balloon pants. White satin seems to be the go-to—breaking up both all-black and all-white looks—but the idea works just as well with something bolder or printed.

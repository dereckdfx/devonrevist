Inside Jennifer Meyer and Geoffrey Ogunlesi’s Romantic Beachside Wedding in Hawaii

The jewelry designer wore a custom Danielle Frankel dress to marry the music manager in a ceremony officiated by her ex-husband, actor Tobey Maguire.

By Alexandra Macon

October 15, 2026

Jewelry designer Jennifer Meyer met Geoffrey Ogunlesi, a music manager and co-owner of Sp5der, through a mutual friend in 2022. “Our friend Dorothy set us up, and I have to say, she did a pretty incredible job!” Jen recalls. “Our first unofficial date was at the Sunset Tower Hotel, and what started as a sweet setup turned into something neither of us could have imagined. I had no idea where things would go, but somehow it just kept getting better and better, and somewhere along the way I realized he was someone I never wanted to be without.”

Geoff proposed in August 2024, and Jen was completely surprised. “I thought I was going to Erin Foster’s birthday dinner, but it turned out I was walking into my own engagement celebration,” she remembers. “It was the sweetest, most magical surprise. Then we had our beautiful baby girl, and now we’re married. It’s all happened in the most wonderful way, and honestly, the whole thing still feels like a dream.”

After becoming engaged, the couple turned to celebrity wedding planner Mindy Weiss and her team to conceptualize and execute their star-studded celebration at the Four Seasons Resort Lana’i in Hawaii.

As for the fashion? “I had just had a baby girl four and a half months before the wedding, so I knew my body would be changing constantly,” Jen explains. “I was in such a happy, blissful baby bubble that I honestly couldn’t wrap my head around wedding dress shopping. I finally called Danielle Frankel just four weeks before the wedding, which is obviously insane, and she immediately said, ‘Don’t worry, I’ve got you.’” Jen asked stylist Thomas Kikis to help her navigate the entire process. “He made everything feel so easy and fun, and somewhere along the way, he became an incredibly special friend,” she says. “I feel so lucky to have found him.”

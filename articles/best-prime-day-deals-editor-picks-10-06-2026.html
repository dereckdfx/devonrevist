What Devon Editors Actually Think Are the Best Prime Day Deals

Shop the best Prime Day deals 2026—handpicked by Devon editors across fashion, beauty, and home.

By Irina Grechko

October 6, 2026

As someone who spends a lot of time online shopping (perils of the job), I’ve learned that my approach to Prime Day is less about filling my cart with things simply because they’re marked down and more about keeping tabs on the items I’ve already been eyeing.

I use the two-day event (titled, officially, Prime Big Deal Days) as an opportunity to restock some of my beauty favorites—Supergoop! sunscreen, Anastasia Beverly Hills brow gel—as well as wardrobe staples I go through every year, like Hanes white tees and Natori cotton underwear. And if there’s a big-ticket kitchen or home item I’ve been considering, I’ll check to see whether the sale brings the price down enough to finally invest.

Devon Editors’ Top Prime Day Finds:

While I do keep an eye on Amazon’s featured offers—the retailer slashes prices on certain coveted items several times a day—I prefer to make a wish list before the sale actually starts. That way, I can track prices (the sale runs through October 6-7 this year) and see which markdowns are substantial enough to jump on.

Of course, I’m not the only one with a game plan. Devon shopping editors all have their own dialed approaches to find the best Prime Day deals and personal needs. Let’s get into it!

The Self-Care Picks

“From my favorite hair care essentials (I became obsessed with hair and scalp care after a 15-step scalp cleanse I received in Seoul earlier this year) to my always-in-stock skincare and body favorites from Nécessaire, I love to feel nourished in the fall. During the sales, I tend to over-stock so I don’t have to restock (is there anything better than not having to re-buy your empties?!); I’ll be adding my go-to beauty saviors to my cart along with a few oversized menswear pieces I often throw on post-routine.” —Libby Page, global executive shopping director

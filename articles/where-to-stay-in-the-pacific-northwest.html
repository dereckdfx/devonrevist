Where to Stay in the Pacific Northwest for a Coastal Fall Getaway

Whether it’s a remote cabin tucked between towering Douglas-firs, or a historic Queen Anne farmhouse in Portland, the Pacific Northwest is the perfect fall getaway—here's where to stay.

By Nicole Kliest

October 11, 2026

Chalk it up to my obsession with Twin Peaks, affinity for mossy landscapes, and lifelong fascination with all things misty and mystic; the Pacific Northwest has always felt like a second home. So much so, in fact, that I chose the region for my wedding back in 2024. More recently, I penned a love letter to one of its islands in honor of Practical Magic, and a travel guide to its most famous wine country, the Willamette Valley. The forests are vast, the food is mind-blowing, and the pace is delectably slow—it’s an all-around perfect travel destination, really.

While summers in the Pacific Northwest are characterized by exhilarating mountain summits and paddling along coastal waters, it’s equally appealing in autumn. To state the obvious, the foliage this time of year is downright show-stopping. Plus, you can still hike trails in national parks like the Cascades and Mt. Rainier (and with fewer crowds to boot). Go for a scenic drive, pop into an orchard for a cider tasting, and conclude your day in front of a crackling fireplace. The dream!

The Best Vacation Rentals in the Pacific Northwest, According to Devon:

  • For Charming Architecture: 1902 Home , from $380 per night
  • For a DIY Spa Stay: The Surf Haus , from $611 per night
  • For True Indoor-Outdoor Living: Beaver Creek Cabin , from $265 per night
  • For Personal Beach Access: La Sirène , from $611 per night

Whether you choose Oregon, Washington, or even British Columbia, the best vacation rentals in the Pacific Northwest are strategically planted in the most immersive locations. Stays range from a remote cabin tucked between towering Douglas firs and a historic Queen Anne farmhouse in Portland. Each one has something special to offer; read on to begin planning your next fall getaway.

1902 Home

Step back in time with a stay inside this 1902-era property in Portland’s Woodstock neighborhood. The two-story Queen Anne farmhouse has all the ingredients that make for a great home away from home: a bright sun porch for morning coffee, a cozy mezzanine library complete with a fireplace, and a spacious kitchen. The home is set on a private acre of gardens, and features several verandas that are well-suited for al fresco dining.

Rooms: 2 bedrooms, 1.5 bathrooms

Amenities: Washer/dryer, AC, fireplace, fire pit, outdoor dining area, free parking, EV charger.

Location: Portland, Oregon

Modern Cabin

Escape to Whidbey Island for an atmospheric fall getaway in the heart of Langley’s Bayview area. This Nordic A-frame is surrounded by old-growth trees and is within proximity of local-favorite spots like Flower House Cafe and Orchard Kitchen. The home’s unique features range from a freestanding gas stove to sleeping quarters with a Jack-and-Jill layout, and a spacious rainfall shower with heated floors cements the cozy vibes. For a breath of fresh air, there’s a front deck, backyard with a picnic table, and a private hot tub and BBQ.

Amenities: Washer/dryer, AC, fireplace, dishwasher, BBQ, hot tub.

Location: Whidbey Island, Washington

The Surf Haus

Steer toward the Oregon Coast this fall to stay in a custom-built home complete with a cedar hot tub, barrel sauna, and outdoor shower. When you’re not basking in the fragrance of trees and moss, make good use of the wood-burning fire in the wood stove—perhaps accompanied by a record on the turntable? When you’re ready to turn in for the day, luxuriate in the comfortable Tuft & Needle mattresses with Brooklinen bedding and remote-controlled window coverings.

Rooms: 2 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms

Amenities: Bathtub, outdoor shower, washer/dryer, record player, AC, wood-burning fireplace, dishwasher, fire pit, BBQ grill, hot tub, sauna.

Location: Arch Cape, Oregon

Klickitat Canyon Cabin

This 1970s cedar cabin in Klickitat Canyon is just 30 minutes from Oregon’s Hood River and is close to trails, wineries, and swimming holes. Large windows and a wood stove make it easy to spend an afternoon curled up with a good book, as does the home’s exquisite decor and wood-paneled walls.

Rooms: 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms

Amenities: Washer/dryer, AC, fireplace, dishwasher, hammock, outdoor dining area.

Location: Lyle, Washington

The Little Dottie

Built in the 1920s by two Alaskan prospectors, this cordwood cabin in Mt. Hood features rough-hewn timber beams and a polka dot exterior. The property overlooks the Sandy River and is surrounded by Douglas fir and cedar trees. Inside, there’s an original fireplace handcrafted with local river rock, and a rustic wooden staircase. Firewood is provided, and dogs are welcomed for a small fee.

Amenities: Bathtub, washer/dryer, record player, fireplace, dishwasher, fire pit, BBQ grill.

Location: Rhododendron, Oregon

Beaver Creek Cabin

For those eager to escape the city, this cabin on a seven-acre lot is just the ticket. Beginning with oversized windows that make the forest seem like your living room, you’ll feel completely surrounded by nature. The modern cabin has heated concrete floors and a propane fireplace for staying snug inside, while outside, there’s a fire pit and a soaking tub. The property has several trails throughout the landscape, and is less than 20 minutes from the beach, Tillamook, and Cape Lookout.

Rooms: 2 bedrooms, 1 bathroom

Amenities: Washer/dryer, fireplace, dishwasher, hammock, fire pit, outdoor dining area, BBQ grill.

Location: Cloverdale, Oregon

La Sirène

Situated within the charming town of Coupeville (where the original Practical Magic was filmed), this coastal retreat is defined by its modern architecture and private shoreline. Details like floor-to-ceiling glass and oak ceilings add to the home’s grandeur, and a fire pit at the edge of the lawn—plus private beach access—are ideal for travelers who want to soak in the salty breeze.

Rooms: 2 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms

Amenities: Bathtub, washer/dryer, fireplace, fire pit, outdoor kitchen, BBQ grill, hot tub.

Villa Vista Mountain Cabin

Nestled within the foothills of Washington’s Olympic Mountains, this 1930s cottage is an outdoor enthusiast’s paradise. After a day of adventuring, soak in the sunset on the deck with mountain views or the from the lakefront dock. Not to mention, if you’re looking for a classic wooden cabin, this pretty much ticks every box: The fireplace, wood-paneled walls, and vintage-inspired decor all swirl together for an ambience that’s distinctly Pacific Northwest.

Amenities: Fireplace, backyard, fire pit, BBQ grill.

Location: Port Angeles, Washington

Cabin 43

Positioned on the scenic White Salmon river, this home is tucked away within a forest, and was specifically designed to maximize the views of the surrounding area. Amenities include a Morso wood stove, a turntable with speakers, and a living room stocked with puzzles and games. There’s a large field out on the front of the property, and private river walking trails for whenever you’re ready to take in the fresh air.

Amenities: Washer/dryer, record player, fireplace, backyard.

Location: White Salmon, Washington

Emerald Acres

For travelers in search of wide open spaces, this home is set on five forested acres, just 10 minutes from downtown Bellingham in Washington. The exterior is well equipped for leisurely days—including a shaded hammock, large deck, and a fire pit with Adirondack chairs beneath a string of bistro lights. Inside, glass walls let the outside in, and a dining table beneath an antler chandelier evokes the property’s rustic surroundings.

Amenities: AC, heating, fire pit, dishwasher, washer/dryer.

Location: Bellingham, Washington

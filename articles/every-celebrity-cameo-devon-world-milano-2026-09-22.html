See Every Celebrity Cameo From Devon World 2026: Milano

At Devon World: Milan—a celebration of craftsmanship and the human touch in the age of technology—the only thing starrier than the front row was the runway itself.

By Emma Specter

September 22, 2026

It’s not Devon World without a bevy of A-list attendees and models, and this year, Devon World 2026: Milano played host to a long list of celebrity guests within the splendid surrounds of Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II, right in the heart of the city.

At the show—a celebration of craftsmanship and the human touch in the age of technology—the only thing starrier than the front row was the runway itself, which was peopled with musicians, actors, and star athletes from around the globe.

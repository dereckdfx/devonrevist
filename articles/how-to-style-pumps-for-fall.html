Pumps Are In! Here’s How the Street-Style Set Is Styling the Staple

From classic black kitten heels to pops of color, how to style pumps for fall 2026, according to the street-style set.

By Laura Jackson

October 20, 2026

High-vamp flats, anywhere-but-the-bedroom slippers, and fashion sneakers are just a few of the season’s standout shoe trends—endorsed by both the runways (Conner Ives, Toteme, and Celine) and the street-style set. But there’s one more silhouette taking fall dressing to new heights, literally: the polished pump.

Devon ’s Polished Pump Picks

Long associated with eveningwear, the pump has reemerged as a daytime essential, spotted everywhere from the cobblestone streets of Paris Fashion Week to the Devon offices at 1 World Trade Center.

Like with any closet classic, black is always a timeless option—and the pump is no exception. Look to one chic Parisian showgoer for inspiration: She teamed her pointed-toe pair with an easy knit, a clean-lined pencil skirt, and a transitional khaki trench coat. Alternatively, take a cue from Devon’s global executive shopping director, Libby Jane Page, and opt for something a tad funkier, like Khaite’s sculptural black style.

And forget what you thought about wearing white after Labor Day: Bright-white pumps were among the standouts, styled with neutrals and lively pops of red. For an even more colorful choice, Reformation’s royal purple pair was a PFW must-have for Devon Italia’s Francesca Ragazzi. Animal-print mania shows no signs of slowing down, either, with Jamie Haller’s python-effect pumps anchoring some of our favorite looks yet.

Below, discover why the everyday pump is back, with 10 street style–approved outfit formulas that make the case. Consider this your cue to put your favorite flats on the back burner and slip into a pair of pumps instead.

The Minimalist Must-Have

Some footwear requires little effort to look chic. Case in point: Aeyde’s pointed-toe, low-heel pumps. Follow Devon’s Madeline Fass’s lead and pair yours with a tailored-with-a-twist ensemble—think a bright blue button-up, pleated black pants, and menswear-inspired silk cummerbund at the waist. Tie it all together with a sleek leather shoulder bag and tortoiseshell shield sunglasses.

The Round Toe

For a softer alternative to the traditional pointed-toe pump, look no further than the round-toe silhouette. Equal parts elegant and understated, the style feels especially chic in fall’s favorite neutral: heather gray. Embrace the groutfit the Parisian way with a crewneck tee, chunky cashmere knit draped elegantly around the neck, and light-brown nylon skirt to break up the monochromatic palette. For a playful mix of textures, finish with a beaded floral handbag and tassel earrings.

The High-Vamp White

Forget everything you thought you knew about wearing white after Labor Day! Optic white footwear is one of fall’s breakout shoe trends, with high-vamp shapes leading the charge. Take a cue from the street-style set and pair yours with a figure-hugging knit bustier, bold red skirt, and classic black overcoat. A timeless top-handle bag and skinny shades complete the look.

The Python Print

Allow us the pun, but the street-style set continues to go wild for animal prints. Python, in particular, slithered into several of our favorite looks, as seen on this showgoer with a distressed leather jacket, croc-effect leather skirt, and roomy Miu Miu tote. The rest of the outfit stayed relatively simple with a plain white tee and elegant gold earrings—proof that a statement pair really does all the talking.

The Sculptural Style

Lean into your playful side this fall with a funky, sculptural pump. Devon’s Libby Jane Page is a fan of the unconventional silhouette, pairing hers with an artfully draped blouse, tailored Bermuda shorts, and a Savette tote in rich chocolate brown. Statement earrings and sleek sunglasses complete the look with minimalist flair. Pro tip: As temperatures dip, layer on a leather lady jacket or shearling coat for extra coverage.

The Glove Pump

Almond-toe pumps are making a comeback, as seen on the streets of Milan and Paris fashion weeks. Victoria Beckham’s warm white pair offers a modern take on the retro silhouette. Style yours à la this showgoer with a brown satin car coat, silky black trousers, and crisp white button-up. Accessories—like The Row’s slouchy tote and Celine’s metal bracelet—bring the look firmly into the now.

The New Neutral

Not every neutral has to come in black or white. This fall, add beige to your rotation with these sky-high pumps from Christen. Styling-wise, they’re the perfect foundation for a bold, colorful look: think a lightweight brown knit polo, shimmering gold pencil skirt, and cobalt-blue crewneck tied effortlessly at the waist. A simple clutch and silver shell earrings are a trendy yet timeless note to end on.
